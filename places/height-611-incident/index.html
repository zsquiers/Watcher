Height 611 incident

Dalnegorsk, Russia, 1986

Dalnegorsk, Russia, 1986 · UAP or anomaly site · 44.560°N, 135.580°E

On January 29, 1986, witnesses saw a glowing sphere crash into a hill known as Height 611. Researchers collected unusual fragments from the site, and the case is still debated. 1
