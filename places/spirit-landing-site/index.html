Gusev crater, 2004

Gusev crater, 2004 · On Mars · Spacecraft landing site · 14.572°S, 175.478°E

NASA's Spirit rover landed in Gusev crater on January 4, 2004. The landing site was named the Columbia Memorial Station. 1
