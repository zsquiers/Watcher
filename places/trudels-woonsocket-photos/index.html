Trudel's Woonsocket photos

East Woonsocket, Rhode Island, 1967

East Woonsocket, Rhode Island, 1967 · UAP or anomaly site · 42.000°N, 71.480°W

On June 10, 1967, Harold Trudel stopped on West Wrentham Road and photographed a domed, metallic-looking object hovering over power lines, taking seven pictures in about five minutes. He had reported earlier sightings in the area, and the photos have long been disputed. 1,2
