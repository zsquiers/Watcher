Big Sur missile film

Anderson Peak, Big Sur, California, 1964

Anderson Peak, Big Sur, California, 1964 · UAP or anomaly site · 36.181°N, 121.625°W

From August to November 1964 an Air Force team with a special telescope filmed missile launches from Vandenberg Air Force Base from this mountaintop. Lt. Robert Jacobs, the on-site commander, has claimed since 1982 that the film showed a UFO that followed an Atlas missile and disabled its warhead with a "beam of energy." The project engineer said the film showed decoy warheads and chaff (see the claims ledger). 1
