Unarius Academy of Science

El Cajon, California

El Cajon, California · Civilian research group · 32.795°N, 116.963°W

Founded in Los Angeles in February 1954 by Ernest and Ruth Norman, who said they channeled teachings from advanced beings on higher planes. Under Ruth Norman, who led it from 1972 to 1993, it raised its public profile, and it became known for the belief that "Space Brothers" would arrive in 33 spaceships. A space-fleet landing that a later leader had predicted for 2001 did not happen. 1
