Borderland Sciences Research Associates

San Diego, California, founded 1945

San Diego, California, founded 1945 · Civilian research group · 32.720°N, 117.160°W

A group founded by Meade Layne to study psychic and "borderland" phenomena. From 1947 it promoted the idea that flying saucers were etheric craft from another plane of existence. Location is approximate. 1
