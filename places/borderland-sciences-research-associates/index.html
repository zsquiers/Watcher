Borderland Sciences Research Associates

3524 Adams Avenue, San Diego, founded 1945

3524 Adams Avenue, San Diego, founded 1945 · Civilian research group · 32.763°N, 117.121°W

A group founded in 1945 by Meade Layne, with Max Freedom Long and others, to study psychic and "borderland" phenomena; its 1950s papers give its headquarters as 3524 Adams Ave., San Diego. From 1947 it promoted the idea that flying saucers were etheric craft from another plane of existence, drawing on sessions with the trance medium Mark Probert. Around 1950 it began incorporating in California as the Borderland Sciences Research Foundation. Riley Hansard Crabb became director in 1959 and ran it from Vista, California, editing its Journal of Borderland Research, the successor to Layne's Round Robin, for 25 years; the foundation is now based in Eureka, California. Its contributors included Vincent Gaddis, who coined the term "Bermuda Triangle," and the sky-creatures theorist Trevor James Constable. 1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8
