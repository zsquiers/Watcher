Tully "saucer nest"

Tully, Queensland, Australia, 1966

Tully, Queensland, Australia, 1966 · UAP or anomaly site · 17.933°S, 145.923°E

In 1966 a farmer said he saw a saucer-shaped craft rise from a swamp and fly away. He found a roughly circular area where the reeds were flattened in clockwise curves, the most famous of the 1960s "saucer nest" reports. British pranksters Doug Bower and Dave Chorley later said this case inspired them to start making crop circles in 1978. 1
