Southaven Park crash story

Shirley, Long Island, 1992

Shirley, Long Island, 1992 · UAP or anomaly site · 40.810°N, 72.895°W

The Long Island UFO Network said a craft crashed in Southaven Park on November 24, 1992, starting a forest fire that Brookhaven National Laboratory's fire department put out, and that the park was closed for days afterward. A local MUFON director who visited found nothing except some trees bent at odd angles. 1
