Haynesville light

Near Haynesville, Louisiana, 1966

Near Haynesville, Louisiana, 1966 · UAP or anomaly site · 33.013°N, 93.170°W

At about 8:30 p.m. on December 30, 1966, a nuclear physicist driving north on U.S. Highway 79 with his wife and children saw a pulsing red-orange light below the treetops in the forest to the west. It suddenly flared to a brilliant white that washed out the car's headlights and lit the woods on both sides of the road, then after about four seconds returned to its red-orange glow. He stopped farther up the road and watched it from outside the car before driving on. The Air Force's Condon study investigated with him in 1967 and listed the case as unexplained. He later found a clearing about 30 feet across where the bark of the surrounding pines was blackened on the side facing the center. The pin marks the area approximately. 1,2
