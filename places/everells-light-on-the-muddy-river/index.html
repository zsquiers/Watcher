Everell's light on the Muddy River

Boston, Massachusetts, 1639

Boston, Massachusetts, 1639 · UAP or anomaly site · 42.335°N, 71.105°W

Governor John Winthrop recorded in his journal that James Everell, "a sober, discreet man," and two companions rowing on the Muddy River saw a great light that flamed up when it stood still and darted toward Charlestown "as swift as an arrow" for two or three hours. Afterward they found themselves about a mile upstream with no memory of rowing there. It is often called the first recorded UFO sighting in America. 1,2
