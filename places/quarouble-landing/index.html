Quarouble, France, 1954

Quarouble, France, 1954 · UAP or anomaly site · 50.390°N, 3.624°E

At about 10:30 p.m. on September 10, 1954, Marius Dewilde, who lived in a level-crossing keeper's house beside the railway at Quarouble near the Belgian border, said his dog's barking drew him outside, where he saw a craft on the tracks and two small beings in helmets. He said a beam of light from the craft paralyzed him before it rose and flew away. Police were called that night, and investigators reported marks on the railway sleepers. It came during France's great wave of sightings in the autumn of 1954. 1,2
