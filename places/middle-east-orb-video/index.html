Middle East orb video

Near Deir ez-Zor, Syria, 2022

Near Deir ez-Zor, Syria, 2022 · UAP or anomaly site · 35.450°N, 40.250°E

On July 12, 2022, an MQ-9 Reaper drone filmed a small spherical object that the Pentagon released as the Middle East Object. AARO's director Sean Kirkpatrick showed it to a Senate subcommittee in April 2023 and later called it a typical example of what AARO sees most. Bellingcat located the footage northeast of Deir ez-Zor, estimated the object at under half a meter across, and concluded it may just be a balloon. 1
