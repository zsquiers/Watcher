Val Johnson incident

Marshall County, Minnesota, 1979

Marshall County, Minnesota, 1979 · UAP or anomaly site · 48.457°N, 97.107°W

Deputy Sheriff Val Johnson said that at about 2 a.m. on August 27, 1979, a beam of light sped toward his patrol car near Stephen, Minnesota, and he heard glass breaking. He said he was unconscious for 39 minutes and that his watch and the car's clock had stopped for 14 minutes. The windshield was cracked, lights were damaged and an aerial was bent. Ufologists consider it significant; skeptic Philip Klass argued it was a hoax. 1
