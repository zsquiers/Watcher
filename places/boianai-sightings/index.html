Boianai, Papua New Guinea, 1959

Boianai, Papua New Guinea, 1959 · UAP or anomaly site · 9.980°S, 149.800°E

On June 26 and 27, 1959, Anglican missionary William Gill and dozens of people at his mission reported a large, disc-shaped object with four "legs" and figures that appeared on top of it. Gill wrote that when he waved, "the figure did the same." Eventually 38 people, including Gill, gathered to watch the main object. The Royal Australian Air Force suggested some of the lights were the planets Jupiter, Saturn and Mars. J. Allen Hynek, who investigated on site in 1973, agreed the lesser lights were stars and planets but not the main object. 1,2
