Elysium Planitia, 2018

Elysium Planitia, 2018 · On Mars · Spacecraft landing site · 4.502°N, 135.623°E

NASA's InSight lander touched down on the flat plains of Elysium Planitia on November 26, 2018, to listen for marsquakes and study the planet's interior. 1
