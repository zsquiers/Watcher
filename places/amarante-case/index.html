Amarante case

Near Nancy, France, 1982

Near Nancy, France, 1982 · UAP or anomaly site · 48.685°N, 6.150°E

At about 12:35 p.m. on October 21, 1982, a 30-year-old biologist in a suburb of a city in eastern France, usually identified as Nancy, said an ovoid, metallic object about 1.5 meters across hovered in his small front garden for 20 minutes, at one point within half a meter of him, then shot straight up and out of sight. His camera jammed when he tried to photograph it. Afterward he found that amaranth plants in the garden had wilted. He reported it to the gendarmerie that evening, and France's official UFO office, GEPAN, investigated and published its findings as Technical Note 17. GEPAN kept the witness and place anonymous, so the pin is approximate. 1,2
