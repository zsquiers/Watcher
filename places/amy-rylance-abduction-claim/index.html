Amy Rylance abduction claim

Gundiah, Queensland, Australia, 2001

Gundiah, Queensland, Australia, 2001 · UAP or anomaly site · 25.830°S, 152.540°E

Late on October 4, 2001, Petra Heller said she saw 22-year-old Amy Rylance lifted by a beam of light through a window of their property near Gundiah into a hovering craft. Rylance was later found dehydrated and disoriented at a service station in Mackay, roughly 790 km (490 miles) north. Supporters cite marks on her body and hair that seemed to have grown out; skeptics consider the case a hoax, and the people involved soon left the area. 1
