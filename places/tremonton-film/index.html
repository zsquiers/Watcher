Tremonton film

Near Tremonton, Utah, 1952

Near Tremonton, Utah, 1952 · UAP or anomaly site · 41.712°N, 112.165°W

Navy photographer Delbert C. Newhouse filmed a group of bright objects moving across the sky near Tremonton. Navy photo analysts, after more than 1,000 hours of study, concluded that they were not any known aircraft, creature or weather phenomenon. The CIA-sponsored Robertson Panel viewed the film in January 1953, watched comparison film of seagulls, and judged that the objects were most probably birds reflecting sunlight. 1
