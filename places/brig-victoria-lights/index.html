Brig Victoria lights

Strait of Sicily, 1845

Strait of Sicily, 1845 · UAP or anomaly site · 36.682°N, 13.743°E

On June 18, 1845, the brig Victoria lay becalmed between Sicily and Malta when, according to the Malta Mail, her topgallant and royal masts suddenly went over the side as if in a squall. Later, as the air filled with a stench of sulphur and an unbearable heat, three luminous bodies were seen to rise from the sea about half a mile from the ship and stay in view for about ten minutes. The British Association reprinted the account in its catalogue of luminous meteors, beside reports from Adalia, now Antalya in Turkey, and Mount Lebanon of a huge meteor seen that same evening. Charles Fort later included it in The Book of the Damned. 1,2
