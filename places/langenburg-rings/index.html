Langenburg rings

Near Langenburg, Saskatchewan, 1974

Near Langenburg, Saskatchewan, 1974 · UAP or anomaly site · 50.840°N, 101.710°W

At about 10:30 a.m. on September 1, 1974, farmer Edwin Fuhr said that while swathing canola he came upon five saucer-shaped objects hovering about a foot above the ground near a slough, spinning rapidly. His swather would not restart, and he watched for about 15 minutes until they rose very fast and silently, leaving only a grey vapor. An RCMP report found five circles on the ground that matched his description. Canada's Royal Mint marked the 50th anniversary with a coin in 2024. The pin marks the area approximately. 1,2
