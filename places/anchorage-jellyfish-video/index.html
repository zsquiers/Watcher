Anchorage "jellyfish" video

Anchorage, Alaska, 2026

Anchorage, Alaska, 2026 · UAP or anomaly site · 61.218°N, 149.900°W

At about 10:13 p.m. on June 15, 2026, Sara Jane Osborn was filming a rainbow over Anchorage when she noticed a silver-and-black object with dangling strands drifting in and around it. It vanished within about three minutes. Her video went viral in November 2026 because it was filmed near Joint Base Elmendorf-Richardson, which said it was aware of the video but had no additional information. Former AARO director Sean Kirkpatrick said the motion is similar to a kite with streamers, and others suggested a high-altitude balloon. Osborn said she and her husband found no kite or anyone flying one. 1,2
