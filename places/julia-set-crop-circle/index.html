Julia Set crop circle

Near Stonehenge, Wiltshire, 1996

Near Stonehenge, Wiltshire, 1996 · UAP or anomaly site · 51.180°N, 1.835°W

Found on July 7, 1996, in a field near Stonehenge: a spiral of about 150 circles shaped like the Julia Set fractal. Believers cite a pilot who said nothing was in the field on a flight about 45 minutes earlier, and call it proof that not all crop circles are hoaxes. Crop-circle maker Rod Dickinson claimed to be one of its makers, and researcher Colin Andrews concluded it was made by people. 1
