Highlands, Scotland, 1990

Highlands, Scotland, 1990 · UAP or anomaly site · 56.780°N, 4.020°W

In August 1990, two hikers photographed a large diamond-shaped object with a military jet nearby. The Ministry of Defence kept the image, and a print resurfaced in 2022. 1
