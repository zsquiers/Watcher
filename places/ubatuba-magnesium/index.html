Ubatuba magnesium

Brazil, 1957

Brazil, 1957 · UAP or anomaly site · 23.450°S, 45.070°W

In 1957 a Brazilian newspaper columnist printed a letter from an unidentified fisherman, who said a flying disc had exploded over a beach near Ubatuba and enclosed fragments of it. The metal was promoted as impossibly pure magnesium. The Condon study found its sample was less pure than commercial magnesium of the time and that its makeup revealed nothing about its origin. The letter writer was never identified. 1,2
