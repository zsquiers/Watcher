Solway Firth Spaceman photo

Burgh Marsh, Cumbria, England, 1964

Burgh Marsh, Cumbria, England, 1964 · UAP or anomaly site · 54.930°N, 3.040°W

On May 23, 1964, firefighter Jim Templeton photographed his five-year-old daughter on Burgh Marsh, overlooking the Solway Firth. One print appeared to show a figure in a white spacesuit behind her. Templeton said Kodak confirmed the photo was genuine, and local police found nothing suspicious. Researcher David Clarke later concluded the figure was most likely Templeton's wife, Annie, seen from behind, with her pale blue dress overexposed to white. 1
