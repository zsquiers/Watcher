Kaikoura lights

Off Kaikoura, New Zealand, 1978

Off Kaikoura, New Zealand, 1978 · UAP or anomaly site · 42.400°S, 173.700°E

In December 1978, air traffic controllers and cargo plane crews reported unexplained lights, and a TV crew filmed them from a plane on December 30 and 31. Suggested explanations include squid-fishing boat lights and Venus. 1
