Florence stadium sighting

Stadio Artemio Franchi, Florence, Italy, 1954

Stadio Artemio Franchi, Florence, Italy, 1954 · UAP or anomaly site · 43.781°N, 11.282°E

During a match between Fiorentina and Pistoiese on the afternoon of October 27, 1954, play stopped as about 10,000 people at the stadium watched objects described as cigar- or egg-shaped move fast over the ground and then stop overhead. Glittering, glassy filaments, known as angel hair, drifted down and quickly fell apart. Similar objects and falls were reported across Tuscany that day. A chemist, Professor Giovanni Canneri, analyzed some of the material. 1,2,3
