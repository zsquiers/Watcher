North Concord Air Force Station

East Mountain, Vermont, 1961

East Mountain, Vermont, 1961 · Military base · 44.550°N, 71.870°W

A Cold War radar station on East Mountain that reported an unidentified object in 1961, visible for 18 minutes, a couple of hours before Betty and Barney Hill's reported abduction in New Hampshire. Renamed Lyndonville Air Force Station in 1962, it closed in 1963. 1
