OIFAA

Lima, Peru

Lima, Peru · Government building · 12.050°S, 77.040°W

The Peruvian Air Force's office for investigating anomalous aerial phenomena, reopened in 2013 after a rise in reported sightings. Reports name it DIFAA or OIFAA. 1
