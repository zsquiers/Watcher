Coyne helicopter incident

Near Mansfield, Ohio, October 18, 1973

Near Mansfield, Ohio, October 18, 1973 · UAP or anomaly site · 40.820°N, 82.450°W

An Army Reserve UH-1H medical helicopter flying from Columbus to Cleveland, commanded by Capt. Lawrence Coyne, met a red light that closed on it at high speed. The crew said a gray, cigar-shaped object stopped over the helicopter, bathed it in green light, and that the aircraft climbed from about 1,700 to 3,500 feet even though Coyne had put it into a dive. Witnesses on the ground reported a similar object. The crew filed an official Army report, and the case is considered one of the best-documented military encounters. Skeptics have suggested an Orionid meteor; the case remains unexplained. 1
