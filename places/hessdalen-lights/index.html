Hessdalen lights

Hessdalen valley, Norway

Hessdalen valley, Norway · UAP or anomaly site · 62.790°N, 11.190°E

Unexplained lights have appeared in this valley since at least the early 1980s, peaking between 1981 and 1984. An automatic monitoring station has recorded them since 1998, and scientists still study them. 1
