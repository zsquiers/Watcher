Japan Air Lines Flight 1628

Eastern Alaska, 1986

Eastern Alaska, 1986 · UAP or anomaly site · 64.001°N, 145.712°W

On November 17, 1986, Captain Kenji Terauchi of a Japan Air Lines cargo 747, on the leg from Reykjavík to Anchorage, reported three objects pacing his plane over eastern Alaska: "two small ships and the mother ship," the largest twice the size of an aircraft carrier. Anchorage controllers advised "evasive action," but the crew said the lights stayed with them. The FAA later dismissed the radar returns as clutter and a split image of the plane, and Philip Klass argued the crew had seen Jupiter and Mars. Location is approximate. 1,2
