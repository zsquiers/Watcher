Poverty Point

Pickering, Louisiana, about 1700 to 1100 BC

Pickering, Louisiana, about 1700 to 1100 BC · Ancient site · 32.637°N, 91.407°W

Huge concentric earthen ridges and mounds built by hunter-gatherers, one of the oldest large earthworks in the Americas. Its main mound took an estimated eight million basketloads of soil. A UNESCO World Heritage Site. 1
