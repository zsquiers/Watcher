Gravity institute at UNC

Chapel Hill, North Carolina

Chapel Hill, North Carolina · Civilian research group · 35.905°N, 79.047°W

In November 1955 the University of North Carolina's trustees approved a new institute "primarily to carry on theoretical research on gravity," with funds collected by Winston-Salem industrialist Agnew Bahnson Jr., and offered its directorship to physicist Bryce DeWitt. DeWitt taught here from 1956 and in 1957 organized the Chapel Hill Conference on gravitation. 1,2
