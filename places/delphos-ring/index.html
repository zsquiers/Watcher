Delphos ring

Kansas, 1971

Kansas, 1971 · UAP or anomaly site · 39.274°N, 97.766°W

In November 1971, teenager Ronnie Johnson said a glowing, mushroom-shaped object hovered just above the ground about 75 feet from him on his family's farm, then flew off. His parents said they glimpsed it, and the family found a glowing ring on the ground that was still there the next day. Scientists said the ring was a fungus. 1
