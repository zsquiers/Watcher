Alan Godfrey encounter

Todmorden, West Yorkshire, England, 1980

Todmorden, West Yorkshire, England, 1980 · UAP or anomaly site · 53.714°N, 2.098°W

PC Alan Godfrey said that at about 5 a.m. on 29 November 1980, on patrol in Todmorden, he came across a diamond-shaped object hovering just above the road, and later described missing time and a possible abduction, as told in Jenny Randles' 1983 book The Pennine UFO Mystery. Months earlier Godfrey had been the officer who found the body of miner Zigmund Adamski on a coal heap in the town, a death the coroner called one of the most puzzling of his career; the two stories became linked in UFO lore. Godfrey later left the police; he has said he wishes he had never seen the UFO. No physical evidence of the encounter has been produced. The pin marks the town. 1,2,3
