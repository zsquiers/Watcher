Allagash abductions

Maine, 1976

Maine, 1976 · UAP or anomaly site · 46.901°N, 69.198°W

Four men on a canoe trip reported a bright object and later recalled an abduction under hypnosis. They were twin brothers Jack and Jim Weiner, Chuck Rak and their guide Charlie Foltz. In 2016 Rak said the abduction story wasn't true, though he still says they saw something in the sky. 1,2,3
