Patna light wheels

Persian Gulf, 1880

Persian Gulf, 1880 · UAP or anomaly site · 27.000°N, 51.300°E

In a December 1883 letter to the science magazine Knowledge, a writer signing himself "Lee Fore Brace" said that in May 1880, at about 11:30 on a dark, calm night aboard the British India steamer Patna in the Persian Gulf, an enormous luminous wheel appeared on each side of the ship. Each had about sixteen spokes 200 to 300 yards long, turned once every twelve seconds and glided flat on the surface, and the wheels kept pace with the ship for about twenty minutes. He said the captain and the third officer saw them too. The letter gives no position, so the pin marks the Gulf only roughly. 1
