Raël's encounter

Puy de Lassolas, near Clermont-Ferrand, France, 1973

Puy de Lassolas, near Clermont-Ferrand, France, 1973 · UAP or anomaly site · 45.727°N, 2.993°E

Claude Vorilhon, now known as Raël, says that on December 13, 1973, while walking at this volcanic crater, he saw a spaceship appear and met one of the Elohim, beings he says created humankind with advanced technology. He founded Raëlism, a UFO religion that teaches the Elohim were mistaken for gods and that prophets including the Buddha, Jesus and Muhammad were Elohim-human hybrids. In 2002 its cloning company, Clonaid, claimed to have produced a human clone, drawing wide critical scrutiny. Scholars of religion class Raëlism as a new religious movement. 1
