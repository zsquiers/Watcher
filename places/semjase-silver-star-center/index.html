Semjase Silver Star Center

Schmidrüti, Switzerland

Schmidrüti, Switzerland · UAP or anomaly site · 47.420°N, 8.930°E

The rural headquarters of FIGU, the group Billy Meier founded in the late 1970s around his claimed contacts with the Plejaren. Meier's photos and films of their "beamships" were made in the countryside around his home in the canton of Zurich. 1,2
