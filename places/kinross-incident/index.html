Kinross AFB, Michigan, 1953

Kinross AFB, Michigan, 1953 · UAP or anomaly site · 46.251°N, 84.472°W

On the evening of November 23, 1953, ground radar at Sault Ste. Marie picked up an unusual target over Lake Superior near the Soo Locks, and an F-89 Scorpion interceptor was scrambled from Kinross Air Force Base with pilot First Lieutenant Felix Moncla and radar operator Second Lieutenant Robert Wilson. Radar controllers watched the jet's return close on the target until the two blips seemed to merge into one, which carried on along the target's course; the jet never reappeared. No trace of the plane or crew was ever found. The pin marks the base the jet flew from. 1
