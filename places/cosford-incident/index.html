Cosford incident

Shropshire, England, 1993

Shropshire, England, 1993 · UAP or anomaly site · 52.640°N, 2.310°W

On March 30 and 31, 1993, military staff and police across England reported a huge triangular craft, including over RAF Cosford. Ministry of Defence investigator Nick Pope found no firm explanation; others blame a Russian rocket re-entering the atmosphere. 1
