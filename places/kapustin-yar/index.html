Kapustin Yar

Astrakhan region, Russia

Astrakhan region, Russia · Military base · 48.570°N, 45.750°E

The Soviet Union's first rocket test range, founded in 1946 and still used by Russia. UFO folklore calls it the Russian Roswell, but there is no evidence for that. 1
