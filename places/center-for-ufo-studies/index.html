Center for UFO Studies

Evanston and Chicago, Illinois, since 1973

Evanston and Chicago, Illinois, since 1973 · Civilian research group · 42.045°N, 87.688°W

Founded by J. Allen Hynek in 1973 and renamed for him after his death in 1986. It keeps archives, including the files of the defunct NICAP, and the UFOCAT catalog of reports. Its case files moved in 2020 to Albuquerque, New Mexico, where the National UFO Historical Records Center looks after them. 1,2
