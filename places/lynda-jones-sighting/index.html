Lynda Jones sighting

Didsbury, Manchester, England, 1979

Didsbury, Manchester, England, 1979 · UAP or anomaly site · 53.407°N, 2.236°W

In summer 1979, Lynda Jones and her two children saw a large orange, crescent-shaped object hovering near the River Mersey in Didsbury. She got home about 90 minutes later than she expected. Under hypnosis in 1980 she described being examined aboard the craft, and she reported burned eyes and other aftereffects. The case is documented in Philip Mantle and Carl Nagaitis's Without Consent; no official confirmation exists.
