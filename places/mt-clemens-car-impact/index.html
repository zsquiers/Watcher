Mt. Clemens car impact

25 Mile Road, near Mt. Clemens, Michigan, 1998

25 Mile Road, near Mt. Clemens, Michigan, 1998 · UAP or anomaly site · 42.690°N, 82.880°W

At about 7 p.m. on November 27, 1998, a woman driving east on 25 Mile Road about six miles north of Mt. Clemens said an intensely bright, white, basketball-sized light came silently at her car and hit it with a slight thump, leaving a cream-gray residue. In 2001, chemist Phyllis Budinger analyzed a scraping of the residue for the Center for UFO Studies. The pin marks the road approximately. 1,2
