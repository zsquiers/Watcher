Phoenix Lights

Arizona, 1997

Arizona, 1997 · UAP or anomaly site · 33.447°N, 112.076°W

On March 13, 1997, thousands reported a huge V-shaped formation of lights. The later lights were identified as military flares, but the earlier formation is still debated. 1
