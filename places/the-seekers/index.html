The Seekers

Chicago area, 1954

Chicago area, 1954 · Civilian research group · 41.878°N, 87.630°W

Dorothy Martin, a Chicago housewife who practiced automatic writing, said beings from a planet called Clarion had warned that a flood would destroy much of North America before dawn on December 21, 1954, and her followers expected a flying saucer to rescue them. Psychologists Leon Festinger, Henry Riecken and Stanley Schachter joined the group to study how believers cope when a prophecy fails, in their 1956 book When Prophecy Fails. Later archival work has alleged that many of the book's core claims were fabricated or exaggerated. The pin marks the approximate area. 1
