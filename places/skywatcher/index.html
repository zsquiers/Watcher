Reported field base near Sierra Blanca, Texas, 2025

Reported field base near Sierra Blanca, Texas, 2025 · Company · 31.170°N, 105.360°W

A private UAP research company launched in January 2025 by former Air Force helicopter pilot Jake Barber and technologist James Fowler, with financier Alex Klokus. It says it uses sensors, imagery analysis and a signaling method it calls a dog whistle to call in and record UAP, and in April 2025 it presented a taxonomy of nine UAP classes, from Class I Tetra to Class IX Egg. Reporting places its field work at a base near Sierra Blanca until July 2025. It went quiet at the end of 2025 without publishing the data it had promised, and claims that it went on to work with government agencies come from anonymous sources. 1,2,3
