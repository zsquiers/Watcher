Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory

Livermore, California

Livermore, California · Secret project site · 37.688°N, 121.707°W

The nuclear weapons laboratory that Edward Teller helped found with Ernest O. Lawrence and directed from 1958 to 1960. In the 1980s Teller's "O-Group" here developed Project Excalibur, a nuclear-pumped X-ray laser meant to shoot down dozens of missiles at once, the most ambitious idea behind the Strategic Defense Initiative; it failed key tests, and Teller and Lowell Wood were accused of overselling it. 1,2,3
