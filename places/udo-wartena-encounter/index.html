Udo Wartena encounter

Montana, May 1940

Montana, May 1940 · UAP or anomaly site · 46.320°N, 111.520°W

In May 1940, prospector Udo Wartena said a landed craft appeared near his mountain claim in Montana and its occupants spoke with him and invited him aboard. He did not tell his story publicly until decades later. Location is approximate. 1
