Don Wood Jr. sky creature story

Near Battle Mountain, Nevada, 1925

Near Battle Mountain, Nevada, 1925 · UAP or anomaly site · 40.640°N, 116.930°W

UNVERIFIED: a pilot later named as Don Wood Jr. said that in 1925 he and three friends landed their biplanes on a mesa near Battle Mountain and watched a round, glowing disc about 8 feet across, then a larger one, before both flew off. The story first appeared as an anonymous reader's letter in Flying Saucers magazine in October 1959, and Trevor James Constable later used it to argue that some UFOs are living creatures. 1,2
