Falcon Lake incident

Manitoba, Canada, 1967

Manitoba, Canada, 1967 · UAP or anomaly site · 49.700°N, 95.300°W

On May 20, 1967, prospector Stefan Michalak said he was burned by exhaust from a landed craft, and he had grid-shaped burns on his chest. Canadian and U.S. investigators could not explain the case. 1
