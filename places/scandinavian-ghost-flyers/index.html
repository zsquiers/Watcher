Scandinavian ghost flyers

Northern Sweden, Norway and Finland, 1933 to 1937

Northern Sweden, Norway and Finland, 1933 to 1937 · UAP or anomaly site · 66.600°N, 20.500°E

In the winter of 1933 to 1934, people across northern Sweden, Norway and Finland reported unknown aircraft, nicknamed the Ghost Flyer and the Flying X. In January 1934 the Swedish government ordered the Air Force to investigate after questions in Parliament, and by March the three countries' militaries had received 487 reports. It is one of the earliest government investigations of unidentified aircraft. The pin marks the region approximately. 1,2
