Strieber's cabin (Communion)

Near Pine Bush, Ulster County, New York, December 26, 1985

Near Pine Bush, Ulster County, New York, December 26, 1985 · UAP or anomaly site · 41.640°N, 74.340°W

Horror novelist Whitley Strieber says that on the night after Christmas 1985 he was taken from his upstate New York cabin by beings he called "the visitors," and he described being examined. His 1987 book Communion, with its cover painting of a gray-skinned, large-eyed being, became a No. 1 bestseller and shaped the popular image of the "Grey" alien. Skeptics such as neurologist Steven Novella point to sleep paralysis and hypnagogic experiences. UNVERIFIED. The pin marks the approximate area. 1
