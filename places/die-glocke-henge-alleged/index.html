Die Glocke "Henge" (alleged)

Near Ludwikowice Kłodzkie, Poland

Near Ludwikowice Kłodzkie, Poland · Secret project site · 50.624°N, 16.498°E

A ring of concrete pillars near the wartime Wenceslaus mine in Lower Silesia, which Polish writer Igor Witkowski said was used to tether Die Glocke ("The Bell"), an alleged Nazi device, during tests. Writer Jason Colavito says it is the remains of an ordinary industrial cooling tower (see the claims ledger). The pin marks the approximate area. 1
