Canary Islands, Spain, 1976

Canary Islands, Spain, 1976 · UAP or anomaly site · 28.100°N, 15.700°W

On June 22, 1976, several hundred civilians and military personnel across Tenerife, La Palma, La Gomera, Gran Canaria and at sea watched a light in the sky for more than 40 minutes. Two witnesses claimed to have seen occupants inside a craft, but the military judge who wrote the Spanish Air Force report questioned that claim. The report was declassified in 1994; much of it had already been published in 1977. 1
