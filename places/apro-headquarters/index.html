APRO headquarters

Tucson, Arizona, 1960 to 1988

Tucson, Arizona, 1960 to 1988 · Civilian research group · 32.223°N, 110.975°W

The Aerial Phenomena Research Organization, one of the earliest civilian UFO groups, founded by Jim and Coral Lorenzen in Sturgeon Bay, Wisconsin, in 1952 and run from Tucson from 1960. It was known for field investigations and its newsletter, the APRO Bulletin, and was more open than NICAP to occupant and abduction reports. Bill Moore was its research director. It folded after Coral Lorenzen's death in 1988. 1
