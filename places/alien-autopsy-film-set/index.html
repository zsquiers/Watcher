Alien Autopsy film set

Camden Town, London, 1995

Camden Town, London, 1995 · UAP or anomaly site · 51.539°N, 0.138°W

In 1995 British producer Ray Santilli released grainy black-and-white footage he said showed an autopsy on an alien from the Roswell crash. Fox broadcast it in August 1995. In 2006 Santilli and Gary Shoefield admitted they had made it on a set in an empty flat here, using plaster dummies filled with sheep brains and raspberry jam (see the claims ledger). 1
