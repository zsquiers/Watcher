Serpent Mound

Peebles, Ohio, about 300 BC or AD 1070

Peebles, Ohio, about 300 BC or AD 1070 · Ancient site · 39.025°N, 83.430°W

A 1,348-foot earthen effigy of a snake with a coiled tail and an oval at its head, the largest serpent effigy in the world. Archaeologists debate whether the Adena or the later Fort Ancient culture built it. Its head appears to align with the summer solstice sunset. It sits on the edge of an ancient impact crater, which has fueled alternative theories. Part of a UNESCO World Heritage listing with Ohio's Hopewell earthworks. 1
