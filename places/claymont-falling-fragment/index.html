Claymont falling fragment

Claymont, Delaware, 2014

Claymont, Delaware, 2014 · UAP or anomaly site · 39.801°N, 75.460°W

Around midnight on June 29, 2014, four people in Claymont said up to 24 silent orange-red lights drifted over their neighborhood, and one of them filmed it on a phone. As the lights moved off, one that seemed to falter dropped something that burned all the way down and landed four houses up the street, where the flames were stamped out. The fragment was kept for investigators from the Mutual UFO Network (MUFON) and analyzed by chemist Phyllis Budinger. 1
