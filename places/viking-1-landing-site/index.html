Viking 1 landing site

Chryse Planitia, 1976

Chryse Planitia, 1976 · On Mars · Spacecraft landing site · 22.697°N, 47.950°W

NASA's Viking 1 touched down in western Chryse Planitia on July 20, 1976, the first U.S. landing on Mars. It photographed the surface and tested the soil for signs of life. 1
