Mexico's congressional UFO hearing

Chamber of Deputies, Mexico City, 2023

Chamber of Deputies, Mexico City, 2023 · UAP or anomaly site · 19.431°N, 99.120°W

On September 12, 2023, Mexico's Congress of the Union held its first public hearing on UFOs. Journalist Jaime Maussan presented two small, mummified bodies he called "nonhuman beings" from Peru (see the claims ledger). 1
