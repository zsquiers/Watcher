Cynthia Everett's light

Camden, Maine, 1808

Camden, Maine, 1808 · UAP or anomaly site · 44.210°N, 69.065°W

On July 22, 1808, schoolteacher Cynthia Everett wrote in her diary about a light that moved across the sky, which she first took for a meteor and then decided was something else. It survives only as a diary entry. 1
