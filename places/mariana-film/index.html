Great Falls, Montana, 1950

Great Falls, Montana, 1950 · UAP or anomaly site · 47.505°N, 111.285°W

On August 15, 1950, Nick Mariana, manager of the Great Falls Electrics baseball team, filmed two bright, silvery objects over the city's Legion Stadium for about sixteen seconds, one of the earliest motion pictures of a UFO. The Air Force said the objects were sun reflections off two F-94 jet fighters, and the 1953 Robertson Panel agreed. A 1956 analysis by Douglas Aircraft engineer Robert M. L. Baker called the jet explanation "quite strained." The results of the investigations have been inconclusive. 1,2
