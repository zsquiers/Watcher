Las Vegas backyard "aliens"

Northwest Las Vegas, 2023

Northwest Las Vegas, 2023 · UAP or anomaly site · 36.250°N, 115.270°W

Around 11:50 p.m. on April 30, 2023, a Las Vegas police officer's body camera caught a light streaking low across the sky, and people in Nevada, Utah and eastern California reported a fireball. About 40 minutes later a family in the northwest valley called 911 to say two beings, 8 to 10 feet tall with big shiny eyes and "100% not human," were in their backyard. NASA said the light was most likely a small meteor that landed nowhere near Las Vegas; police found no craft and closed the case. UNVERIFIED. The pin marks the approximate area. 1,2,3,4
