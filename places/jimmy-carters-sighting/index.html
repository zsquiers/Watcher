Jimmy Carter's sighting

Leary, Georgia, 1969

Leary, Georgia, 1969 · UAP or anomaly site · 31.486°N, 84.513°W

On January 6, 1969, before a Lions Club meeting, future president Jimmy Carter and others saw a bright light in the evening sky that he later reported as a UFO. Skeptics concluded it was Venus; a 2016 suggestion points to a barium cloud released that evening for upper-atmosphere research. 1,2
