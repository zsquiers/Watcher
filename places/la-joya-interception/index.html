La Joya air base, Peru, 1980

La Joya air base, Peru, 1980 · UAP or anomaly site · 16.790°S, 71.880°W

In April 1980, Peruvian Air Force pilot Óscar Santa María Huertas took off from the La Joya air base near Arequipa in a Soviet-built Sukhoi attack jet to intercept an object hovering near the base. He said he fired 64 rounds at it with no effect, and that over about 22 minutes it climbed, stopped abruptly and outran his jet. The case is among the best known in Peru, and he told it in detail in a chapter of Leslie Kean's 2010 book. The pin marks the area approximately. 1,2
