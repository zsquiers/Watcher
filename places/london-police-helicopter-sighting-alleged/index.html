London police helicopter sighting (alleged)

Central London, 1997

Central London, 1997 · UAP or anomaly site · 51.487°N, 0.124°W

UNVERIFIED: in Roy Teague's 2026 book, PC Paul Hammond and Army Warrant Officer David Frampton say that in 1997, aboard a police helicopter over central London near the MI6 building and Parliament, they circled a 40 to 50-foot metallic craft shaped like a paper dart. Heathrow and Gatwick reported nothing on radar. On the infrared camera its rear heated up before it turned and shot into what they described as a hole in the sky that closed behind it. They say they never reported it, fearing the ridicule another officer had faced after a 1979 helicopter sighting. No footage or records have been released. The pin marks the approximate area. 1
