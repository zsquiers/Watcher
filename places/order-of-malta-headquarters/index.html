Order of Malta headquarters

Palazzo Malta, Rome

Palazzo Malta, Rome · Government building · 41.906°N, 12.480°E

The Sovereign Military Order of Malta, or Knights of Malta, is a Catholic lay religious order that traces its origins to about 1099 in Jerusalem. It has no territory of its own but is recognized as a sovereign entity under international law, with about 13,500 members, United Nations observer status since 1994, and extraterritorial headquarters here in Rome. Reporting has named CIA directors John McCone and William Casey as members, though neither publicly acknowledged it, and William Colby said he declined an invitation to join. In 2011 journalist Seymour Hersh claimed leaders of the Joint Special Operations Command were members, which its former head denied. Conspiracy theories have tied the order to crimes including the Kennedy assassination. 1,2,3,4
