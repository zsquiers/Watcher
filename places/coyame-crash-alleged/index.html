Coyame crash (alleged)

Chihuahua, Mexico, 1974

Chihuahua, Mexico, 1974 · UAP or anomaly site · 29.460°N, 105.095°W

UNVERIFIED: an anonymous 1992 document claimed that in 1974 a UFO tracked on radar collided with a small plane from El Paso, crashed near Coyame and was recovered by the Mexican Army before U.S. forces seized it (see the claims ledger). The pin marks the town. 1
