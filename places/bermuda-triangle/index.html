Bermuda Triangle

Western North Atlantic

Western North Atlantic · UAP or anomaly site · 25.500°N, 70.400°W

A loosely defined area between Miami, Bermuda, and Puerto Rico that popular books linked to the disappearance of ships and aircraft, including the Navy's Flight 19 in 1945. The U.S. Coast Guard and the insurer Lloyd's of London have found no more losses there than in other heavily traveled ocean areas. 1
