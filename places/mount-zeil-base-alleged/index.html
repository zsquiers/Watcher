Mount Zeil base (alleged)

MacDonnell Ranges, Northern Territory, Australia

MacDonnell Ranges, Northern Territory, Australia · Secret project site · 23.402°S, 132.396°E

UNVERIFIED: one of four mountains Pat Price named in 1973 as hiding underground UFO bases. Mount Zeil, at 1,531 meters, is the highest peak in the Northern Territory. Army remote viewers later revisited it as a blind training target in Skip Atwater's Project 8200 (see the claims ledger). No physical evidence of any base has ever been found; the pin marks the mountain, not a confirmed site. 1,2,3
