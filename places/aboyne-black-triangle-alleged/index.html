Aboyne black triangle (alleged)

Aboyne, Aberdeenshire, Scotland, 1989

Aboyne, Aberdeenshire, Scotland, 1989 · UAP or anomaly site · 57.076°N, 2.780°W

UNVERIFIED: an anonymous former Grampian Police officer, called Officer F by Roy Teague, says that at about 1 or 2 a.m. in 1989, outside Aboyne police station, streetlights and house lights went out in a rolling wave, his patrol car died, and a silent matte-black triangle passed 50 to 60 feet overhead heading west. He says the dispatcher then asked where he had been for 30 minutes, though only about a minute had passed for him, and that a recent MRI found a small metallic object beside his right eye. His identity, medical records and any police log have not been made public. The pin marks the town. 1,2
