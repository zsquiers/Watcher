Fra Mauro highlands, 1971

Fra Mauro highlands, 1971 · On the Moon · Spacecraft landing site · 3.645°S, 17.471°W

Alan Shepard and Edgar Mitchell landed the lunar module Antares here in February 1971. Mitchell later became one of the best-known astronauts to say he believed alien visits were real. 1,2
