Broad Haven school sighting

Broad Haven, Pembrokeshire, Wales, 1977

Broad Haven, Pembrokeshire, Wales, 1977 · UAP or anomaly site · 51.780°N, 5.100°W

On February 4, 1977, pupils at Broad Haven Primary School said a yellow cigar-shaped craft had landed in a field next to the school; the headmaster separated them and got them to draw what they saw, and the drawings broadly matched. More reports followed that spring, and the press nicknamed the area the Welsh Triangle. Officials who investigated the sightings suspected pranksters, and a local businessman said in 1996 that he had wandered the area in a silver suit in 1977 as a prank. The case has drawn comparison with the 1994 Ariel School sighting in Zimbabwe. 1,2
