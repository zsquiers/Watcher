Gravity Research Foundation

New Boston, New Hampshire

New Boston, New Hampshire · Civilian research group · 42.976°N, 71.693°W

Roger Babson, the economist who founded Babson College, set up the foundation in 1948 to find ways to shield against gravity, and held its conferences here through the late 1960s. Over time it turned from trying to block gravity to trying to understand it. Its annual essay contest has been won by at least six later Nobel laureates in physics. 1
