Chilean Navy helicopter video

Off the coast west of Santiago, Chile, 2014

Off the coast west of Santiago, Chile, 2014 · UAP or anomaly site · 33.200°S, 71.750°W

Just before 2 p.m. on November 11, 2014, the crew of a Chilean Navy AS532 Cougar helicopter on a training flight up the coast west of Santiago filmed an object with its infrared camera for about ten minutes. It was not visible to the naked eye or on radar, did not answer radio calls, and at two points seemed to release a dark trail. Chile's official UFO committee, CEFAA, studied the footage for two years and released it in January 2017 as unexplained. The pin marks the area approximately. 1,2
