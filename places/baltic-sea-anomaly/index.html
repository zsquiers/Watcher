Baltic Sea anomaly

Gulf of Bothnia, between Sweden and Finland, 2011

Gulf of Bothnia, between Sweden and Finland, 2011 · UAP or anomaly site · 61.377°N, 18.448°E

A roughly 60-meter round shape on the seabed, about 90 meters down, seen on side-scan sonar by Swedish treasure hunters Peter Lindberg and Dennis Åsberg of Ocean X in June 2011. Its saucer-like look drew comparisons to the Millennium Falcon and claims of a crashed craft or ancient structure. Stockholm University geologist Volker Brüchert found the recovered samples were granite, gneiss, and sandstone, and experts consider it a natural glacial rock formation. The pin uses the coordinates circulated online, 61°22.6'N 18°26.9'E; Ocean X has not officially confirmed them. 1,2,3
