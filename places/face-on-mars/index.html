Face on Mars

Cydonia, 1976

Cydonia, 1976 · On Mars · UAP or anomaly site · 40.750°N, 9.460°W

A mesa about 1.5 kilometers across in the Cydonia region. In a picture taken by NASA's Viking 1 orbiter on July 25, 1976, it looked like a human face, and NASA's own caption said shadows gave the illusion of a nose and mouth. Some, including Richard C. Hoagland, took it as evidence of a lost Martian civilization. Sharper images from later orbiters show an ordinary eroded hill. 1,2
