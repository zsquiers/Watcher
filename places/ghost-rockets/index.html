Lake Kölmjärv, Sweden, 1946

Lake Kölmjärv, Sweden, 1946 · UAP or anomaly site · 66.350°N, 22.850°E

From May to December 1946, Swedes reported about 2,000 rocket-like objects, and the Defense Staff judged 225 of them to be real physical objects. On July 19, a winged object reportedly crashed into Lake Kölmjärv, but a three-week military search found nothing. Many feared Soviet tests of captured German missiles, but investigators found no rocket fragments, and many sightings were probably meteors. The pin marks the lake approximately. 1
