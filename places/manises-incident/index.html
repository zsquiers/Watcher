Manises incident

Valencia, Spain, 1979

Valencia, Spain, 1979 · UAP or anomaly site · 39.490°N, 0.480°W

On November 11, 1979, a passenger jet diverted to Valencia's Manises airport after its crew saw red lights following the plane. A Spanish Air Force fighter was sent up, and Spain later declassified the case files. 1
