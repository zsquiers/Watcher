Malmgren shootdown claim

Marshall Islands (claimed), 1962

Marshall Islands (claimed), 1962 · UAP or anomaly site · 7.100°N, 171.200°E

Economist Harald Malmgren said in a 2025 interview that the U.S. shot down a non-human craft with a classified directed-energy weapon during a 1962 nuclear test in the Marshall Islands, and that he handled the debris. No evidence has been made public, and FBI files released in May 2025 contradict parts of his account of his jobs and clearances at the time. The U.S. held no nuclear tests in the Marshall Islands in 1962; that year's Pacific tests were at Johnston Atoll and Christmas Island, including the high-altitude Starfish Prime shot.
