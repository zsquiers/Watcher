Research Institute for Advanced Studies

Baltimore, Maryland

Baltimore, Maryland · Company · 39.290°N, 76.612°W

Founded in 1955 by George Bunker of the Glenn L. Martin Company, ancestor of Lockheed Martin, to support fundamental research. That year Martin put German physicists Burkhard Heim and Pascual Jordan under contract, and the New York Herald-Tribune reported that the company was building a laboratory for the institute as part of its gravity research. It became Martin Marietta Laboratories in 1973 and turned away from basic research. 1,2
