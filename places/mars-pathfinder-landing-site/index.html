Mars Pathfinder landing site

Ares Vallis, 1997

Ares Vallis, 1997 · On Mars · Spacecraft landing site · 19.097°N, 33.254°W

Mars Pathfinder bounced to a landing on airbags in Ares Vallis on July 4, 1997, and released Sojourner, the first rover to drive on Mars. 1
