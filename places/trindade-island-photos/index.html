South Atlantic, Brazil, 1958

South Atlantic, Brazil, 1958 · UAP or anomaly site · 20.510°S, 29.320°W

On January 16, 1958, a photographer aboard a Brazilian Navy ship took four photos of a Saturn-shaped object. They were released to the press with the government's approval, and skeptics believe they were faked. 1
