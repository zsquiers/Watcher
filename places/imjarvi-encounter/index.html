Imjärvi encounter

Near Heinola, Finland, 1970

Near Heinola, Finland, 1970 · UAP or anomaly site · 61.203°N, 26.035°E

At about 4 p.m. on January 7, 1970, skiers Aarno Heinonen and Esko Viljo said that near the village of Imjärvi a fire-colored, saucer-shaped light appeared near them and released a mist, and that for a couple of seconds they saw a small being in a pale green suit holding a black device. The whole event lasted about ten seconds. Both felt very ill afterward and saw a doctor, and Heinonen was unable to work for months. The pin marks the area approximately. 1,2
