St. Clair triangle

Illinois, 2000

Illinois, 2000 · UAP or anomaly site · 38.604°N, 89.807°W

Before dawn on January 5, 2000, a business owner and police officers in Highland, Lebanon, Shiloh and Millstadt reported a huge, low, nearly silent triangular object with bright lights. Scott Air Force Base reported nothing on radar. Skeptoid's Brian Dunning argues it was an advertising blimp in transit, which the FAA also told a newspaper at the time. Others say a blimp can't explain reports of hovering and fast acceleration. 1,2
