Aetherius Society

Fulham Road, London

Fulham Road, London · Civilian research group · 51.478°N, 0.203°W

George King said that on May 8, 1954, in his flat in Maida Vale, a voice told him to become "the voice of Interplanetary Parliament," and that he later received telepathic messages from a Venusian "Cosmic Master" named Aetherius. The society he founded in the mid-1950s aims to work with these Masters, and says prayer energy stored in "Spiritual Energy Batteries" has helped prevent wars and disasters. Some historians consider it the first UFO religion. Its London headquarters has been here since 1958. 1,2
