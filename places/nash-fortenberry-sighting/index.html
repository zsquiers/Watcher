Nash-Fortenberry sighting

Chesapeake Bay, Virginia, 1952

Chesapeake Bay, Virginia, 1952 · UAP or anomaly site · 37.000°N, 76.300°W

On the night of July 14, 1952, Pan American pilots William Nash and William Fortenberry were flying a DC-4 at 8,000 feet toward Norfolk when they saw six glowing red-orange discs in tight formation far below, joined by two more. The objects flipped on edge, reversed sharply and sped away. The pilots radioed a report and were interviewed by Air Force investigators the next morning. Project Blue Book listed the case as unknown. Donald Menzel suggested ground lights distorted by haze, and others have proposed a mirage. 1,2
