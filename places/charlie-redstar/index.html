Carman, Manitoba, Canada, 1975

Carman, Manitoba, Canada, 1975 · UAP or anomaly site · 49.500°N, 98.000°W

In 1975 people around this prairie town reported a red ball of light, nicknamed Charlie Redstar, for months, and crowds gathered in fields to watch for it. 1
