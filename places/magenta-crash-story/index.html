Lombardy, Italy, 1933

Lombardy, Italy, 1933 · UAP or anomaly site · 45.465°N, 8.884°E

According to papers that Italian researcher Roberto Pinotti says reached him anonymously from 1996, a craft crashed near Magenta, west of Milan, on June 13, 1933. Mussolini is said to have set up a secret study group, Gabinetto RS/33, under the inventor Guglielmo Marconi, which stored the wreck in the SIAI-Marchetti hangars at Vergiate. No trace of the group has been found in Italian archives, and Italian researchers at CICAP and CISU consider the papers probably forged. 1,2
