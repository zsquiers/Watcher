Corporal Armando Valdés case

Near Putre, Chile, April 1977

Near Putre, Chile, April 1977 · UAP or anomaly site · 18.197°S, 69.560°W

On April 25, 1977, Chilean Army Cpl. Armando Valdés reportedly vanished for about 15 minutes while his patrol watched lights near their camp. His men said he returned with several days' beard growth, and his watch showed a date five days ahead. 1
