Bariloche incident

Argentina, 1995

Argentina, 1995 · UAP or anomaly site · 41.150°S, 71.310°W

On July 31, 1995, an Aerolíneas Argentinas flight approaching Bariloche reported a bright object pacing the plane, and the airport's lights reportedly failed during the landing. 1
