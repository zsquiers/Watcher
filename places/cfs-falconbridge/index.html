CFS Falconbridge

Near Sudbury, Ontario, Canada, 1975

Near Sudbury, Ontario, Canada, 1975 · Military base · 46.580°N, 80.800°W

A Canadian air defence radar station. On November 11, 1975, its staff tracked a brilliantly lit object on radar and watched it through binoculars, according to a NORAD report. 1,2
