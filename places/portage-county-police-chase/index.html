Portage County police chase

Ravenna, Ohio, to Conway, Pennsylvania, 1966

Ravenna, Ohio, to Conway, Pennsylvania, 1966 · UAP or anomaly site · 41.158°N, 81.242°W

Before dawn on April 17, 1966, Portage County sheriff's deputies Dale Spaur and Wilbur Neff chased a bright, saucer-shaped object east from near Ravenna at speeds over 100 miles per hour, about 80 miles into Pennsylvania. Officers from other towns joined along the way, and the police chief of Mantua photographed an object. Project Blue Book's Major Hector Quintanilla concluded the deputies had seen a satellite and then the planet Venus. J. Allen Hynek's office disagreed, noting that Venus had risen too recently to be mistaken for the object. The chase is said to have inspired the police chase in Close Encounters of the Third Kind. Spaur's life fell apart in the following months. 1,2
