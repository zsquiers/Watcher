Aristarchus crater

Oceanus Procellarum

Oceanus Procellarum · On the Moon · UAP or anomaly site · 23.700°N, 47.400°W

More reports of transient lunar phenomena, brief glows and color changes on the surface, come from here than from anywhere else on the Moon: 122 by 2007. The Lunar Prospector probe later confirmed radon gas escaping from the crater, one possible cause (see the claims ledger). 1
