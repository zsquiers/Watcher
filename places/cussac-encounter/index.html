Cussac encounter

Cussac, Cantal, France, 1967

Cussac, Cantal, France, 1967 · UAP or anomaly site · 45.000°N, 2.997°E

On August 29, 1967, a 13-year-old boy and his 9-year-old sister tending cattle near Cussac told gendarmes they saw four small, dark figures near a spherical object, which rose, glowed intensely and disappeared with a whistling sound. GEPAN, the UFO office of the French space agency CNES, investigated in 1978. Later inquiries questioned details of the account, and critics have proposed a helicopter, without an agreed explanation. 1
