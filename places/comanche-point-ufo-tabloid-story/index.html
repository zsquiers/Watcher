Comanche Point UFO (tabloid story)

Grand Canyon, Arizona, 1994

Grand Canyon, Arizona, 1994 · UAP or anomaly site · 36.093°N, 111.803°W

FALSE: on September 27, 1994, the tabloid Weekly World News ran a cover story claiming that a 4,000-year-old UFO had been found in limestone rubble below Comanche Point, a 7,073-foot summit on the Grand Canyon's East Rim (see the claims ledger). No wreckage or other evidence has ever surfaced. The pin marks the point. 1,2
