Mount Kuromanta

Kazuno, Akita, Japan

Kazuno, Akita, Japan · Ancient site · 40.284°N, 140.821°E

A small, cone-shaped hill officially named Kuromatayama, about 2 km northeast of the Jōmon-era Ōyu Stone Circles, a UNESCO World Heritage site, with the Honmiya Shrine on its summit. Locals call it Kuromanta, and it is popularly called Japan's oldest pyramid: a survey in the early 1990s reported a flattened summit and traces of shaping on its slopes, and Jōmon and Yayoi pottery fragments have been found near the top. Some also tell of UFO lights in the area. UNVERIFIED: the pyramid idea rests on local accounts and popular write-ups, and no published archaeological report confirming an artificial structure was found.
