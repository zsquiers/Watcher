Thakung, near Pangong Tso, India, 2012 to 2013

Thakung, near Pangong Tso, India, 2012 to 2013 · UAP or anomaly site · 33.950°N, 78.700°E

From August 2012 to February 2013, Indian troops at Thakung near Pangong Tso lake in Ladakh, close to the disputed border with China, logged 329 sightings of bright unidentified objects, and Indian officials feared Chinese drones were crossing the Line of Actual Control. At the Army's request, the Indian Institute of Astrophysics sent two astronomers, who identified the two objects as the planets Jupiter and Venus, unusually bright in the thin, clear air at that altitude.
