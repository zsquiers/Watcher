Cangzhou air base sighting

Cangzhou, Hebei, China, 1998

Cangzhou, Hebei, China, 1998 · UAP or anomaly site · 38.304°N, 116.839°E

On October 19, 1998, according to the Hebei Daily, two military jets were ordered up to intercept a low-flying object that suddenly appeared over a military airbase at Cangzhou, southeast of Beijing. It was described as looking like a "short-legged mushroom" with two beams of light shining down from its underside. When the jets approached, it climbed at "ghostlike" speed to more than 20,000 meters and disappeared from radar and sight. The South China Morning Post called it the only officially confirmed UFO sighting in China. The pin marks the city, not the base. 1,2
