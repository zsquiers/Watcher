Bridgewater Triangle

Southeastern Massachusetts

Southeastern Massachusetts · UAP or anomaly site · 41.904°N, 71.077°W

About 200 square miles of southeastern Massachusetts with corners at Abington, Freetown, and Rehoboth, a name coined by writer Loren Coleman. It has a long history of reported UFOs, strange creatures, and other anomalies, and includes the Hockomock Swamp. 1
