Mount Shasta and the I AM Activity

Mount Shasta, California

Mount Shasta, California · Civilian research group · 41.409°N, 122.195°W

Guy Ballard said that while hiking on Mount Shasta he met a man who introduced himself as Saint Germain, an "ascended master," and was taught by him. With his wife Edna, Ballard founded the I AM Activity in Chicago in the early 1930s; it had up to a million followers in 1938. Some historians consider it, rather than the Aetherius Society, the first UFO religion. 1,2
