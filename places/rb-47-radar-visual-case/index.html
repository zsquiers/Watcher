RB-47 radar-visual case

Mississippi to Oklahoma, 1957

Mississippi to Oklahoma, 1957 · UAP or anomaly site · 32.300°N, 93.800°W

On July 17, 1957, an Air Force RB-47 electronic-reconnaissance jet from Forbes AFB, Kansas, picked up an unexplained radar signal on its monitoring gear. For more than an hour, across Mississippi, Louisiana, Texas and Oklahoma, the crew saw a bright light that ground radar also tracked, sometimes vanishing from all three at once. Blue Book blamed an airliner. The Condon report studied it as its Case 5 and concluded that the phenomenon remains unidentified. Skeptic Philip Klass proposed a meteor, a star and radar quirks.
