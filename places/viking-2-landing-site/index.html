Utopia Planitia, 1976

Utopia Planitia, 1976 · On Mars · Spacecraft landing site · 48.269°N, 134.010°E

NASA's Viking 2 lander set down on the plains of Utopia Planitia in 1976 and, like Viking 1, ran experiments looking for microbial life in the soil. 1
