Colares, Brazil, 1977

Colares, Brazil, 1977 · UAP or anomaly site · 0.936°S, 48.280°W

In 1977, residents of this Amazon island town reported lights that fired burning beams at people. The Brazilian Air Force sent a team under Capt. Uyrangê Hollanda to investigate, and its files were later released. 1
