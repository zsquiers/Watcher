Westall school sighting

Melbourne, Australia, 1966

Melbourne, Australia, 1966 · UAP or anomaly site · 37.940°S, 145.140°E

On April 6, 1966, more than 200 students and teachers at two Westall schools reported a craft descending into a nearby field before speeding away. No official explanation was ever given. 1
