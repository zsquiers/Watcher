Berwyn Mountain incident

Llandrillo, Denbighshire, Wales, 1974

Llandrillo, Denbighshire, Wales, 1974 · UAP or anomaly site · 52.923°N, 3.438°W

On the evening of 23 January 1974, people around the Berwyn Mountains reported a loud bang and bright lights on the hillsides, and ufologists later claimed a UFO had crashed and been secretly recovered by the military, dubbed the “Roswelsh incident” by tabloids. The Institute of Geological Sciences recorded a magnitude 3.5 earthquake at 8:38 p.m., felt across north Wales, and a bright meteor was widely seen over Wales and northern England that night; declassified Ministry of Defence files attribute the event to the two together. A crash big enough to cause such a shock would have left an obvious crater, and none was found. 1
