Piedmont, Missouri, 1973 to 1981

Piedmont, Missouri, 1973 to 1981 · Civilian research group · 37.155°N, 90.696°W

After residents of Piedmont and Wayne County reported strange lights in the sky in early 1973, physicist Harley Rutledge of Southeast Missouri State University began a field study that April, one of the first by scientists with instruments. His team watched with telescopes, cameras, spectrum analyzers, audio detectors and a gravity meter, and logged more than 140 UFOs by 1977. Rutledge said many could be explained as aircraft or atmospheric effects, and he did not embrace the idea that any were alien craft. In 2023 Missouri named Piedmont and Wayne County the state's UFO capitals. 1,2
