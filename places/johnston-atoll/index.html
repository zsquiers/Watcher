Johnston Atoll

Central Pacific, nuclear test site 1958 and 1962

Central Pacific, nuclear test site 1958 and 1962 · Military base · 16.730°N, 169.534°W

A U.S. military atoll about 1,300 km (800 miles) southwest of Hawaii. In 1962 it was the launch site for Operation Fishbowl, a series of high-altitude nuclear tests carried on Thor rockets, including Starfish Prime and Bluegill Triple Prime. One failed launch, Bluegill Prime, exploded on the pad in July 1962 and contaminated the island with plutonium. 1,2
