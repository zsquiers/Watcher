Xiaoshan Airport closure

Hangzhou, China, 2010

Hangzhou, China, 2010 · UAP or anomaly site · 30.230°N, 120.430°E

On July 7, 2010, Hangzhou's airport halted flights for about an hour after an unidentified object was reported. An investigation by members of UFO research groups from Beijing and Shanghai concluded it may have been an aircraft, possibly military. 1,2
