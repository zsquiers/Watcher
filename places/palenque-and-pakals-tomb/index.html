Palenque and Pakal's tomb

Chiapas, Mexico, about AD 683

Chiapas, Mexico, about AD 683 · Ancient site · 17.484°N, 92.046°W

A Maya city whose Temple of the Inscriptions holds the tomb of King Pakal. In the 1960s Erich von Däniken claimed the carved lid shows an astronaut at the controls of a spacecraft. Mayanists read it as Pakal falling into the underworld beneath the world tree, a common Maya theme. 1
