São Paulo state, Brazil, 1986

São Paulo state, Brazil, 1986 · UAP or anomaly site · 23.180°S, 45.880°W

On May 19, 1986, radar tracked about 20 unknown objects over southeastern Brazil and the Brazilian Air Force scrambled jets. Days later, the Air Force minister discussed the event publicly. 1
