Bledsoe Cape Fear sighting

Cape Fear River near Fayetteville, North Carolina, January 2007

Cape Fear River near Fayetteville, North Carolina, January 2007 · UAP or anomaly site · 34.970°N, 78.860°W

Chris Bledsoe, a home builder struggling with Crohn's disease and a failing business, says that while fishing on the Cape Fear River with his son and coworkers in early January 2007 he saw glowing orbs, encountered small beings, lost about four hours, and was later healed of his illness. He says others with him shared the encounter. Bledsoe says the encounters continued, and he has since drawn visits from scientists, military officers, and members of Congress. His account is told in his 2023 book UFO of God. UNVERIFIED: the core claims rest on his and his family's testimony. The pin marks the approximate area. 1,2
