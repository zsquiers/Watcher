Campeche, Mexico, 2004

Campeche, Mexico, 2004 · UAP or anomaly site · 19.850°N, 90.530°W

On March 5, 2004, a Mexican Air Force surveillance plane recorded 11 objects on infrared. The Defense Ministry released the footage, and many analysts attribute it to distant oil-platform flares. 1
