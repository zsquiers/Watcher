Heaven's Gate

Rancho Santa Fe, California, 1997

Rancho Santa Fe, California, 1997 · Civilian research group · 33.020°N, 117.203°W

A new religious movement founded in 1974 by Marshall Applewhite and Bonnie Nettles, whose theology scholars describe as a mix of Christian millenarianism, New Age belief and ufology. Members believed they could become immortal extraterrestrial beings and ascend to the "Next Level," at first aboard a UFO. On March 26, 1997, deputies found that 39 members had died in a house here in a coordinated ritual suicide timed to the closest approach of Comet Hale-Bopp. 1
