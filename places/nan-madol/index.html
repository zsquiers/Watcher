Nan Madol

Pohnpei, Micronesia

Pohnpei, Micronesia · Ancient site · 6.844°N, 158.336°E

The capital of the Saudeleur dynasty until about 1628: 92 artificial islets of stone and coral linked by tidal canals. James Churchward claimed it was part of the lost continent of Mu, ideas considered pseudoscience, and Graham Hancock's Netflix series Ancient Apocalypse made false claims about the site's age. 1
