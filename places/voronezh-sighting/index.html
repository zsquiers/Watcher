Voronezh sighting

Russia, 1989

Russia, 1989 · UAP or anomaly site · 51.660°N, 39.200°E

In late September 1989, children reported a landed craft and tall beings in a city park. The Soviet news agency TASS reported the story, which drew attention worldwide. 1
