Antônio Villas Boas abduction

Minas Gerais, Brazil, 1957

Minas Gerais, Brazil, 1957 · UAP or anomaly site · 19.861°S, 49.769°W

In October 1957, farmer Antônio Villas Boas said he was taken aboard a landed craft while plowing his fields at night near São Francisco de Sales. It is one of the earliest widely reported abduction accounts. 1
