Valensole landing

Provence, France, 1965

Provence, France, 1965 · UAP or anomaly site · 43.837°N, 5.984°E

Early on July 1, 1965, lavender farmer Maurice Masse said he found a craft on six legs in his field, described as egg-shaped or as a spherical vehicle, with two small beings beside it, one of which paralyzed him with a tube before the craft flew off. Gendarmes found a shallow depression with a hole at its center. Investigators judged Masse sincere, and the case has never been explained, though there was no other witness. 1,2
