Iraq "jellyfish" UAP video

Anbar Province, Iraq, 2018

Anbar Province, Iraq, 2018 · UAP or anomaly site · 33.330°N, 43.550°E

In January 2024, filmmaker Jeremy Corbell released a thermal video he said U.S. forces recorded over a joint operations base in Iraq in 2018. It shows a jellyfish-like object with hanging tendrils drifting over the base and switching between black and white. Corbell said it then went into a nearby lake for about 17 minutes and flew off at high speed, but the released clip does not show that part. A former Marine who served at the base said the object became the base's ghost story and that he never saw it enter the water. The Pentagon did not comment on the footage. A physics professor said it did not look like a camera artifact but that no conclusion was possible without more data, while users of the skeptic forum Metabunk suggested a cluster of balloons. 1,2,3
