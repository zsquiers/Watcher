Valentich disappearance

Bass Strait, Australia, 1978

Bass Strait, Australia, 1978 · UAP or anomaly site · 39.000°S, 143.600°E

On October 21, 1978, 20-year-old pilot Frederick Valentich radioed that a large craft was hovering over his plane, then contact was lost. Neither he nor the aircraft was ever found. 1
