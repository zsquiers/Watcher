Archives for the Unexplained

Norrköping, Sweden, founded 1973

Norrköping, Sweden, founded 1973 · Civilian research group · 58.588°N, 16.192°E

One of the world's largest libraries of UFO and paranormal material, with kilometers of shelving, founded in 1973 and run by volunteers including journalist Clas Svahn. Researchers can visit by appointment. 1
