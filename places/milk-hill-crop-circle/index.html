Milk Hill crop circle

Alton Barnes, Wiltshire, 2001

Alton Barnes, Wiltshire, 2001 · UAP or anomaly site · 51.375°N, 1.858°W

In August 2001 a spiral formation of about four hundred circles, reported as 1500ft across, appeared in a wheat field at Milk Hill, one of the largest crop circles on record. Farmers Weekly noted that the area "has been the scene of mysterious circles for years." Enthusiasts see such circles as proof of alien visits; sceptics say they are the work of pranksters. 1
