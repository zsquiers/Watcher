Gorman dogfight

Fargo, North Dakota, 1948

Fargo, North Dakota, 1948 · UAP or anomaly site · 46.920°N, 96.815°W

On October 1, 1948, National Guard pilot George Gorman chased a ball of light in his P-51 Mustang, reporting that it outmaneuvered and outclimbed him. Airport controllers and a light-plane pilot also saw the light. Project Sign learned that a lighted weather balloon had been released from Fargo shortly before, and concluded that Gorman chased the balloon and perhaps Jupiter, with his own maneuvers making it seem to dodge him. 1
