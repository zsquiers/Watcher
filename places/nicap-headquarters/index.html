NICAP headquarters

Washington, DC, 1956 to 1980

Washington, DC, 1956 to 1980 · Civilian research group · 38.910°N, 77.044°W

The National Investigations Committee on Aerial Phenomena, a civilian group founded in 1956 with T. Townsend Brown as its first director. Under Donald Keyhoe, who led it from 1957 to 1969, it pressed Congress for open UFO hearings. From about 1957 to 1962 its board of governors included Roscoe Hillenkoetter, the first director of the CIA and Keyhoe's Naval Academy classmate. It dissolved in 1980. 1,2
