Chilbolton "Arecibo reply"

Chilbolton, Hampshire, 2001

Chilbolton, Hampshire, 2001 · UAP or anomaly site · 51.145°N, 1.438°W

In 2001 a crop formation appeared in a field near the Chilbolton radio telescope that copied the 1974 Arecibo radio message, with changes: silicon in place of carbon and a figure with a large head and smaller body. Skeptics note that the Arecibo message has traveled only a tiny fraction of its way to its target, and that senders able to receive radio could simply reply by radio. 1
