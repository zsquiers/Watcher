Hawara, Faiyum, Egypt, about 1800 BC

Hawara, Faiyum, Egypt, about 1800 BC · Ancient site · 29.274°N, 30.899°E

The ruined pyramid of Pharaoh Amenemhat III, and beside it the site of the "Labyrinth" that Herodotus and Strabo described as a vast complex of some 3,000 rooms, half of them underground. Flinders Petrie excavated its foundations in 1888 and 1889. In 2008 the Mataha Expedition, a team from Egypt's NRIAG, Ghent University, and Louis De Cordier's foundation, ran a ground-penetrating radar survey that reported buried walls and structures. UNVERIFIED: in 2025, on The Joe Rogan Experience, researcher Ben van Kerkwyk and Joe Rogan discussed claims that the scans show a large metallic "Tic Tac"-shaped object, some 40 meters long, in a chamber deep underground. No scan data showing such an object has been published, and Egyptologists consider the claim unsupported. Further excavation has not been permitted. 1
