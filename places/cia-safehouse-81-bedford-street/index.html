CIA safehouse, 81 Bedford Street

81 Bedford St, New York, NY 10014

81 Bedford St, New York, NY 10014 · Secret project site · 40.732°N, 74.005°W

A Greenwich Village apartment the CIA used for MKUltra from about 1953 to 1955. Federal Bureau of Narcotics agent George Hunter White, working for the CIA, secretly dosed unwitting visitors with LSD here and observed the effects. He later ran a similar safehouse in San Francisco, known as Operation Midnight Climax. Confirmed program. 1
