Cosmo Isle Hakui

Hakui, Ishikawa, Japan

Hakui, Ishikawa, Japan · Civilian research group · 36.898°N, 136.788°E

A space and UFO museum that opened in July 1996 as the centerpiece of Hakui's effort to revive the town with UFOs. When city employee Jōsen Takano found a full-size replica rocket would cost ¥160 million, he went to NASA himself and borrowed items including an unused backup Viking lander and moon rocks, writing "100 years" as the loan period, and bought Soviet spacecraft from Russia. Its "Vostok" capsule is actually from a Zenit reconnaissance satellite of the same design, displayed with a mannequin inside. The UFO television producer Jun'ichi Yaoi is its honorary director, and it holds material on the Roswell incident. The town's UFO theme draws on the sōhachibon, a strange fire that a local temple document says flew across the mountains; town literature calls the document 1,200 years old, a date no independent study has confirmed. 1,2,3,4,5,6
