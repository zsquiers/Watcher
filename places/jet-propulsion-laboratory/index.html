Jet Propulsion Laboratory

Pasadena, California

Pasadena, California · Government building · 34.201°N, 118.171°W

A NASA research laboratory that grew out of the Caltech rocket group that Jack Parsons, Frank Malina and Edward Forman formed in 1934; their first rocket motor test, on Halloween 1936 in the Arroyo Seco canyon here, is marked by a monument. The group became JPL in 1943 and joined the newly created NASA in December 1958 after building Explorer 1, the first U.S. satellite. 1
