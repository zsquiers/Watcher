Ariel School sighting

Ruwa, Zimbabwe, 1994

Ruwa, Zimbabwe, 1994 · UAP or anomaly site · 17.878°S, 31.254°E

On September 16, 1994, more than 60 schoolchildren said a craft landed near their playground and small beings approached them. Harvard psychiatrist John Mack interviewed many of the children, and several have stood by their accounts as adults. 1
