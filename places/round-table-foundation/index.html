Glen Cove, Maine, 1948 to 1958

Glen Cove, Maine, 1948 to 1958 · Civilian research group · 44.125°N, 69.102°W

Andrija Puharich's private research laboratory, started in a donated barn in 1948 with wealthy benefactors, where he studied psychics, mediums and mind-altering substances until it was disbanded in 1958. A December 1952 session here produced the first messages from beings calling themselves the Nine. 1,2
