Yukon mothership sighting

Fox Lake, Yukon, Canada, 1996

Fox Lake, Yukon, Canada, 1996 · UAP or anomaly site · 61.250°N, 135.470°W

On December 11, 1996, at least 31 people along the Klondike Highway reported an enormous craft with rows of lights. A satellite analyst later matched it to a re-entering Soviet rocket stage. 1,2
