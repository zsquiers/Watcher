Dechmont Woods encounter

Livingston, Scotland, 1979

Livingston, Scotland, 1979 · UAP or anomaly site · 55.911°N, 3.549°W

At about 10 a.m. on November 9, 1979, forestry worker Robert Taylor said he came upon a dome-shaped object about 20 feet across hovering in a clearing in the woods on Dechmont Law. Two smaller spheres like sea mines seized him and dragged him toward it, and he smelled something like burning brakes before he passed out. He came to with his trousers torn and grazes on his chin and thighs. Police visited the site, found ladder-shaped marks in the ground and recorded the matter as an assault. A plaque was placed near the site in 1992. 1
