Big Lake Park, Council Bluffs, Iowa, 1971

Big Lake Park, Council Bluffs, Iowa, 1971 · UAP or anomaly site · 41.282°N, 95.862°W

On the evening of December 17, 1971, about 11 people in separate groups reported a hovering object with lights around its rim over Big Lake Park, and some saw a glowing red mass fall from it to the ground, where flames rose 8 to 10 feet. Firefighters found a patch of molten metal about 4 by 6 feet running down a levee, still too hot to touch, and it stayed warm for about two hours in freezing weather. Iowa State University's Ames Laboratory found it was mostly iron, much like cast iron or carbon steel, with slag, and ruled out a meteorite or space hardware. The pin marks the park approximately. 1
