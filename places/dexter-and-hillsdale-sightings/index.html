Dexter and Hillsdale sightings

Michigan, 1966

Michigan, 1966 · UAP or anomaly site · 42.338°N, 83.889°W

On March 20, 1966, farmer Frank Mannor and his son near Dexter said they saw a glowing, football-shaped object over a marsh. The next night about 87 students at Hillsdale College watched lights over the college arboretum for hours. The Air Force's J. Allen Hynek suggested marsh gas, an explanation that was widely ridiculed and led Congressman Gerald Ford to call for a congressional investigation. 1
