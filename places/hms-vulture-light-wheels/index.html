HMS Vulture light wheels

Persian Gulf, 1879

Persian Gulf, 1879 · UAP or anomaly site · 26.433°N, 53.183°E

At about 9:40 p.m. on May 15, 1879, Commander J. Eliot Pringle of HMS Vulture saw luminous waves racing through the calm water and passing under his ship near Stiffe's Bank. From the deck they looked like two great wheels with glowing spokes turning in opposite directions. From the mizzen top, 50 feet up, he saw that they were really parallel bands of light about 25 feet wide, moving at roughly 84 miles an hour, and that the turning was an effect of perspective. The light reached well under the surface and lit the bottoms of the ship's boats, and the display lasted about 35 minutes. The Navy's hydrographer had his report published in Nature. 1
