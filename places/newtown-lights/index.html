Newtown and New Milford, Connecticut, 1987

Newtown and New Milford, Connecticut, 1987 · UAP or anomaly site · 41.410°N, 73.310°W

On May 26, 1987, more than a hundred people reported a large, multicolored arc of lights passing over Interstate 84 near Newtown and later hovering over New Milford, and some drivers said their cars lost power. It came during the Hudson Valley wave of boomerang sightings, many of which state police traced to small planes flying in tight formation with lights. This case was never explained. 1,2
