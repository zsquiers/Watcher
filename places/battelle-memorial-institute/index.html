Battelle Memorial Institute

Columbus, Ohio

Columbus, Ohio · Civilian research group · 39.992°N, 83.016°W

A private nonprofit research institute founded in 1929. In the early 1950s, under the Air Force's Project Stork, its scientists analyzed more than 3,200 UFO reports for Project Blue Book Special Report No. 14. 1,2,3
