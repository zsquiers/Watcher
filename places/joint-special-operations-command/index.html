Joint Special Operations Command

Pope Field, Fort Bragg, North Carolina

Pope Field, Fort Bragg, North Carolina · Military base · 35.171°N, 79.014°W

The U.S. military's command for its most secret special-operations units, created in December 1980 after the failed Operation Eagle Claw hostage rescue in Iran, and part of U.S. Special Operations Command. Its commanders have included Stanley McChrystal, from 2003 to 2008, and William McRaven, from 2008 to 2011. In 2011 journalist Seymour Hersh claimed both men and others in the command belonged to the Knights of Malta; McChrystal denied it. 1,2,3
