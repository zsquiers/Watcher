Tesla's Colorado Springs lab

Colorado Springs, Colorado, 1899 to 1900

Colorado Springs, Colorado, 1899 to 1900 · Civilian research group · 38.825°N, 104.820°W

Where Nikola Tesla built a giant experimental station to study wireless power and lightning. In 1899 he recorded faint, regular signals on his receivers that he came to believe were messages from another planet, likely Mars or Venus. The lab was torn down in 1904. 1
