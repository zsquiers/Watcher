Craft over a Florence stadium shed angel hair onto the crowd

Claimed by Spectators and players, and Italian ufologists · 1954 · Press reports at the time and later interviews

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Thousands at the stadium said they saw objects overhead release glittering filaments, and a sample tested by Professor Giovanni Canneri was reported to contain boron, silicon, calcium and magnesium. 1,2

The official record

The filaments tended to disintegrate soon after landing, which makes them hard to study. Elsewhere, falls of angel hair have often turned out to be silk from ballooning spiders, though that has not been shown for Florence; the objects themselves have never been identified. 1,3
