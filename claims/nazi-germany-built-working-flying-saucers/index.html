Nazi Germany built working flying saucers

Claimed by Postwar writers, from Rudolf Schriever in 1950 to Jason Jorjani today · 1950 · Magazine interviews, books and podcasts since 1950

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Rudolf Schriever told Der Spiegel in 1950 that he had designed a flying disc for Nazi Germany. Later writers described Haunebu and Vril saucers and, from 2000, a bell-shaped device called Die Glocke, often tied to SS General Hans Kammler, who ran the V-2 rocket and late jet programs and vanished in 1945. On American Alchemy, Jason Jorjani says he found Hitler's secret UFO program. 1,2,3

The official record

No saucer, blueprint or wartime record has surfaced. Schriever's two accounts contradict each other, there is no evidence that one named designer, Klaus Habermohl, existed, and the Haunebu and Vril stories come from later esoteric writing, including that of Holocaust denier Ernst Zündel. Germany's real flying-wing jet, the Horten brothers' Ho 229, first flew under power in February 1945. Kammler's fate is uncertain: witnesses said he took cyanide in May 1945, while a U.S. intelligence order that November suggests he may have been in American custody. 1,2,4
