A Marine found a crashed craft in Peru and was threatened into silence

Claimed by Jonathan Weygandt · 2001 · Disclosure Project testimony

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Weygandt said that in 1997, while guarding a radar station in Peru, he helped secure a crash site and found an egg-shaped craft buried in a cliff, still humming and leaking a liquid that ate into his uniform. He said he was then held for about two days, interrogated for hours and told that if he talked he would "come up missing." 1

The official record

His own account is the only source for the crash and the interrogation. He named two sergeants who he said saw the craft with him, but he offered no documents or photos, the briefing gives no location for the site, and no record or other witness has publicly confirmed it. 1
