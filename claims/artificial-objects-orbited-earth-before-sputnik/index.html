Artificial objects orbited Earth before Sputnik

Claimed by Beatriz Villarroel · 2025 · Papers in PASP and Scientific Reports

Disputed · Officials and the claimant give conflicting accounts.

The claim

Beatriz Villarroel and colleagues say brief, star-like flashes on 1950s Palomar survey plates line up in rows, avoid Earth's shadow, and cluster around nuclear tests and UFO reports. They suggest flat, reflective objects in high orbit, years before Sputnik, the first known satellite, launched in 1957. 1,2

The official record

Astronomers Nigel Hambly and Adam Blair found the flashes are narrower and rounder than real starlight and concluded they are most likely flaws in the photographic emulsion, perhaps made when the survey plates were copied. Villarroel's team replies that a split-second flash should look sharper than a star. The original plates have not yet been examined to settle it. 3,4
