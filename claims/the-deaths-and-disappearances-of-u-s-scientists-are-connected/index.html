The deaths and disappearances of U.S. scientists are connected

Claimed by Social media posts, podcasts and some members of Congress · 2026 · Social media, podcasts and cable news

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Since General McCasland went missing in February 2026, posts and podcasts have linked him to about 15 other scientists, engineers and officials tied to aerospace, nuclear or space research who died or disappeared over roughly four years, suggesting they were silenced. Rep. Eric Burlison asked for FBI involvement, and House Oversight Committee chair James Comer said the cases were unlikely to be a coincidence. 1

The official record

In April 2026 the FBI said it was looking for connections and the White House said it was reviewing the cases, but no link has been shown. The FBI found that the man who killed an MIT plasma physicist in December 2025 acted alone, with no connection to terrorism, and other deaths on the lists were attributed to illness, suicide, a carjacking and a drowning. Skeptics such as Mick West note that with hundreds of thousands of people in cleared aerospace and nuclear jobs, many such deaths would be expected by chance; reviews by CBS, the Los Angeles Times and Snopes found no obvious links. 1,2
