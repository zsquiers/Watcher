The objects shot down in February 2023 were unexplained craft

Claimed by Commentators and members of the public · 2023 · News coverage of the shootdowns

Officially explained · An official investigation gave an ordinary explanation.

The claim

After U.S. jets shot down three objects over Alaska, Canada, and Lake Huron in February 2023, officials said little about what they were, and speculation about UAP spread. 1

The official record

Documents released in November 2024 showed debris from the Lake Huron object had been recovered and came from a company that sells weather monitoring equipment. Officials said the objects were most likely benign balloons. 1
