Mystery drones over New Jersey were a hidden threat

Claimed by Residents, officials, and commentators · 2024 · Reports and news coverage

Officially explained · An official investigation gave an ordinary explanation.

The claim

In late 2024, thousands of people in New Jersey and nearby states reported large drones at night, and some officials suggested they were unexplained or foreign. 1

The official record

Federal investigators found the reports mainly involved authorized drones, manned aircraft, and other misidentified objects, though military officials confirmed some unauthorized drones near bases. 1
