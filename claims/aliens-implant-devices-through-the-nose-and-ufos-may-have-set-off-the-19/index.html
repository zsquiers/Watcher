Aliens implant devices through the nose, and UFOs may have set off the 1965 blackout

Claimed by Bruce Rapuano · 2023 · Dominion Lost, a book printed through Amazon

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Rapuano describes nasal implant procedures he says he underwent from childhood, argues the abductors' instruments show precise knowledge of human anatomy, and speculates that the 1965 Northeast blackout was set off by the electromagnetic effects of UFO propulsion during mass sightings. 1

The official record

The account rests on his own memories, and the book offers arguments from medicine and physics rather than physical evidence from his case. The 1965 blackout has a documented cause: a misset protective relay on a line from the Sir Adam Beck power station near Niagara Falls tripped and set off a cascade across the grid. The book's main review, in the Journal of Scientific Exploration, was by Steve Aspin, himself an abduction author, who endorsed it. 1,2
