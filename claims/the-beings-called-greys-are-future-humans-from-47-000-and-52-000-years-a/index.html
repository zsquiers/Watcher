The beings called Greys are future humans from 47,000 and 52,000 years ahead

Claimed by Online theorists, building on a video of the late researcher Amy Eskridge · 2026 · A resurfaced video, podcasts and social media

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

In a video that resurfaced in May 2026, around the release of the Pentagon's UAP files, Amy Eskridge, an anti-gravity researcher and former president of the Institute for Exotic Science, said the beings she called ultraterrestrials are "us from the future": groups she labeled P-47 and P-52, from 47,000 and 52,000 years ahead, descended from the survivors of a near-future apocalypse, one that went underground and one that stayed on the surface. Podcasts and posts have since built this into a theory that Grey aliens are future humans who travel back in time to observe us or repair their own decline. 1,2

The official record

The video's date and context are unknown, and Eskridge offered no evidence for the claim. The released government files do not mention time-traveling humans, and no record supports the P-47 and P-52 labels. The fuller story of Greys as degraded future humans, with factions and time-travel missions, comes from later podcasts and social media rather than from anything she is documented to have said. 1,2
