Admiral Byrd flew into a hollow Earth beyond the poles

Claimed by Raymond Bernard's 1964 book The Hollow Earth, and a later "secret diary" · 1964 · Books and websites

Shown false · The evidence for it was shown to be fake or mistaken.

The claim

Raymond Bernard's 1964 book The Hollow Earth is subtitled The Greatest Geographical Discovery in History Made by Admiral Richard Byrd in the Mysterious Land Beyond the Poles—The True Origin of the Flying Saucers. A "secret diary" attributed to Byrd, which surfaced long after his death, describes him flying through an opening at the pole into a warm inner world with advanced beings. 1

The official record

Byrd never made such a claim, and the diary has no known original. His real 1925–1927 diary was published by Ohio State University Press in 1998. The idea of a hollow Earth is disproven: seismic waves travel through the planet in ways that show a solid inner core and a liquid outer core, and a hollow shell of Earth's size would collapse under its own gravity. 2,3
