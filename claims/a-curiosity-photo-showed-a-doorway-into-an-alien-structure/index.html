A Curiosity photo showed a doorway into an alien structure

Claimed by Social media posts · 2022 · Viral posts of a NASA rover image

Officially explained · An official investigation gave an ordinary explanation.

The claim

In May 2022 a picture from NASA's Curiosity rover of a neat rectangular opening in a rock face on Mount Sharp spread online as a door built into the cliff.

The official record

NASA identified it as a small natural fracture where cracks in the layered rock meet, much smaller than a human door, and the team called it a doorway into the ancient past only in the geological sense. 1,2
