A jellyfish-shaped UAP over a U.S. base in Iraq dived into a lake and flew out again

Claimed by Jeremy Corbell · 2024 · His release of the video on X in January 2024

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Corbell said U.S. forces filmed the object with a thermal camera over a joint operations base in Iraq in 2018. He said it went into a nearby lake for about 17 minutes, came back out and left at high speed. 1,2

The official record

The released clip does not show the object entering or leaving the water, and the Pentagon did not comment on the footage. A former Marine who served at the base said it never seemed to threaten anyone and that he never saw it drop into the water. Skeptics have suggested a cluster of balloons. 1,2,3
