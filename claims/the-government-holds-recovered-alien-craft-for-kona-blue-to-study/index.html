The government holds recovered alien craft for Kona Blue to study

Claimed by Officials connected to the AAWSAP program · 2011 · Proposal to the Department of Homeland Security

Officially denied · The government says it is not true.

The claim

Around 2011, officials connected to the AAWSAP program proposed a special access program at the Department of Homeland Security, called Kona Blue, to reverse-engineer extraterrestrial craft they believed the government possessed. 1

The official record

AARO's 2024 report disclosed the proposal and said DHS leaders rejected it as without merit. AARO found no evidence that any recovered craft existed for it to study. 1,2
