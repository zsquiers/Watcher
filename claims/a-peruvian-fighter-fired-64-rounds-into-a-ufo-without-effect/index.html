A Peruvian fighter fired 64 rounds into a UFO without effect

Claimed by Óscar Santa María Huertas · 1980 · The pilot's interviews, documentaries and a book chapter

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Santa María Huertas said he hit an object over La Joya with 64 rounds of cannon fire but it was unharmed, and that it then outmaneuvered and outclimbed his jet. 1

The official record

The detailed published account is his own account, given in later interviews and a book chapter; no gun-camera film, radar data or official Peruvian Air Force finding has been released to back it up. 1
