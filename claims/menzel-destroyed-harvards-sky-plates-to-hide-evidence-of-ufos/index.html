Menzel destroyed Harvard's sky plates to hide evidence of UFOs

Claimed by UFO writers online, drawing on Dorrit Hoffleit's memoir · 2024 · Online articles, including one calling the gap in Harvard's plates the "MJ-12 gap"

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Some UFO writers suggest that the photographic plates Donald Menzel had destroyed at Harvard may have recorded UFOs or other anomalies, and call the 1953 to 1968 hole in the observatory's plate record the MJ-12 gap. They point out that Menzel was both a leading UFO debunker and one of the members named in the Majestic 12 papers. 1,2

The official record

The destruction itself is documented: his colleague Dorrit Hoffleit recalled Menzel having his secretary destroy a third of the plates sight unseen, and an account of her life says he was discarding plates in the name of saving space. But no record says what the destroyed plates showed, and sight unseen means no one looked. The 15-year gap in new plates came from a cost-cutting halt to plate-making, and the Majestic 12 papers that name Menzel were judged fakes by the FBI. Interest grew after astronomer Beatriz Villarroel's team reported short-lived star-like flashes on early-1950s sky plates from Palomar Observatory, two of them during the July 1952 Washington, D.C., sightings; those were Palomar plates, not Harvard's. 2,3,4,5
