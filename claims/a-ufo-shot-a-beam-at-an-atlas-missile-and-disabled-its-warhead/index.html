A UFO shot a beam at an Atlas missile and disabled its warhead

Claimed by Robert Jacobs · 1982 · A 1982 National Enquirer article, a 1989 UFO journal piece and a 2021 press conference

Disputed · Officials and the claimant give conflicting accounts.

The claim

Jacobs says the team's film of a 1964 Atlas launch showed an "intelligently controlled flying device" that flew alongside the warhead and fired a "beam of energy" at it. He says a senior official asked him "What the hell was that?" and then ordered him never to discuss it. Major Florenz Mansmann, who was involved in the project, reportedly backed the account in a 1987 letter to a UFO researcher. 1

The official record

Kingston George, the project engineer, wrote in Skeptical Inquirer in 1993 and 2009 that the film showed the deployment of decoy warheads and chaff, a highly classified technology meant to fool Soviet missile defenses, and that Jacobs was not cleared to be told what the team had recorded. The film itself has never been released. 1
