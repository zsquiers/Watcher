A flying saucer crashed near Roswell in 1947

Claimed by Roswell Army Air Field press release; later researchers · 1947 · Military press release, revived in 1980s books

Officially explained · An official investigation gave an ordinary explanation.

The claim

In July 1947 the Army Air Field announced it had recovered a flying disc. From 1980 on, books and witnesses described a crashed craft and alien bodies. 1

The official record

The Air Force's 1994 report identified the debris as a Project Mogul balloon train, and a 1997 report attributed the bodies accounts to later crash-test dummies and misremembered events. A 1995 GAO search found some base records had been destroyed. 1,2,3
