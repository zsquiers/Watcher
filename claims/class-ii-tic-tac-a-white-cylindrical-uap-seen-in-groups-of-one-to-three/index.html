Class II Tic Tac: a white cylindrical UAP seen in groups of one to three

Claimed by Skywatcher · 2025 · Skywatcher's research pages and its April 2025 video Mapping the Unknown

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Skywatcher describes Class II as a bright white cylinder 9 to 12 meters long that hovers, stretches out in stable flight and shifts color from white to purple and pink. It says the craft drops from over 60,000 feet at more than Mach 1.5, stops abruptly, shows a strong infrared signature with no visible propulsion, and affects nearby electronics and aircraft, with helicopter crews reporting nausea. 1

The official record

Skywatcher calls this a preliminary classification and says its hypotheses are exploratory and should not be read as conclusive. The optical, infrared and radar data and the full report for the class are listed as coming soon and have not been published. The name echoes the 2004 Nimitz Tic Tac, but nothing public ties the two. 1
