Film shows the autopsy of an alien from the Roswell crash

Claimed by Producer Ray Santilli · 1995 · Footage released in 1995 and broadcast by Fox as Alien Autopsy: Fact or Fiction

Shown false · The evidence for it was shown to be fake or mistaken.

The claim

Santilli said the black-and-white footage was shot by a military cameraman and showed doctors dissecting a body recovered after the Roswell incident. 1

The official record

In 2006 Santilli and fellow producer Gary Shoefield admitted they had made the footage on a set in a London flat, using dummies built by sculptor John Humphreys and filled with sheep brains, chicken entrails and raspberry jam. Santilli still claimed it recreated genuine footage he had seen in 1992, but no such footage has ever been produced. 1
