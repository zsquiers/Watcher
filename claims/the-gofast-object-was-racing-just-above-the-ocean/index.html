The GoFast object was racing just above the ocean

Claimed by Popular commentary on the 2015 video · 2017 · Commentary on the released video

Officially explained · An official investigation gave an ordinary explanation.

The claim

Many viewers of the GoFast video took it to show a small object skimming the ocean surface at very high speed. 1

The official record

AARO's February 2025 analysis concluded the object was about 13,000 feet up, drifting with the wind, and that the impression of speed came from the jet's own motion (parallax). It could not identify the object itself. 2
