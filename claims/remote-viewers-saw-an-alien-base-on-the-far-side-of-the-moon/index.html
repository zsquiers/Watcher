Remote viewers saw an alien base on the far side of the Moon

Claimed by Ingo Swann · 1998 · Swann's book Penetration: The Question of Extraterrestrial and Human Telepathy

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

In his 1998 book Penetration, psychic Ingo Swann, a key subject of the U.S. government's remote viewing research, described remote viewing a secret alien base on the hidden side of the Moon for a mysterious official. He concluded that extraterrestrials were living on Earth in humanoid bodies. 1

The official record

The account rests entirely on Swann's own story; the official he described has never been identified, and no spacecraft image of the far side, including China's Chang'e 4 landing there in 2019, has shown any base. Remote viewing itself was judged unproven when the government ended the Stargate Project in 1995.
