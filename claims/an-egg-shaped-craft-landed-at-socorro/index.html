An egg-shaped craft landed at Socorro

Claimed by Police officer Lonnie Zamora · 1964 · Police report, investigated by Project Blue Book

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Socorro police officer Lonnie Zamora reported in April 1964 that he saw an egg-shaped craft on the ground and two small figures beside it before it took off with a roar and flame. 1

The official record

Project Blue Book investigated and listed the case as unknown, one of its best-known unexplained landings. Some researchers have suggested a student hoax, but no explanation has been confirmed. 1
