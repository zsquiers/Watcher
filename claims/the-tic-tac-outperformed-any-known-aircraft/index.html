The Tic Tac outperformed any known aircraft

Claimed by David Fravor · 2017 · Interviews, and sworn testimony in 2023

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Navy Cmdr. David Fravor says that in November 2004 he saw a white, oval object hovering over a disturbance in the ocean, which mirrored his jet's moves as he spiraled down and then shot away faster than anything he knew. 1

The official record

The Department of Defense confirmed in 2020 that the FLIR1 video from the encounter is authentic and that the object remains unidentified. No official analysis of its speed or performance has been made public. 2
