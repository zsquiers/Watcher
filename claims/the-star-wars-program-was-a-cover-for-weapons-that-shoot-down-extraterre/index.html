The Star Wars program was a cover for weapons that shoot down extraterrestrial craft

Claimed by Steven Greer · 2001 · The Disclosure Project briefing document

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Steven Greer wrote that the Star Wars effort "has really been a cover for black project deployment of weapon systems to track, target and destroy ETVs," and that three unnamed sources had told him at least two extraterrestrial craft had been destroyed by experimental space-based weapons since the early 1990s. Robert Jacobs, who filmed the 1964 Big Sur missile test, speculated in the same document that what he saw may have been "the first shot across the bow." 1

The official record

Greer names none of his sources, and no record supports the claim. The public record of SDI is of weapons that largely failed or never left the laboratory: the X-ray laser failed key tests in 1986, and the American Physical Society found the technologies decades away from readiness. Another witness in the same briefing document called Star Wars "a big boondoggle," and Jacobs said his own view was speculation. 1,2
