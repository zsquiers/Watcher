Air Force units told new officers they were joining a secret alien-craft program

Claimed by The Wall Street Journal, citing AARO · 2025 · The Pentagon Disinformation That Fueled America's UFO Mythology, June 2025

Partly confirmed · Some of it is on the record; the rest is not.

The claim

The Journal reported that AARO found incoming personnel in some classified Air Force units were shown UFO photos, told they would help reverse-engineer alien technology, and sworn to secrecy, sometimes with threats. Many believed it. The practice most likely began as cover for real secret aircraft and continued until a Defense Secretary memo ended it in 2023. 1

The official record

The Defense Department acknowledged the pranks described by AARO and said its 2024 historical report had left them out because the investigation was not finished, promising a second volume. The name Yankee Blue and many details come from the Journal's reporting and have not been published in an official report. 1
