A saucer crashed at Aztec with 16 alien bodies

Claimed by Frank Scully · 1950 · Book, Behind the Flying Saucers

Shown false · The evidence for it was shown to be fake or mistaken.

The claim

Frank Scully's 1950 bestseller said a flying saucer about 100 feet across landed near Aztec, New Mexico, in 1948 and that the military recovered it with the bodies of 16 small crew members. 1

The official record

Journalist J. P. Cahn showed in 1952 that Scully's sources were confidence men, and the story collapsed. No record or physical evidence of the crash has ever surfaced. 1
