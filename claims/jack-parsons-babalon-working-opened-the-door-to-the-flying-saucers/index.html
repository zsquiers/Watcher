Jack Parsons' Babalon Working opened the door to the flying saucers

Claimed by Occultist Kenneth Grant; revived by Nick Redfern · 2010 · Grant's writings on Thelema, and Redfern's 2010 book Final Events

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

British occultist Kenneth Grant suggested that the Babalon Working, the rituals Jack Parsons and L. Ron Hubbard performed in early 1946 to bring the goddess Babalon into human form, opened a door through which the flying saucers began to appear, leading to the Kenneth Arnold sighting and Roswell in 1947. Parsons' wife Marjorie Cameron reported seeing an unusual object in the sky in 1946, and she later suggested the 1952 Washington flap was a spiritual reaction to Parsons' death. Nick Redfern's 2010 book Final Events gave the idea new life, describing a secret government group said to believe UFOs are demonic. 1,2

The official record

The claim cannot be tested: it rests on the belief that a ritual can open a gateway, and on timing, since the 1947 wave followed the rituals by about a year. Cameron's 1946 sighting has no date, place or other witnesses on record, and Parsons logged it as a materialization of Babalon rather than as an aircraft. The Arnold sighting, Roswell and the 1952 Washington radar returns have their own investigations and explanations, none of which involve Parsons, and Redfern's government group rests on anonymous sources. 1
