NASA ran a psychic training program for gifted children, the "space kids"

Claimed by Gordon Cooper · c. 2000 · A Coast to Coast radio interview, as reported by Andrew Basiago

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Andrew Basiago wrote that in a July 19 interview with host Mike Siegel on Coast to Coast, Cooper was asked who the space kids were and answered that they were "children with exceptional mental abilities run through a kind of MK program." Basiago added that Cooper described the program as cultivating telepathy, remote viewing and out-of-body experiences. 1

The official record

Basiago's article is the only known source. It gives no year, no recording or transcript has surfaced, and the details about telepathy and remote viewing are Basiago's paraphrase, not a quote. Standard accounts of Cooper's life do not mention it. Basiago says he was himself a child in a secret government program, so the report comes from someone with a stake in the story.
