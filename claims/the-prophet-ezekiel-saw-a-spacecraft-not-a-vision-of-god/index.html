The prophet Ezekiel saw a spacecraft, not a vision of God

Claimed by NASA engineer Josef F. Blumrich · 1973 · The Spaceships of Ezekiel, first published in German in 1973 and in English in 1974

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Blumrich, then chief of a systems layout branch at NASA's Marshall Space Flight Center, set out to disprove Erich von Däniken's reading of Ezekiel's vision but said a close study convinced him the prophet had seen a shuttlecraft from another planet. He drew detailed plans of the craft and gave technical specifications in the book. 1

The official record

The book is considered pseudoscience. Ronald Story wrote that Blumrich "doctors up his Biblical quotes" to fit a spaceship, and Jerome Clark called it a "creative but misplaced effort" to turn a metaphorical account into engineering. The passage is traditionally read as a prophetic vision of God. 1
