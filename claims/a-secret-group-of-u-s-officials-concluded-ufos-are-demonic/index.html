A secret group of U.S. officials concluded UFOs are demonic

Claimed by Nick Redfern · 2010 · The book Final Events

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

In Final Events, Nick Redfern relays accounts from anonymous sources of a cabal he calls the Collins Elite, formed out of concern about the occult rituals of rocket pioneer Jack Parsons. It is said to have concluded that UFOs and abductions are the work of demons rather than extraterrestrials. In the book's framing story, two Defense Department physicists told ufologist Ray Boeche in 1991 that the department was trying to contact nonhuman entities they believed were demonic. Luis Elizondo has since said a faction of government insiders opposed UFO research on religious grounds. 1,2

The official record

The story rests entirely on anonymous and pseudonymous informants, including a man calling himself "Richard Duke," and Redfern himself says he cannot vouch for it; he chose the name Collins Elite. No record of such a group has surfaced, and Wikipedia classes the book as pseudohistory. Elizondo's version of the claim first appeared in Final Events, and skeptic Jason Colavito notes it paints the Pentagon as full of officials who see themselves as spiritual warriors against a demon invasion. 1,2
