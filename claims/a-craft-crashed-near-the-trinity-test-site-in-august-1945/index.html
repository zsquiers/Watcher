A craft crashed near the Trinity test site in August 1945

Claimed by Reme Baca and Jose Padilla, publicized by Paola Harris and Jacques Vallée · 2003 · A 2003 newspaper story, radio, a 2011 memoir and the 2021 book Trinity

Shown false · The evidence for it was shown to be fake or mistaken.

The claim

Baca and Padilla say that on August 16, 1945, when they were 7 and 9, they were looking for a lost cow on the Padilla family ranch near the Trinity site when they saw a flash and found an avocado-shaped metal craft with small, insect-like creatures moving in the wreckage. They say state police officer Eddie Apodaca came to look, soldiers hauled the wreck away over several days, and they kept a piece of it. 1,2

The official record

The story first appeared in 2003, nearly 60 years later, and rests on the two men alone. Researcher Douglas Dean Johnson found that Apodaca was fighting in Europe at the time and did not join the state police until 1951, and that Baca had earlier pitched other versions to UFO authors, dated 1947 and 1946, with a disk-shaped craft and a pickup truck instead of horses. Skeptic Brian Dunning concludes the story was made up. 1,2
