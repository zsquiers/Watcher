U.S. forces fired on the 2024 drones over English bases with no effect

Claimed by Roy Teague · 2026 · Somewhere in the Skies podcast, citing an unnamed police source

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Roy Teague says a well-placed police source who dealt with the RAF told him that American personnel repeatedly shot at the drones over U.S.-used bases in England with rifles, shotguns and other small arms, and tried an EMP weapon on one of the larger ones, all with no effect, and were told off by the British for firing on British soil. He says officers were briefed that the objects were probably Russian or Chinese devices for harvesting phone data. 1

The official record

The U.S. Air Force confirmed the drone flights and said the craft ranged in sizes and configurations, but did not say who flew them and declined to discuss its countermeasures. It said there was no impact on residents, facilities or assets, and the Ministry of Defence said only that it has counter drone capabilities. No official account mentions gunfire or an EMP weapon, and Teague's source is unnamed. 2,3
