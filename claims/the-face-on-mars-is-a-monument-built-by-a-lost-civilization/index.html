The Face on Mars is a monument built by a lost civilization

Claimed by Richard C. Hoagland and others · 1976 · Books, talks and websites since the Viking images

Officially explained · An official investigation gave an ordinary explanation.

The claim

Promoters such as Richard C. Hoagland argue that the face-like mesa in Cydonia, with nearby hills they see as pyramids, is an artificial monument left by a lost civilization on Mars. 1

The official record

NASA's caption on the original 1976 picture already said the likeness came from shadows and that planetary geologists attribute the formation to natural processes; Viking's chief scientist called it a trick of light and shadow. Far sharper images from Mars Global Surveyor in 2001, and later from Mars Express and the Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter, show an eroded hill, and scientists regard the face as an optical illusion. 1,2
