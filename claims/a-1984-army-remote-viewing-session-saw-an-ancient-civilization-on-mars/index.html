A 1984 Army remote viewing session saw an ancient civilization on Mars

Claimed by Joseph McMoneagle · 1984 · A remote viewing session transcript, released in 2000

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

In a session targeting Mars about a million years ago, the viewer described huge pyramids, an obelisk and carved channels, and very tall, thin people hibernating in the structures as their world was devastated by storms. The released CIA transcript is widely shared as proof that ancient Martians existed. 1,2,3

The official record

The document is real, but it records only what the viewer said; there is no way to check the description against the target, and McMoneagle himself says there is no real way to validate it. No orbiter, lander or rover has found any sign of buildings or past life on Mars. The 1995 review commissioned by the CIA found that remote viewing had never been used to guide intelligence operations. 3,4,5
