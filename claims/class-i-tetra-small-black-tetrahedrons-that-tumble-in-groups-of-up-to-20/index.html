Class I Tetra: small black tetrahedrons that tumble in groups of up to 20 or more

Claimed by Skywatcher · 2025 · Skywatcher's research pages and its April 2025 video Mapping the Unknown

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Skywatcher describes Class I as black tetrahedrons 1.5 to 2 meters across, flying in coordinated groups of 3 to more than 20. It says they tumble rapidly on several axes while holding a steady course, give off bursts of white vapor, strobe several times a second on cameras, show a strong infrared signature, come down below about 2,000 feet, and jink when hit with a laser rangefinder, which gets no reflection back. 1,2

The official record

Skywatcher calls this a preliminary classification and says its hypotheses are exploratory, not conclusive. Its optical, infrared, radar and radio data and the full report for the class are listed as coming soon and have not been published. 1
