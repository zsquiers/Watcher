Military helicopters escorted an object that injured three people

Claimed by Betty Cash, Vickie Landrum, and Colby Landrum · 1980 · Witness accounts and a federal lawsuit

Disputed · Officials and the claimant give conflicting accounts.

The claim

Three people near Huffman, Texas, said a fiery, diamond-shaped object escorted by about two dozen helicopters, some of them tandem-rotor Chinooks, made them sick with radiation-like injuries in December 1980. 1

The official record

An Army Inspector General investigation found no evidence linking the helicopters to any branch of the military, and a federal judge dismissed their lawsuit against the government in 1986. 1
