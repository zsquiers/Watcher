Aliens are breeding hybrids to infiltrate and take over human society

Claimed by David M. Jacobs · 1998 · Books drawing on hypnosis sessions with abductees

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Jacobs concluded from hundreds of interviews, many under hypnosis, that a systematic abduction program is producing alien-human hybrids, with people taught to help them blend in, and that the program's goal may be taking over the Earth. 1

The official record

The evidence is testimony recovered largely through hypnosis by an interviewer with no training in psychology or hypnotherapy, as critics such as Martin Gardner pointed out; psychologists including Susan Clancy argue hypnosis can create detailed false memories and that sleep paralysis explains many experiences. No physical evidence of hybrids has been produced, and fellow researchers such as John Mack called his conclusions unsupportably dire. 1
