Something unknown in the sky killed the Dyatlov hikers

Claimed by Various authors and documentaries · 1959 · Books, websites and documentaries

Officially explained · An official investigation gave an ordinary explanation.

The claim

Because the hikers fled their tent half-dressed, some had severe injuries, and others nearby reported orange spheres in the sky, theorists have blamed secret weapons tests or UFOs. 1

The official record

A Russian inquiry reopened in 2019 concluded in 2020 that a snow slab or avalanche most likely forced the group out, and a 2021 study by Swiss scientists showed how a small slab could cause such injuries. Some relatives and researchers still dispute that conclusion, but no evidence points to a UFO. 1
