The Fátima apparitions were close encounters with alien beings

Claimed by Historians Joaquim Fernandes and Fina d'Armada · 1995 · A trilogy of books, first published in Portugal in 1995 and in English by Anomalist Books

Disputed · Officials and the claimant give conflicting accounts.

The claim

Fernandes and d'Armada say they were given access in 1978 to the original records of the case kept at the Sanctuary of Fátima since 1917. Their books argue that the events "did not involve 'Marian apparitions'" but a series of close encounters with alien beings, and that a 1941 "cover story" hid its significance as the first major close encounter case of the 20th century. 1

The official record

The Catholic Church declared the miracle worthy of belief in 1930 and treats it as a Marian apparition. Skeptics such as Joe Nickell attribute the "dancing sun" to optical effects and weather, such as the sun seen through thin clouds, and note that similar sun miracles have been reported where crowds of pilgrims stared at the sun. Not all witnesses saw the sun move. 2
