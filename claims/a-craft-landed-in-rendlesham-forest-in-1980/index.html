A craft landed in Rendlesham Forest in 1980

Claimed by Charles Halt · 1980 · Official memo to the UK Ministry of Defence

Disputed · Officials and the claimant give conflicting accounts.

The claim

Deputy base commander Lt. Col. Charles Halt reported in an official memo that airmen saw a glowing, metallic-looking object with colored lights in the forest outside RAF Woodbridge, and he later investigated the site himself. 1

The official record

The UK Ministry of Defence said the event posed no threat to national security and never investigated it as a security matter. Skeptics attribute the lights to a fireball, the Orfordness lighthouse, and bright stars; Halt and other witnesses stand by their accounts. 1
