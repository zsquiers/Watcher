Unknown aircraft were flying over Scandinavia in the 1930s

Claimed by Witnesses and Swedish Air Force officers · 1934 · Military reports and press coverage

Partly confirmed · Some of it is on the record; the rest is not.

The claim

Hundreds of people reported mysterious phantom aircraft over the far north, and in 1934 Swedish General Pontus Reuterswärd said that it could not be denied that the country's airspace was being violated. 1

The official record

An early statement from the investigation was that there have never been any Ghost Flyers, and Reuterswärd was criticized for his. The Swedish military's final report in July 1935 judged that 42 of the 487 reports were of real aircraft crossing the borders, which may have been smugglers or foreign flights, and very few of the sightings resemble modern UFO reports. 2
