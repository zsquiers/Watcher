Majestic 12, a secret UFO committee set up by Truman

Claimed by William L. Moore · 1984 · Documents received on film

Shown false · The evidence for it was shown to be fake or mistaken.

The claim

Documents that surfaced in the 1980s describe a secret group of officials, created by President Truman in 1947, to manage crashed-saucer recoveries. 1

The official record

The FBI concluded the documents were bogus, and the National Archives found no record of the group. The researcher who circulated them later admitted working with Air Force agents who were spreading disinformation. 1,2,3
