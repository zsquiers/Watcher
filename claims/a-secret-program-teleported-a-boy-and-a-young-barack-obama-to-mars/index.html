A secret program teleported a boy, and a young Barack Obama, to Mars

Claimed by Andrew Basiago · 2012 · Talks and interviews, widely reported by 2012

Officially denied · The government says it is not true.

The claim

Basiago says a DARPA program called Project Pegasus teleported him to Mars in the early 1980s, along with other young people including Barack Obama. 1,2

The official record

No documents or witnesses support the story, and a White House spokesman denied that Obama had ever been to Mars. 1
