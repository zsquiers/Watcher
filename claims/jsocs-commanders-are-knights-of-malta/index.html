JSOC's commanders are Knights of Malta

Claimed by Journalist Seymour Hersh · 2011 · A talk at Georgetown University's campus in Doha, Qatar

Officially denied · The government says it is not true.

The claim

In a January 2011 talk, Seymour Hersh said that former Joint Special Operations Command head Stanley McChrystal, his successor William McRaven and others in the command were members of the Knights of Malta and Opus Dei, and that many of them saw their work as "a crusade, literally." 1,2

The official record

McChrystal's spokesman called the claims "completely false and without basis in fact," saying McChrystal is not and never has been a member, and Opus Dei said he isn't one of its members either. Hersh offered no evidence, and he and The New Yorker declined to comment further. Foreign Policy's explainer found little to suggest the order is the kind of secretive cabal Hersh described. 1,2,3
