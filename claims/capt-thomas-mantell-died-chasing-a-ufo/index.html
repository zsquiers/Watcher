Capt. Thomas Mantell died chasing a UFO

Claimed by Early press and UFO writers · 1948 · News reports

Officially explained · An official investigation gave an ordinary explanation.

The claim

In January 1948, Kentucky Air National Guard pilot Thomas Mantell crashed and died while chasing a large, bright object, and the case was widely reported as a UFO death. 1

The official record

Investigators later concluded he was most likely chasing a Skyhook balloon, then a top-secret project he would not have known about, launched from Clinton County Air Force Base. 1
