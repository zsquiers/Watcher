A Chilean Navy helicopter filmed an object that ejected a mysterious trail

Claimed by Chile's CEFAA committee · 2017 · CEFAA release, via a HuffPost article by Leslie Kean

Contested · An official finding that critics reject.

The claim

After two years of study, the great majority of CEFAA's members agreed to call the object unidentified, after ruling out every explanation they had considered. 1

The official record

Within five days of the release, analysts on the Metabunk forum matched the object's path and timing to Iberia flight IB6830 taking off from Santiago, and the dark trail to an aerodynamic contrail from its wings, which looks dark in infrared. The data that pointed to the airliner had been in CEFAA's own files. 1,2
