A craft landed on the railway at Quarouble and its crew paralyzed a witness

Claimed by Marius Dewilde · 1954 · Statements to police and the press

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Dewilde said two small helmeted beings walked toward a dark craft on the railway line and that a beam of light from it paralyzed him before it flew off. 1,2

The official record

Investigators reported marks on the sleepers, and railway engineers are said to have estimated that they showed the pressure of a 30-ton object, but a passing train was blamed for at least one depression at the site. Dewilde was the only witness. His second reported encounter weeks later is generally regarded by ufologists as a hoax, which has weighed on his first. 1,2
