A craft crashed in Italy in 1933 and the United States later took it

Claimed by David Grusch · 2023 · Interviews and congressional testimony

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Roberto Pinotti says documents sent to him anonymously show that a craft crashed near Magenta in 1933 and was studied in secret by Mussolini's Gabinetto RS/33. In 2023 David Grusch said he had been briefed that the craft was recovered in Italy, and that Pope Pius XII passed word of it to the United States, which took it after the Allied advance into northern Italy in 1944 or 1945. 1,2,3

The official record

The documents trace back to a single anonymous source, lack the protocol numbers and stamps of real government papers, and match nothing in any Italian archive; researchers at Italy's skeptics' group CICAP and the ufology center CISU consider them probably forged. Pinotti says tests dated the paper and ink to the 1930s, but the full lab reports have never been released. In June 2024 a Vatican archive official said he didn't know where Grusch got his information, and that the archives hold no such document. 1,2,3
