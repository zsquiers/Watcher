An object removed from Patient Seventeen's leg is extraterrestrial

Claimed by Steve Colbern · 2017 · Lab analyses presented in the documentary Patient Seventeen

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Analyses of the last object Roger Leir removed, presented in the 2017 documentary, were said to show an elemental makeup with hallmarks of an extraterrestrial object. 1

The official record

Vice reported that even so, this is far from conclusive evidence, and that two outside experts it consulted were puzzled by the results but would not go on camera; no peer-reviewed study has been published. An earlier set of Leir's objects, tested at New Mexico Tech, proved to be made of commonly found elements, and skeptic Joe Nickell judged the implants most likely ordinary fragments such as glass or metal. 1,2
