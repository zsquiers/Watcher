Betty and Barney Hill were abducted in 1961

Claimed by Betty and Barney Hill · 1961 · Accounts recovered under hypnosis, published in 1966

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

The Hills said that while driving through New Hampshire's White Mountains in 1961 they were taken aboard a craft and examined, details they recalled under hypnosis two years later. 1

The official record

No official investigation confirmed an abduction. Their psychiatrist, Benjamin Simon, concluded the case was a psychological aberration rather than a real event, and the star map Betty drew has been widely disputed. 1
