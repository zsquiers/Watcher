An Air Force jet vanished after merging with a UFO over Lake Superior

Claimed by Ufologists, from Donald Keyhoe on · 1955 · Books and radio interviews since the 1950s

Disputed · Officials and the claimant give conflicting accounts.

The claim

Radar controllers saw the F-89's return close on an unknown target until the two blips seemed to merge, and the single blip went on along its course without the jet ever reappearing; ufologists have long argued the jet was taken or destroyed by the object it was chasing. 1

The official record

The Air Force said the target was a Royal Canadian Air Force transport flying off its planned course, but the Canadian pilot later denied any interception, and the Air Force's suggestion that Moncla suffered vertigo does not appear in its own accident board findings. No wreckage was confirmed; a 2006 claim that divers had found the jet was exposed as a fraud. The disappearance remains unexplained, but nothing ties it to an unknown craft. 1
