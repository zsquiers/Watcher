Bob Lazar reverse-engineered alien craft at S-4

Claimed by Bob Lazar · 1989 · Television interviews with George Knapp, KLAS-TV

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Bob Lazar said that in 1988 and 1989 he worked at a hidden site called S-4, near Area 51, helping reverse-engineer one of nine flying saucers of extraterrestrial origin, whose propulsion ran on a stable isotope of element 115. 1

The official record

The government has never commented on his claims, and no record of his work at S-4 has been released. Inquiries found he had worked at Los Alamos as a technician for a contractor, not as a physicist, and no record shows the MIT and Caltech master's degrees he claims. Element 115, first made in 2003, has proven extremely unstable, with no isotope lasting more than about a second. 1,2
