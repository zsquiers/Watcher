The Tunguska blast was an alien spaceship exploding

Claimed by Alexander Kazantsev, and later UFO writers · 1946 · The short story Explosion, and later books

Officially explained · An official investigation gave an ordinary explanation.

The claim

Soviet science fiction writer Alexander Kazantsev imagined the 1908 explosion as the nuclear blast of a spaceship from Mars, and the idea was later presented as fact. 1

The official record

Kazantsev wrote it as fiction, inspired by the bombing of Hiroshima. Scientists attribute the event to a meteor air burst, the explosion of a space rock in the atmosphere, which explains the flattened forest and the lack of a crater. 1
