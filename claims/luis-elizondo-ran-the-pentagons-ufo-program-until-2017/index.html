Luis Elizondo ran the Pentagon's UFO program until 2017

Claimed by Luis Elizondo · 2017 · Interviews, books, and testimony

Disputed · Officials and the claimant give conflicting accounts.

The claim

Luis Elizondo says he directed the Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program until he resigned in protest in 2017. 1

The official record

The Pentagon has given conflicting statements: officials said funding ended in 2012 and at times that he had no assigned AATIP duties, while other accounts and records support his involvement. 1,2
