The government set out to debunk UFOs

Claimed by UFO researchers · 1953 · Declassified panel report

Partly confirmed · Some of it is on the record; the rest is not.

The claim

UFO researchers have long argued that the government deliberately worked to make UFOs look silly so the public would stop reporting them.

The official record

The CIA-convened Robertson Panel in 1953 recommended a public education effort with a debunking aim to reduce public interest in flying saucers. How far that recommendation was carried out is debated. 1
