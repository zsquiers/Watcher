The United States is in contact with a "Galactic Federation" of aliens

Claimed by Haim Eshed · 2020 · An interview with Yedioth Ahronoth and the book The Universe Beyond the Horizon

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Eshed told Yedioth Ahronoth that "the Unidentified Flying Objects have asked not to publish that they are here, humanity is not ready yet." He said the U.S. government had signed an agreement letting the aliens do experiments on Earth, that American astronauts and alien representatives share an "underground base in the depths of Mars," and that President Trump knew and had been "on the verge of revealing" it before being asked to hold back to prevent "mass hysteria." 1,2

The official record

He offered no evidence, and no government confirmed any of it. The Pentagon declined to comment, and NASA said it had "yet to find signs of extraterrestrial life." Isaac Ben-Israel, chairman of the Israel Space Agency, said Eshed had gone "too far" and had long favored unconventional readings of space signals. The interviewer, Raanan Shaked, found him sincere but said the alien-treaty story echoed decades-old UFO lore rather than anything Eshed had seen himself. The remarks came as his book was published, and ufologist Nick Pope suggested a publicity stunt or something "lost in translation." 1,2,3
