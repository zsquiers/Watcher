There is no evidence of extraterrestrial technology

Claimed by AARO · 2024 · Official report to Congress

Contested · An official finding that critics reject.

The claim

AARO's historical report concluded that no U.S. government investigation had found evidence of extraterrestrial technology, and that many sightings were misidentified ordinary objects. 1

The official record

Witnesses at House hearings and members of Congress have called the report incomplete, saying AARO did not get access to the programs whistleblowers described. AARO stands by its findings. 2
