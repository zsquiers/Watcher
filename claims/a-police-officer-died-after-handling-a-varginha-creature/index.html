A police officer died after handling a Varginha creature

Claimed by Brazilian ufologists, including Vitório Pacaccini and Edison Boaventura Jr. · 1996 · Books and articles on the Varginha case

Officially denied · The government says it is not true.

The claim

Ufologists say military police soldier Marco Eli Chereze took part in a night operation near the Jardim Andere neighborhood on January 20, 1996, to capture one of the creatures, and touched it without protective equipment. He died about four weeks later. They point to a very low white blood cell count, suggest an unknown biological toxin, and note that he was buried quickly in a sealed coffin with no public wake. 1

The official record

His autopsy put the cause of death down to septicemia, a common bacterial infection, and the military police inquiry, IPM 18/97, found acute respiratory failure, septicemia and bacterial shock and stated there was no link whatsoever between his death and the alleged operation. No record places him at a capture. Pacaccini himself does not claim a direct link outright, and Boaventura says he does not deny the medical report but wants to question the context. 1
