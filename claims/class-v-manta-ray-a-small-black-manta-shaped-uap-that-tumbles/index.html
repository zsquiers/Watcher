Class V Manta Ray: a small black manta-shaped UAP that tumbles

Claimed by Skywatcher · 2025 · Skywatcher's research pages and its April 2025 video Mapping the Unknown

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Skywatcher describes Class V as a black, manta-ray-shaped object 2 to 3 meters across that tumbles on several axes, gives off pink, green, teal or white light or vapor, and moves both with and against the wind. It says the class is rarely seen, appears near Class II Tic Tacs, and produced confirmed radar tracks in some sightings. 1

The official record

Skywatcher calls this a preliminary classification. Its optical, infrared and radar data for the class are listed as coming soon and have not been published, so the radar tracks and other details cannot be checked. 1
