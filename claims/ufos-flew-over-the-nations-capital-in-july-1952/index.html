UFOs flew over the nation's capital in July 1952

Claimed by Air traffic controllers and pilots · 1952 · Radar reports and pilot sightings

Officially explained · An official investigation gave an ordinary explanation.

The claim

On two July weekends in 1952, radar operators at Washington's airports tracked unknown targets, and pilots reported lights, including near restricted airspace over the White House and Capitol. 1

The official record

At the Pentagon's largest press conference since World War II, the Air Force attributed the radar returns to temperature inversions. Some radar operators and meteorologists disputed that the inversions were strong enough. 1
