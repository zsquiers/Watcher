Billy Meier photographed and filmed Pleiadian spacecraft

Claimed by Billy Meier · 1975 · Photos, films and the FIGU group

Shown false · The evidence for it was shown to be fake or mistaken.

The claim

Meier says the Plejaren let him photograph and film their beamships so he could prove their visits. He has also offered metal samples, sound recordings, and photos of extraterrestrial women and of scenes from prehistoric Earth. 1

The official record

In 1997 his ex-wife, Kalliope, said the craft were models he made from household items such as trash can lids and carpet tacks, and that photos of the alien women Asket and Nera were really of Michelle DellaFave and Susan Lund of the Golddiggers dance troupe; the women were later confirmed to be Golddiggers performers from The Dean Martin Show. In interviews with author Gary Kinder, Meier admitted using models to recreate scenes after his wife showed photos of half-built models. Scientists, skeptics and most ufologists consider his photos hoaxes. 1
