Aliens and humans share an underground base at Dulce

Claimed by Paul Bennewitz · 1980 · Letters, reports, and later retellings

Shown false · The evidence for it was shown to be fake or mistaken.

The claim

Paul Bennewitz came to believe there was an underground alien base inside Archuleta Mesa near Dulce, New Mexico, and the story grew through later books and lectures. 1,2

The official record

The story grew out of disinformation fed to Bennewitz by Air Force agents, and no evidence of a base has ever been found. 1,3
