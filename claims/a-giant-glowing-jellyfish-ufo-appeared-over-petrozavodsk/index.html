A giant glowing jellyfish UFO appeared over Petrozavodsk

Claimed by Witnesses across northwestern Russia · 1977 · Soviet press reports

Officially explained · An official investigation gave an ordinary explanation.

The claim

In September 1977 a huge glowing object shaped like a jellyfish was seen over Petrozavodsk and far beyond, and it prompted a Soviet state UFO research program. 1

The official record

It is usually attributed to the secret launch of a Soviet Kosmos satellite from the Plesetsk Cosmodrome that night. 1
