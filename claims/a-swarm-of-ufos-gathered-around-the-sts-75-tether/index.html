A swarm of UFOs gathered around the STS-75 tether

Claimed by UFO documentaries and online videos · 1997 · Space shuttle video, recorded from NASA broadcasts and shown in UFO documentaries

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

After the Tethered Satellite System's cable broke on February 25, 1996, Columbia's cameras followed the drifting, sunlit tether. In the footage dozens of glowing discs, many with a notch cut from one side, drift and change direction around it. UFO documentaries presented them as large craft drawn to the tether. 1,2

The official record

The tether's rupture itself scattered many pieces of debris, which floated near the shuttle. Former NASA engineer James Oberg noted that the famous swarm footage was shot three days after the break, a few hours after a routine water dump that creates clouds of ice particles, with the camera focused at infinity, so small particles within a few meters showed as large out-of-focus circles. The notch's position follows each circle's place in the frame, the mark of a camera artifact, and the tether looks thick only because it was brightly sunlit. Objects as large as claimed would have been seen from the ground. 1,2
