The U.S. government ran a psychic spying program

Claimed by Former participants and journalists · 1995 · Declassification and interviews

Confirmed · The core claim is backed by official records.

The claim

For decades participants said the Army, DIA, and CIA funded research into remote viewing, using psychics to gather intelligence. 1

The official record

The program, last called Stargate, was confirmed when it was declassified in 1995. A CIA-sponsored review concluded it never produced useful intelligence. 1
