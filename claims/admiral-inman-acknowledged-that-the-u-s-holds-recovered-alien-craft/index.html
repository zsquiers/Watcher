Admiral Inman acknowledged that the U.S. holds recovered alien craft

Claimed by UFO researcher Bob Oechsler · 1992 · A recorded phone call, published as a transcript in the MUFON UFO Journal in 1992

Disputed · Officials and the claimant give conflicting accounts.

The claim

Researcher Bob Oechsler, a former NASA contractor, approached Inman after a May 1988 speech and in July 1989 reached him by phone, secretly taping the call. Asked whether "any of the recovered vehicles would ever become available for technological research," Inman replied, "I honestly don't know. Ten years ago, the answer would have been no," and referred him to Everett Hineman, the CIA's deputy director for science and technology. UFO researchers read this as tacit confirmation that the government holds crashed craft. 1,2

The official record

Inman never said what vehicles he meant, said he had "no exposure at all" to some of the subjects raised, and is reported to have said afterward that he thought the questions were about underwater vehicles. When published, the transcript drew an editorial response questioning Oechsler's interpretation. A version of the audio released in 2023 had missing segments simulated with AI, so it cannot be treated as the original recording. 1,2
