UFOs shut down nuclear missiles at Malmstrom in 1967

Claimed by Former missile officer Robert Salas and other veterans · 1996 · Interviews and books

Disputed · Officials and the claimant give conflicting accounts.

The claim

Former Air Force missile officer Robert Salas says that in March 1967 glowing objects hovered near Malmstrom's missile sites just as ten Minuteman missiles at Echo Flight went offline. 1

The official record

The Air Force's investigation of the Echo Flight failure found no connection to any UFO sighting, and later accounts tie the shutdown to an electrical fault, possibly from a secret test of electromagnetic pulse effects. Salas and other former officers stand by their accounts. 1
