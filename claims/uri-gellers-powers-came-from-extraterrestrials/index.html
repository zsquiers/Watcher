Uri Geller's powers came from extraterrestrials

Claimed by Andrija Puharich · 1974 · The book Uri

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

In Uri, Andrija Puharich wrote that he and Geller had communicated with super-intelligent computers from outer space, and that extraterrestrials had given Geller his abilities, from bending metal to telepathy, and chosen him to help humanity. Under hypnosis, Geller had said he was sent to Earth from a spaceship 53,000 light years away. 1,2

The official record

Geller later denied the space story, while allowing a slight possibility that some of his energies had an extraterrestrial connection. His tests at Stanford Research Institute were published in Nature in 1974 as worth further study, but critics found serious flaws: psychologists David Marks and Richard Kammann found Geller could have peeked through a hole in the lab wall, and Marks concluded he was a clever magician with no psychic ability. Reviewers called Uri credulous, and James Randi called its theories silly. 1,2
