A UFO crashed near Coyame, Mexico, after colliding with a plane

Claimed by An anonymous 1992 document, later authors Noe Torres and Ruben Uriarte · 1992 · "Research Findings on the Chihuahua Disk Crash," dated March 23, 1992, published by Leonard Stringfield in 1994

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

The document says that in 1974 a UFO tracked by radar into U.S. and then Mexican airspace collided with a small private plane flying from El Paso to Mexico City. The craft was said to have been recovered first by the Mexican Army and then captured by American military forces. 1

The official record

The document reached researcher Elaine Douglass from a source who has never been identified, and Stringfield himself warned of "the risk of publishing a bunch of baloney." Researcher Kevin Randle concluded that the case has "little in the way of evidence": a small plane did crash in the area, but almost nothing else is known, and even author Noe Torres has said "the evidence is definitely thin." 1
