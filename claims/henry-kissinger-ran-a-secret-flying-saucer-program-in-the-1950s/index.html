Henry Kissinger ran a secret flying-saucer program in the 1950s

Claimed by Researcher William Steinman, author Tony Brunt and podcaster Jesse Michels · 1984 · A researcher's account, a 2011 self-published book and a 2025 podcast

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Researcher William Steinman said that in 1984 the widow of an Air Force engineer who had died in 1960 told him her husband had worked on recovered discs and reported directly to Kissinger, then a little-known Harvard academic, who was "completely in charge" of the flying-saucer program. Author Tony Brunt revived the account in 2011, and a 2025 episode of Jesse Michels's American Alchemy podcast built a wider case around Kissinger's Army counterintelligence work in postwar Germany, his consulting for the Psychological Strategy Board, his ties to Nelson Rockefeller and his alleged links to the Majestic 12 papers. 1,2

The official record

The account rests on one secondhand report of a private conversation, and no document supports it. Kissinger's government consulting is on the record but has no known connection to UFOs. His counterintelligence work in 1945 and 1946 involved tracking Gestapo officers and running denazification, not recruiting German scientists, and even the podcast calls any role in Operation Paperclip speculative. The Majestic 12 papers that some link him to were judged fake by the FBI. 1,2,3,4,5
