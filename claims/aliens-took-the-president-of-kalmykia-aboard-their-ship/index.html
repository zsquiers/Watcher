Aliens took the president of Kalmykia aboard their ship

Claimed by Kirsan Ilyumzhinov · 2010 · Interviews, including Russian television

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Kirsan Ilyumzhinov, then president of the Russian republic of Kalmykia, says aliens took him from his Moscow apartment in September 1997 and showed him their ship, while his aides searched the apartment for him. 1

The official record

No evidence beyond his own account has been produced. When he repeated the story in 2010, a member of parliament asked President Medvedev to investigate whether he had revealed state secrets. 2
