CERN's collider opens portals for non-human beings

Claimed by Viral posts since 2009, and Swiss researcher Astrid Stuckelberger · 2025 · Online videos and interviews

Officially denied · The government says it is not true.

The claim

Viral posts claim that the Large Hadron Collider near Geneva opens portals to other dimensions. Many trace to a 2009 remark by CERN's then research director, Sergio Bertolucci, that the collider might open a "door" to an extra dimension, out of which something might come. In an interview reported in January 2025, Swiss public-health researcher Astrid Stuckelberger said CERN scientists had told her that beings from other universes come through a portal beneath the laboratory. 1,2

The official record

CERN says there is no portal beneath its facilities, that its experiments study the origins of the universe, and that imaginative works inspired by its research should not be confused with the research itself. Bertolucci never spoke of beings: his speculative extra dimension would open for a tiny fraction of a second, with no practical use and no risk. Physicist Dejan Stojkovic said that making even a microscopic wormhole would take an accelerator the size of the universe, and CERN's 2008 safety review found the collider presents no danger, since cosmic rays have caused collisions at similar or higher energies for billions of years. Stuckelberger has no training in physics, and her sources are unnamed. 1,2,3
