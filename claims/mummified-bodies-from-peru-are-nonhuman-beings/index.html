Mummified bodies from Peru are nonhuman beings

Claimed by Journalist Jaime Maussan · 2023 · Mexico's congressional hearings on UFOs, September and November 2023

Shown false · The evidence for it was shown to be fake or mistaken.

The claim

Maussan presented two small mummified bodies to Mexico's Congress, saying they came from a mine near Nazca, Peru, were more than 1,000 years old and were not "part of our terrestrial evolution," citing tests at the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM). In November he returned with doctors who said the bodies had once been living organisms. 1

The official record

UNAM said it never endorsed the claims and had only carbon-dated a sample. A 2017 report by Peru's prosecutor's office had found that similar bodies promoted by Maussan were "recently manufactured dolls," and forensic archaeologist Flavio Estrada, who examined the specimens for Peru, said they were not extraterrestrials or a new species. Maussan had earlier presented a supposed alien child in 2015 that proved to be a human child. 1
