Project Serpo: a secret exchange program with an alien planet

Claimed by Anonymous emails, promoted online by Bill Ryan · 2005 · Emails to a UFO discussion group, then a website

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

The story says an alien who survived a crash near Roswell in the late 1940s was repatriated, and that twelve U.S. military personnel later traveled to its home planet, Serpo, in the Zeta Reticuli system, staying from 1965 to 1978. 1

The official record

There is no physical evidence or official record of the program. Richard Doty, a known source of Air Force disinformation, offered similar material in 1983 and then backed out, and even the story's main promoter later said the releases contained disinformation. 1,2
