Aliens visit Earth and governments are hiding their technology

Claimed by Paul Hellyer · 2005 · Public statements and an exopolitics conference in Toronto

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Former Canadian defence minister Paul Hellyer said he believed UFOs were real and later urged world governments to disclose alien technology that he said could help solve climate change. 1

The official record

He offered no documents or firsthand evidence from his time in government. He also repeated the Project Serpo story of an alien exchange program, which came from Rick Doty, who later admitted spreading disinformation. 1
