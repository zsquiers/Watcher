French defense experts judged alien visitors the likeliest explanation for UFOs

Claimed by The COMETA association · 1999 · COMETA report, published in VSD magazine

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

The report concluded that unknown flying objects were almost certainly physically real and that the extraterrestrial hypothesis was the most probable explanation, though not proven. 1,2

The official record

It was not an officially commissioned government report: COMETA was a private association, though it was handed to Prime Minister Lionel Jospin, who reportedly read it, and the government made no official response. Critics including a writer in L'Express called it not credible, and when France's official UFO office later put it online it said this did not mean approval of the report. 1,2
