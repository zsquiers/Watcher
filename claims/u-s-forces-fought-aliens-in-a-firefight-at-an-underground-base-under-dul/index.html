U.S. forces fought aliens in a firefight at an underground base under Dulce, New Mexico, in 1979

Claimed by Philip Schneider · 1995 · His lecture tour of 1995

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Philip Schneider said he was a geological engineer who had been in a 1979 firefight with aliens deep under Archuleta Mesa near Dulce, part of a secret treaty arrangement between the government and extraterrestrials. 1

The official record

There is no evidence for the story, and author Greg Valdez could find no evidence to substantiate Schneider's claimed credentials. Valdez traces it to earlier tales from John Lear, Bob Lazar and the disinformation fed to Paul Bennewitz, with whom claims of alien activity at Dulce began, and the ufologist Norio Hayakawa dismisses Schneider's claims as delusions. 1,2
