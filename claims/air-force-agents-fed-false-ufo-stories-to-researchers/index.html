Air Force agents fed false UFO stories to researchers

Claimed by William L. Moore · 1989 · Speech, MUFON symposium

Partly confirmed · Some of it is on the record; the rest is not.

The claim

Researcher Bill Moore said Air Force intelligence agents, including Richard Doty, fed disinformation to UFO researchers such as Paul Bennewitz in the 1980s, and that he helped them. 1

The official record

There is no official acknowledgment. Doty has admitted his role in interviews, and the Bennewitz case is well documented, but the full scope of the operation and who directed it remain unknown. 2,3
