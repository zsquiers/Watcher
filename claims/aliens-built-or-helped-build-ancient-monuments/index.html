Aliens built or helped build ancient monuments

Claimed by Erich von Däniken · 1968 · Books and television, beginning with Chariots of the Gods?

Shown false · The evidence for it was shown to be fake or mistaken.

The claim

Ancient astronaut proponents say visitors from space built or helped build monuments such as the pyramids of Giza, Puma Punku, and the Moai of Easter Island, and were remembered as gods. 1

The official record

Archaeologists have documented the human builders, tools, and methods behind these monuments, and the ancient astronaut idea is regarded as pseudoarchaeology. Several of its key examples were shown to be mistaken or borrowed. 1,2
