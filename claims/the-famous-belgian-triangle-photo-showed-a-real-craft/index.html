The famous Belgian triangle photo showed a real craft

Claimed by An anonymous photographer · 1990 · Photo published during the Belgian UFO wave

Shown false · The evidence for it was shown to be fake or mistaken.

The claim

A photo of a black triangular object with lights at its corners, taken at Petit-Rechain in April 1990, became the emblem of the Belgian UFO wave. 1

The official record

In 2011 the photographer, Patrick Maréchal, said it was a polystyrene triangle with lightbulbs. The wider wave, including radar contacts that led the Belgian Air Force to scramble F-16s in March 1990, is still debated. 1
