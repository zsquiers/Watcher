Two non-human beings stood in a Las Vegas backyard after a fireball in 2023

Claimed by A Las Vegas family, in a 911 call · 2023 · A 911 call and police body camera footage, released in June 2023

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Shortly before midnight on April 30, 2023, a fireball crossed the sky over Nevada, Utah and California, and a Las Vegas police body camera recorded a light streaking low overhead. About 40 minutes later a man in northwest Las Vegas called 911 to say something had fallen into his backyard and two beings were there: "like 8 foot, 9 feet, 10 foot," with big shiny eyes, "100% not human." Officers came out and returned over several days, and drone video showed a circular mark in the dirt. 1,2,3

The official record

The fireball itself was real, but it was not a craft that landed. Mike Hankey of the American Meteor Society placed it about 100 miles north of Las Vegas, and NASA's planetary defense officer, Lindley Johnson, said it was likely a bright meteor less than a meter across and that nothing from it landed in anyone's backyard. Police found no craft or beings and closed the case. Nothing beyond the family's account supports the beings. 1,2,4
