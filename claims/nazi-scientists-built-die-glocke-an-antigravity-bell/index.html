Nazi scientists built Die Glocke, an antigravity "Bell"

Claimed by Polish writer Igor Witkowski and British journalist Nick Cook · 2000 · Witkowski's 2000 book The Truth About the Wonder Weapon and Cook's 2001 The Hunt for Zero Point

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Witkowski said he learned from a war-crimes trial affidavit, supplied by an unnamed Polish official, of a secret SS project in Lower Silesia. Cook described the device as bell-shaped, about 12 feet high, with two counter-rotating cylinders of a radioactive, liquid-metal substance code-named "Xerum 525," and suggested that SS General Hans Kammler traded the technology to the United States for his freedom. 1,2

The official record

Mainstream reviewers have called the claims pseudoscientific, recycled rumors and a hoax. Skeptic Robert Sheaffer called Cook's book "a classic example of how to spin an exciting yarn based on almost nothing," and Jason Colavito says the concrete "Henge" said to have tethered the Bell is the remains of an ordinary industrial cooling tower. 1
