John von Neumann secretly ran the Philadelphia Experiment and the Montauk Project

Claimed by Al Bielek and Preston Nichols · 1992 · Lectures and the Montauk Project book series

Officially denied · The government says it is not true.

The claim

In the Montauk Project story, launched by Preston Nichols and Peter Moon's 1992 book and Al Bielek's talks, von Neumann had directed the 1943 Philadelphia Experiment and was still alive in the 1980s, running time-travel and mind-control experiments at Montauk, New York. 1,2

The official record

The U.S. Navy says no such experiment was conducted and denies that von Neumann worked on one. He died in February 1957, so he could not have been at Montauk in the 1980s. Nichols and Bielek said they recovered their memories of the project years later, and researcher Jacques Vallée called their accounts highly questionable. 1,2,3
