A craft landed near a Melbourne school in front of 200 people

Claimed by Westall students and teachers · 1966 · Eyewitness accounts

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

In April 1966 more than 200 students and teachers at two Westall schools in Melbourne reported a craft descending into a nearby field before speeding away. 1

The official record

No official explanation was given. A newspaper at the time suggested a weather balloon, and witnesses still dispute that. 1
