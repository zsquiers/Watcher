Class VIII Hornet: a two-part UAP with hanging tendrils

Claimed by Skywatcher · 2025 · Skywatcher's research pages and its April 2025 video Mapping the Unknown

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Skywatcher describes Class VIII as a two-part object 2 to 4 meters across, colored purple, red, blue and black, with hanging tendrils. It says the upper and lower parts rotate out of step and may be physically connected, that the object rolls on two axes, produced strong radar tracks and a heat signature, was briefly visible to the naked eye, and descended suddenly in a way that suggested awareness. 1

The official record

Skywatcher calls this a preliminary classification and says it remains unconfirmed whether the two parts are connected. Its optical, infrared and radar data for the class are listed as coming soon and have not been published. 1
