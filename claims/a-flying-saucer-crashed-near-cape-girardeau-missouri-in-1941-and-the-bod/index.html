A flying saucer crashed near Cape Girardeau, Missouri, in 1941 and the bodies were taken by the military

Claimed by Charlette Mann, via Leonard Stringfield · 1991 · Leonard Stringfield's 1991 report on crash-retrieval stories

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Charlette Mann said her grandfather, Reverend William Huffman, told the family that in the spring of 1941 he was called out at night to pray over the crew of a crashed aircraft and found a disc and non-human bodies, which the military took away after swearing witnesses to secrecy. She described it as a deathbed confession. 1,2

The official record

The account comes only from family members, decades later, and a photo Mann described resembles a known hoax image. Records disagree on when Huffman began his pastorate, local media call the story the town's urban legend, and AARO's 2024 historical review found no evidence of any crash-retrieval program. 2,3
