A UFO dropped a mass of molten metal in an Iowa park

Claimed by Council Bluffs witnesses, and later Jacques Vallée and Garry Nolan · 1971 · Witness reports, laboratory analyses and a 2022 journal paper

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Witnesses said a hovering object dropped a glowing mass that left molten metal burning on a levee. Ames Laboratory ruled out a meteorite and said it did not resemble space hardware, and the Air Force gave reasons it could not be falling space debris. 1

The official record

The material itself is ordinary: a University of Iowa metallurgist judged it most likely a carbon steel, and a 2022 Stanford-led analysis found its titanium, iron and chromium isotopes consistent with terrestrial material, though unevenly mixed, as if incompletely mixed when it fell. How the metal came to be there has never been established, and its origin remains unknown. 1,2
