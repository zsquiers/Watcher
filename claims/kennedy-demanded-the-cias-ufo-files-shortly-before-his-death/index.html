Kennedy demanded the CIA's UFO files shortly before his death

Claimed by Author William Lester, and promoters of the Majestic documents · 2011 · A 2011 book by Lester, and documents circulated since 1999

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

William Lester says a November 12, 1963 memo from President Kennedy, which he obtained from the CIA under the Freedom of Information Act, ordered CIA director John McCone to organize the agency's UFO files and brief him by the following February; Kennedy was killed ten days later. A separate, partly burned document that surfaced in 1999 is said to show CIA officials resisting him, calling him by the codename Lancer. A handwritten note on it reads "Angleton has MJ directive," tying it to CIA counterintelligence chief James Jesus Angleton and the Majestic 12 papers. 1,2

The official record

Lester's memo hasn't turned up anywhere outside his book, and a JFK Library researcher found no copy and called its redactions and missing markings odd. The burned memo came from an anonymous source who claimed to be a former CIA officer, has never been verified, and most historians consider it a fake. Researcher Seamus Coogan calls it fraudulent and traces it, like other discredited Majestic papers, to Timothy Cooper, who later disowned one of his documents as a prank. 1,2,3
