Wernher von Braun warned that a fake alien threat would be used to justify weapons in space

Claimed by Carol Rosin · 2000 · Her Disclosure Project testimony, December 2000

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Carol Rosin said von Braun taught her that the case for space weapons would move through a series of enemies, first the Russians, then terrorists, then "third-world country crazies," then asteroids, and finally aliens, "the final scare," and that he told her, "the last card is the alien card." 1

The official record

The account rests entirely on Rosin's recollection, given about 23 years after von Braun died in 1977, and no writing or recording by von Braun supports it. Her telling uses later terms, such as "Nations of Concern." Reagan's own remarks about an alien threat, from 1985 and 1987, described it as something that would unite nations rather than justify weapons. 1,2,3
