The U.S. shot down a non-human craft during a 1962 nuclear test

Claimed by Harald Malmgren · 2025 · Recorded interview

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Weeks before his death, economist Harald Malmgren said the U.S. shot down a non-human craft during a 1962 nuclear test in the Marshall Islands and that he handled the debris. 1

The official record

There is no official comment and no public evidence. FBI files contradict parts of his account of his jobs at the time, and the U.S. held no nuclear tests in the Marshall Islands in 1962. 1,2
