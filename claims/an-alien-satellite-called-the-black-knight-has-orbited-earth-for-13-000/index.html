An alien satellite called the Black Knight has orbited Earth for 13,000 years

Claimed by Online conspiracy theory · 1998 · Websites and videos, built on photos from the 1998 STS-88 shuttle mission

Shown false · The evidence for it was shown to be fake or mistaken.

The claim

The story says an ancient alien satellite circles Earth in a polar orbit, and points to radio signals Nikola Tesla heard in 1899, delayed radio echoes in 1928, 1954 newspaper claims of unknown satellites, a dark orbiting object reported in 1960, and NASA photos of a black object taken during a 1998 shuttle mission. 1,2,3

The official record

The theory combines several unrelated stories into one narrative. Tesla never claimed his signals came from an object in orbit; the 1960 object was identified at the time as the remains of an Air Force Discoverer 8 satellite; and the 1998 photos, which NASA itself took and published, show a thermal blanket that came loose during a spacewalk and drifted away. Donald Keyhoe's 1954 claims came when no country could yet launch a satellite. 1,2,3
