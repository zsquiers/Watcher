Class III Blob: a single pulsing red orb that jumps between positions

Claimed by Skywatcher · 2025 · Skywatcher's research pages and its April 2025 video Mapping the Unknown

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Skywatcher describes Class III as a red, cloud-like orb 3 to 5 meters across with a pulsing bright core, always seen alone. It says the object oscillates side to side, makes rapid, near-instantaneous jumps between positions, can flare into a much larger red shape, and shows a strong, steady heat signature. 1

The official record

Skywatcher calls this a preliminary classification and records no direct interactions with it. Its optical, infrared and radar data for the class are listed as coming soon and have not been published. 1
