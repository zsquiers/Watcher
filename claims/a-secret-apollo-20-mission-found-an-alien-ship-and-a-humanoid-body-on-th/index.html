A secret Apollo 20 mission found an alien ship and a humanoid body on the Moon

Claimed by YouTube user "retiredafb" (William Rutledge) · 2007 · YouTube videos from April 2007

Shown false · The evidence for it was shown to be fake or mistaken.

The claim

Videos posted in 2007 said a secret joint U.S.-Soviet Apollo 20 mission launched from Vandenberg in 1976 and found an ancient alien ship on the far side of the Moon, with the body of a female humanoid nicknamed Mona Lisa. A man calling himself William Rutledge gave an interview, by instant message only, saying he had been on the crew. 1,2

The official record

Apollo 20 was canceled in 1970 so that its rocket could launch Skylab, and Apollo 17 in December 1972 was the program's last lunar landing. The first videos appeared on April Fool's Day, no astronaut named Rutledge exists, and the French videographer Thierry Speth has said he created the hoax; his book about it carries the disclaimer "This story is a fiction." 1,2,3,4,5
