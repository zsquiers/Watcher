An orange light over Delaware dropped a piece of its energy store

Claimed by Phyllis Budinger · 2014 · Frontier Analysis technical report for MUFON

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Budinger found the fragment was more than half palmitic acid soaked into fine glass fibers, a mix studied as a heat-storage material, and offered as a possible speculation that it came from some sort of energy storage process related to the object. 1

The official record

She also noted that glass fiber is most often used as insulation and that the fragment looked like insulation, though she said it did not match Chinese lanterns. Palmitic acid and glass fiber are both common manufactured materials, and a chemical analysis alone cannot show that the fragment came from the lights. It is one report on one sample, not independently reviewed. 1
