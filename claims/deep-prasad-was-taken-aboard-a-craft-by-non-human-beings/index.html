Deep Prasad was taken aboard a craft by non-human beings

Claimed by Deep Prasad · 2019 · Social media posts and podcast interviews

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Technology entrepreneur Deep Prasad says that sometime between 2017 and 2019 he was taken aboard a craft by non-human beings, an experience he compared to a kundalini awakening and that he says changed the direction of his life. 1,2

The official record

There is no official comment and no independent evidence; the account rests on his own statements. 1
