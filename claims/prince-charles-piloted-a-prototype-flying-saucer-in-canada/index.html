Prince Charles piloted a prototype flying saucer in Canada

Claimed by Canadian diver Dan Costello, in the documentary The King of UFOs · 2024 · The King of UFOs, a 2024 documentary by Mark Christopher Lee

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Costello says that in 1975 at Sandy Point, Nova Scotia, he saw a craft shaped like a UFO, with dual rotor blades like a helicopter but almost no sound, that gave off a blue ionic flame. He says the future King Charles III, then a Royal Navy helicopter pilot said to be stationed on HMS Endeavour, was one of three pilots in a secret operation called Project Serpico. 1

The official record

Costello is the only source, and no record of such a craft or operation has surfaced. Director Mark Christopher Lee says he wrote to the King about it and the palace has not replied. The documentary links the story to the 1967 Shag Harbour incident nearby, but offers no documents. 1
