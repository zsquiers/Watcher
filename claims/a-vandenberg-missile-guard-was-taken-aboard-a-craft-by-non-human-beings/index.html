A Vandenberg missile guard was taken aboard a craft by non-human beings in 1964

Claimed by Richard Barth · 2025 · Accounts to researcher Robert Hastings (November 2025) and in a YouTube video

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Barth says that one night in September 1964, guarding a Minuteman test site in fog, he saw a figure he took for an officer, then "my arm was suddenly paralyzed" and he blacked out. He describes waking aboard a craft surrounded by four small beings with large heads and dark eyes, and a telepathic order to tell no one. He says his memories returned two weeks later, and he claims non-human observers were monitoring missile sites around the world. 1,2

The official record

Barth's service record places him at Vandenberg in 1964, but no official military records confirm the abduction and no other witness has been named. The two published versions differ on some details. Hastings, who considers the account credible, notes that experiencer reports do not "constitute scientific evidence." 1,2
