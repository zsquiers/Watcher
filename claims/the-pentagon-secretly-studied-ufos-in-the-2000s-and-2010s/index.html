The Pentagon secretly studied UFOs in the 2000s and 2010s

Claimed by The New York Times and former officials · 2017 · News report

Confirmed · The core claim is backed by official records.

The claim

In December 2017 The New York Times reported that the Pentagon had run a secret program to study UFOs, funded at Senator Harry Reid's urging. 1

The official record

The Pentagon confirmed the program existed. It began in 2007, about $22 million went to a contract with Robert Bigelow's company, and officials said funding ended in 2012. 2
