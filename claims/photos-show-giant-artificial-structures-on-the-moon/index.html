Photos show giant artificial structures on the Moon

Claimed by Writer Richard C. Hoagland · 1997 · Hoagland's lectures, books and websites

Officially explained · An official investigation gave an ordinary explanation.

The claim

Hoagland says NASA photographs show ruins of artificial structures on the Moon, including towers and glass-like domes. In 1997 he received a satirical Ig Nobel prize for identifying artificial features on the Moon and Mars, including ten-mile-high buildings on the far side of the Moon. 1

The official record

Astronauts, NASA photo analysts and others attribute the features to photo processing defects and grainy, fuzzy spots resulting from excessive photo magnification. Later, sharper images from orbiters such as the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter show natural terrain. 1
