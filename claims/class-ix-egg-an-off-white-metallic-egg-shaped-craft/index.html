Class IX Egg: an off-white metallic egg-shaped craft

Claimed by Skywatcher · 2025 · Skywatcher's research pages and its April 2025 video Mapping the Unknown

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Skywatcher describes Class IX as an off-white, metallic, egg-shaped craft of unknown size, and says anecdotal reports suggest it appeared near the team's location. The shape matches the craft that Jake Barber, a Skywatcher co-founder, says he helped recover by helicopter. 1,2

The official record

Skywatcher itself calls this a provisional class with no corroborated motion, structure or spectral data, based on a single low-quality recording. No radar signature was confirmed and no infrared sensors were available. 1,3
