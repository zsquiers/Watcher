NASA photos showed a base on the far side of the Moon

Claimed by Karl Wolfe · 2001 · Disclosure Project witness accounts and interviews

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Karl Wolfe said that at Langley Air Force Base in the 1960s, an airman processing Lunar Orbiter imagery showed him a mosaic of the Moon's far side with towers, spherical buildings and very large dish-shaped structures, and told him they had discovered a base there. He said NASA was hiding the evidence. 1,2,3,4

The official record

Wolfe described something he was told and shown once; he kept no copy, and the other airman has never been identified. The five Lunar Orbiter missions of 1966 and 1967 photographed 99 percent of the Moon, including the far side, and since 2007 the Lunar Orbiter Image Recovery Project has re-digitized the original tapes at higher resolution; no structures have been found in them. The first Lunar Orbiter, the one Wolfe named, mainly photographed candidate Apollo landing sites on the near side. 5
