A Skunk Works chief admitted the U.S. can already travel to the stars

Claimed by Ben Rich · 1993 · Talk to UCLA engineering alumni, as recalled by attendees

Officially explained · An official investigation gave an ordinary explanation.

The claim

Attendees recalled that retired Skunk Works director Ben Rich closed a 1993 talk by saying the U.S. now had the technology to take E.T. home, and UFO writers present this as an insider's admission of interstellar travel. A longer version, widely shared with his name, reads: "We already have the means to travel among the stars, but these technologies are locked up in black projects, and it would take an act of God to ever get them out to benefit humanity." 1

The official record

No recording or transcript of the talk is known, and the wording changes from one retelling to the next. Aerospace historian Peter Merlin found in Rich's papers that the line was a standard closing joke Rich had used since at least 1983, when he told a symposium that the Skunk Works had been assigned to get E.T. back home. Merlin calls the longer "act of God" version an un-sourced quote, one of several statements attributed to Rich without corroborating proof. 1
