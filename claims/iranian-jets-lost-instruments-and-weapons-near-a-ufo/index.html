Iranian jets lost instruments and weapons near a UFO

Claimed by Imperial Iranian Air Force crews · 1976 · Military reports, relayed in a U.S. Defense Intelligence Agency memo

Partly confirmed · Some of it is on the record; the rest is not.

The claim

Two Iranian F-4 crews reported that as they closed on a bright object over Tehran in September 1976, they lost instruments and communications, and one crew's weapons system failed as it prepared to fire. 1

The official record

A U.S. Defense Intelligence Agency memo relayed the crews' accounts and called the case outstanding, a classic for UFO study. No physical evidence exists; skeptics including Philip Klass and James Oberg suggest the pilots first saw Jupiter. 1
