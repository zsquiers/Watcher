A craft with two small beings landed in a lavender field at Valensole

Claimed by Maurice Masse · 1965 · His account to the gendarmes, then to investigators and the press

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Masse said he found a landed craft with two small beings beside it in his lavender field before dawn. When he approached, one pointed a tubelike object at him and he could not move. He said he watched them looking at his plants and making grunting sounds until they went back inside and the craft flew off. 1

The official record

Gendarmes found a shallow depression with a hole at its center, and ufologists cite landing gear impressions in the soil. Investigators judged Masse sincere, but no one else saw the craft or the beings, and the case has never been explained. 1,2
