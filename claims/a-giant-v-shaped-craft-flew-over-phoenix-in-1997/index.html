A giant V-shaped craft flew over Phoenix in 1997

Claimed by Thousands of witnesses across Arizona · 1997 · Eyewitness reports

Officially explained · An official investigation gave an ordinary explanation.

The claim

On March 13, 1997, thousands of people across Arizona reported a huge, silent, V-shaped formation of lights passing overhead, and later a row of hovering lights over Phoenix. 1

The official record

The hovering lights were identified as illumination flares dropped by A-10 aircraft on a training exercise, and researchers identified the moving formation as A-10s flying back to Davis-Monthan Air Force Base. Many witnesses reject these explanations. 1
