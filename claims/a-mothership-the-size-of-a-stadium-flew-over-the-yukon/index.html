A mothership the size of a stadium flew over the Yukon

Claimed by Witnesses, and investigator Martin Jasek · 1996 · Witness reports collected by a Yukon UFO group

Officially explained · An official investigation gave an ordinary explanation.

The claim

At least 31 people in four places along the Klondike Highway described a huge craft with rows of lights on December 11, 1996, and investigator Martin Jasek concluded they had seen a mothership. 1,2

The official record

Satellite analyst Ted Molczan found that the rocket stage that launched the Soviet Cosmos 2335 satellite re-entered over the region that evening, in a west-to-east track visible from all the sighting areas. He judged that witnesses described it well but read a structure into a line of burning pieces. 1
