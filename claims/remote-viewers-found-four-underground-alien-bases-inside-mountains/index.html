Remote viewers found four underground alien bases inside mountains

Claimed by Pat Price · 2024 · Pat Price's 1973 transcript and the Project 8200 sessions, published in Skip Atwater's 2024 book

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

In 1973 Pat Price, working for SRI as a contractor, made a self-initiated remote viewing transcript describing four underground UFO bases: inside Mount Hayes in Alaska, Monte Perdido in the Pyrenees, Mount Inyangani (now Nyangani) in Zimbabwe and Mount Zeil in Australia. He described tunnels, disc-shaped equipment and, at Mount Hayes, humanoid beings working alongside military personnel. Army remote viewers given the sites as blind training targets in Skip Atwater's Project 8200 described buried facilities, electronics and unusual personnel, and Atwater writes that the data appear to confirm four subterranean sites whose purpose involves precise observation, relay and perhaps navigation. In 2026 Lyn Buchanan described Mount Zeil as a port of entry for friendly visitors. 1,2,3

The official record

Every target came from Price's own narrative, and there was no physical answer key against which to score the sessions, so their partial agreement on buried structures and electronics cannot confirm a site exists. Atwater says the results were never reported up the chain of command and Project 8200 remained a training exercise. Paul H. Smith, who viewed Mount Hayes, writes that he is not sure the findings are real, though he thinks some may be. Buchanan says he has never done the Pyrenees session. No images, documents or other physical evidence of any base has surfaced, and the 1995 review commissioned by the CIA found remote viewing had never been used to guide intelligence operations. 1,2,3,4
