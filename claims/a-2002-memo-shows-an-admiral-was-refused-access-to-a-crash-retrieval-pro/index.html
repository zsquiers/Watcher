A 2002 memo shows an admiral was refused access to a crash-retrieval program

Claimed by Notes attributed to Eric W. Davis · 2019 · About 15 pages posted online in 2019, known as the Wilson memo or EWD notes

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

The pages are presented as physicist Eric W. Davis's notes of an October 2002 conversation in which retired Vice Adm. Thomas R. Wilson, a former Defense Intelligence Agency director, said that while on the Joint Staff he learned of a program to reverse-engineer recovered craft inside an aerospace contractor, and was denied access to it. They were uploaded to an image-sharing site on April 19, 2019, and later raised at the May 2022 House UAP hearing. Their storyline closely matches what David Grusch alleged in 2023. 1,2

The official record

With no provenance, it is unclear if the documents are legitimate. Wilson denies the meeting took place, saying, "It's all fiction. I wouldn't know Eric Davis if he walked in right now," and Davis has given only "no comment" responses. The Pentagon intelligence officials asked about it at the 2022 hearing said they were not aware of it, and AARO's 2024 historical review found no evidence of any program holding recovered alien technology. 1,2,3
