Governments have hidden proof of non-human visitors since the 1940s

Claimed by Jonathan Caplan · 2026 · The book Not For Disclosure and a Sky News interview

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Caplan says the craft are real and non-human intelligences are here, and that the United States has held a crashed craft or biological remains since the 1940s. He told Sky News that Congress has been misled about it for 80 years. His book says it draws on sworn testimony, inside sources and previously unpublished documents, including papers from the Majestic 12 group. 1,2,3

The official record

International Business Times noted that none of his allegations had been independently verified beyond his own account, and that he did not back his claim that public money was diverted to secret UAP programs with specific figures. No government body has confirmed his account. 2
