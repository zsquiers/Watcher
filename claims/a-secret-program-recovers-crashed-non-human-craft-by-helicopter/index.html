A secret program recovers crashed non-human craft by helicopter

Claimed by Jake Barber · 2025 · NewsNation special with Ross Coulthart

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Former Air Force helicopter pilot Jake Barber says he flew missions for a secret program recovering crashed craft, including a white egg-shaped object he believes is non-human, and NewsNation aired a clip of it beneath a helicopter. 1

The official record

The Defense Department said it was looking into his claims, and AARO said it was aware of them. NewsNation said three veterans backed him, but no independent evidence has shown what the object was or where the footage came from. 2
