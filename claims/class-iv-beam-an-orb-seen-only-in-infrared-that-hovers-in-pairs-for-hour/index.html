Class IV Beam: an orb seen only in infrared that hovers in pairs for hours

Claimed by Skywatcher · 2025 · Skywatcher's research pages and its April 2025 video Mapping the Unknown

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Skywatcher describes Class IV as an orb of unknown size, seen in pairs, that is strongly visible to infrared cameras but invisible to ordinary cameras. It says the objects hover for over an hour at a time, vibrate sideways, make sharp, dodging moves when lased, and that their infrared signature faded out when the team switched on a jammer and returned when it was switched off. 1

The official record

Skywatcher calls this a preliminary classification, and its infrared data and full report for the class are listed as coming soon. An object seen only in infrared and never in visible light is hard for anyone else to confirm without the data. 1
