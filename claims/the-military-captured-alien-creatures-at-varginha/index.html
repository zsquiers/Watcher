The military captured alien creatures at Varginha

Claimed by Local witnesses and Brazilian ufologists · 1996 · Witness accounts and press reports

Officially explained · An official investigation gave an ordinary explanation.

The claim

In January 1996, three young women in Varginha, Brazil, said they saw a strange creature, and rumors spread that the military had captured beings nearby. 1

The official record

A 1997 Brazilian military inquiry concluded the women had seen a local man known as Mudinho, and that troop movements in the area that week were routine. 1
