Police officers' UFO encounters show a non-human intelligence is visiting Earth

Claimed by Roy Teague · 2026 · Close Encounters of the Police Kind and podcast interviews

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Roy Teague has interviewed more than 250 serving and retired UK police officers about UFO sightings and says their testimony convinces him beyond reasonable doubt that a non-human intelligence is visiting Earth. His cases include a police helicopter crew who say they circled a metallic paper dart-shaped craft over central London in 1997, a red glowing sphere that he says paced PC David Flanagan's patrol car along the A19 and knocked out its radios, and Officer F's 1989 encounter in Aboyne with a black triangle, 30 minutes of missing time and a later-found metallic object near his eye. 1,2

The official record

Police officers are trained observers, and Teague names most of his witnesses, but almost all of the accounts were given decades after the events, in interviews he conducted himself, and none comes with released radar data, footage, police logs or medical records. Some witnesses are anonymous (he says about 12 of 300), including Officer F. The London crew say they never reported their sighting at the time, so no contemporaneous record exists. Teague himself suggests many modern black triangles may be secret military craft. 2
