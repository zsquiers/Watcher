The Navy UAP videos show real, unidentified objects

Claimed by To The Stars Academy and The New York Times · 2017 · Video release and news report

Confirmed · The core claim is backed by official records.

The claim

Three cockpit videos recorded by Navy pilots in 2004 and 2015, published in 2017, were presented as genuine military footage of objects the pilots could not identify. 1

The official record

In April 2020 the Department of Defense officially released the three videos, confirmed they were authentic Navy footage, and said the objects remained unidentified. AARO's 2025 analysis of one of them, GoFast, found no anomalous speed. 2,3
