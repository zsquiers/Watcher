Two Finnish skiers met a being in a pale green suit

Claimed by Aarno Heinonen and Esko Viljo · 1970 · Interviews and books

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

The two men said a glowing saucer released a mist and that they briefly saw a small being in a pale green suit, after which both fell ill. 1

The official record

The encounter lasted about ten seconds. Critics note that the two men's drawings of the being differ and only one saw the device it held, and that Heinonen went on to report dozens of further sightings and contacts, which casts doubt on the original case. 1
