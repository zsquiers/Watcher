Chinese military jets chased a UFO over a Hebei air base

Claimed by The Hebei Daily · 1998 · Chinese state press, as reported by the South China Morning Post

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

The province's official newspaper reported that two jets were sent up after a mushroom-shaped object with beams of light over a military airbase, which then climbed away to more than 20,000 meters. 1,2

The official record

The case is known only from the state press report; no radar data, film or official explanation has been published. In 2021 the South China Morning Post reported that the People's Liberation Army now runs a three-tier system for reporting and analyzing unidentified objects. 1,2
