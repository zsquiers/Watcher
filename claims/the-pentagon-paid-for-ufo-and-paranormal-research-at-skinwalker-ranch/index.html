The Pentagon paid for UFO and paranormal research at Skinwalker Ranch

Claimed by James Lacatski · 2021 · Book, Skinwalkers at the Pentagon

Partly confirmed · Some of it is on the record; the rest is not.

The claim

Former DIA scientist James Lacatski and co-authors say the DIA-funded AAWSAP program studied UFOs and paranormal events, with Skinwalker Ranch as its main field site. 1

The official record

The program's existence, its roughly $22 million contract with Bigelow's company, and its 38 technical papers are on the record. The paranormal events described at the ranch have not been independently verified. 1
