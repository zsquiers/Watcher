Vannevar Bush led a secret U.S. study of flying saucers

Claimed by Robert Sarbacher, as reported by Wilbert Smith · 1950 · Canadian government memo, November 21, 1950

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Canadian engineer Wilbert Smith wrote that U.S. scientist Robert Sarbacher told him Americans believed flying saucers were real, and that their investigation, led by Vannevar Bush, was more highly classified than the hydrogen bomb. 1

The official record

The memo is a genuine Canadian document, classified Top Secret until 1969, but it records a secondhand conversation. No U.S. document confirms a study led by Bush. The Majestic 12 papers later named him too, but they are considered fake, and one carried a photocopied Truman signature lifted from a real 1947 letter to Bush. 1,2,3
