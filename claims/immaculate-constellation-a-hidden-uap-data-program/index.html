Immaculate Constellation, a hidden UAP data program

Claimed by Michael Shellenberger · 2024 · Report entered into the record, House Oversight hearing

Officially denied · The government says it is not true.

The claim

A report given to Congress by journalist Michael Shellenberger, later attributed to former Pentagon official Matthew Brown, says an unacknowledged special access program called Immaculate Constellation collects high-quality UAP imagery and sensor data, hidden from Congress. 1,2

The official record

A Pentagon spokesperson said the Department of Defense has no record, present or historical, of any special access program by that name. 3
