A glowing ball hit a car and left residue from the object

Claimed by Phyllis Budinger · 2001 · Frontier Analysis technical report

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Budinger found a metal oxide, a kaolinite-type clay and a plant-based cellulose material in the residue, and judged it very probable that the metal oxide came from the object, since it did not look like a common paint ingredient. 1

The official record

The residue was a tiny scraping mixed with the car's own paint layers, and Budinger herself noted that cellulose fillers and silicate additives are used in car paint, so their source could not be settled without the paint specifications. By the time the car was examined it showed no dent and no residue. There was one witness, and no outside lab has checked the result. 1
