Travis Walton was taken aboard a craft for five days

Claimed by Travis Walton · 1975 · Police reports, interviews, and a 1978 book

Disputed · Officials and the claimant give conflicting accounts.

The claim

Logger Travis Walton's crew said a beam from a hovering craft struck him in November 1975; he reappeared five days later saying he had been aboard with alien beings. 1

The official record

There was no official finding. His co-workers passed later polygraph tests, but the examiner in Walton's first test judged him grossly deceptive, and skeptics such as Philip Klass and Michael Shermer argued it was a hoax. 1
