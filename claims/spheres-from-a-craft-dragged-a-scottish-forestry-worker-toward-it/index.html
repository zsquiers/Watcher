Spheres from a craft dragged a Scottish forestry worker toward it

Claimed by Robert Taylor · 1979 · Statements to police and the press

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Taylor said two smaller spheres seized him and pulled him toward a hovering dome; his trousers were torn and he had grazes, and police recorded the matter as an assault. 1

The official record

Steuart Campbell, who visited the site with police, said the marks matched PVC pipes that had been stored in the clearing. Others, including skeptic Steve Donnelly, have suggested a temporal lobe epilepsy seizure, with the objects as hallucinations, and a 2013 newspaper report raised a mini-stroke and a nearby saucer-shaped water tower. No one else saw the objects. 1
