Class VII Jellyfish: a white UAP with trailing tentacles that interferes with electronics

Claimed by Skywatcher · 2025 · Skywatcher's research pages and its April 2025 video Mapping the Unknown

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Skywatcher describes Class VII as a white, bulbous body about 2 meters across with tentacles 3 to 5 meters long, moving in a winding, snake-like path. It says the class has shown electromagnetic interference and is often mistaken for other classes at a distance. 1

The official record

Skywatcher calls this a preliminary classification, and its optical, infrared, radar and radio data for the class are listed as coming soon. The class shares its shape with the Iraq and Anchorage jellyfish videos, but Skywatcher has not linked them, and skeptics have proposed balloons for both. 1,2,3
