Aliens intruded on an Ellsworth missile site and abducted its guards in 1977

Claimed by The L-9 document; later, veterans Mario Woods and Michael Johnson · 1977 · An alleged FOIA document, and a 2026 podcast interview

Disputed · Officials and the claimant give conflicting accounts.

The claim

A document describing a November 1977 intrusion at Ellsworth's L-9 launch facility says security airmen met figures in glowing suits near a hovering craft. In 2026, former security policemen Mario Woods and Michael Johnson said they lost hours of time while guarding an Ellsworth missile site that year. 1,2

The official record

No official record of the event has been released. The L-9 document was exposed as a hoax in 1984 and is strongly suspected to be a fabrication by Richard Doty, who served at Ellsworth then; it names different airmen than Woods and Johnson. Their own account has not been independently corroborated, and their accounts of how long they were missing vary. 1
