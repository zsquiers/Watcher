Military remote viewers read Saddam Hussein's plans during the Gulf War

Claimed by Lyn Buchanan · 2003 · The book The Seventh Sense

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Lyn Buchanan writes that his main task during the 1991 Gulf War was to access Saddam Hussein and learn his plans and intentions for the coming day, and that hardly a move made by Saddam and his troops had not been predicted and passed up the chain of command the day before. David Morehouse also said he remote viewed the war. 1,2

The official record

No document showing these results has been released, and the claims rest on the viewers' own accounts. The CIA-commissioned 1995 review by the American Institutes for Research found that information from remote viewing had never been used to guide intelligence operations, and the program was closed. Publishers Weekly said skeptics would vigorously dispute Buchanan's claims. 3,4
