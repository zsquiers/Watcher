UFOs have watched, and interfered with, U.S. nuclear weapons

Claimed by Robert Hastings · 2008 · UFOs and Nukes, and a 2010 National Press Club briefing

Disputed · Officials and the claimant give conflicting accounts.

The claim

Robert Hastings says more than 160 veterans have told him of unidentified objects over nuclear missile sites, weapons storage areas and test ranges, sometimes as missiles went offline, and that this shows a deliberate interest in nuclear weapons. 1,2

The official record

Most accounts are decades-old recollections without supporting records. The Air Force's investigation of the best-known case, the 1967 missile failures at Malmstrom, found no link to UFOs, and skeptics argue the cases recycle reports that were already explained. 1,3
