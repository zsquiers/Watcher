A cigar-shaped craft with lit windows flew over the eastern U.S.

Claimed by Witnesses in Tennessee, Ohio and nearby states · 1968 · UFO reports to the Air Force and the Condon study

Officially explained · An official investigation gave an ordinary explanation.

The claim

A few of the people who saw fiery objects cross the sky on March 3, 1968, described a cigar-shaped craft with a row of lighted windows and a fiery tail. 1

The official record

NORAD identified the objects as pieces of the Soviet Zond 4 launch re-entering the atmosphere. Astronomer William Hartmann, writing in the Condon Report, used the case to show how witnesses can turn a line of burning fragments into a single craft. 1
