Navy pilots saw UAP almost daily off the East Coast

Claimed by Ryan Graves · 2019 · Interviews, and sworn testimony in 2023

Partly confirmed · Some of it is on the record; the rest is not.

The claim

Former Navy pilot Ryan Graves says that in 2014 and 2015 his squadron off the Virginia coast encountered unidentified objects nearly every day, some posing a safety risk to aircraft. 1

The official record

The Navy acknowledged a rise in such reports and in 2019 drafted formal guidelines for pilots to report them. The objects have not been publicly identified, and no official explanation for them as a group has been released. 2
