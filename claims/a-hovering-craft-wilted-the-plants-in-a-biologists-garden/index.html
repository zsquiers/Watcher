A hovering craft wilted the plants in a biologist's garden

Claimed by A biologist known as Monsieur Henri · 1982 · Gendarmerie report and GEPAN investigation

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

The witness said an ovoid metallic object hovered over his garden for 20 minutes, and afterward the amaranth plants nearest it had wilted, which he linked to the object. 1

The official record

GEPAN found nothing in the witness's account or manner to suggest a serious distortion and saw no reasonable grounds to reject it, but there was a single witness and no trace in the grass. Its plant analyses proved inconclusive: the plants had been dug up and the first samples were not kept cold enough. The note also points out that temperatures had dropped below freezing the day before, which could explain the drying of leaves on part of the bed. 1
