Class VI Bright Star: a reflective tetrahedron that flashes colored light

Claimed by Skywatcher · 2025 · Skywatcher's research pages and its April 2025 video Mapping the Unknown

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Skywatcher describes Class VI as a reflective tetrahedron 1.5 to 3 meters across that flashes teal, green, blue, red and white light and oscillates side to side so fast that it leaves propeller-like artifacts on radar. It says the class showed a strong heat signature and disrupted the team's sensors with what it calls directed energy. 1

The official record

Skywatcher calls this a preliminary classification. Its optical, infrared, radar and radio data and the full report for the class are listed as coming soon, so the claimed directed-energy effects have not been shown publicly. 1
