The military recovered a craft at Kecksburg

Claimed by Kecksburg witnesses · 1965 · Witness accounts

Disputed · Officials and the claimant give conflicting accounts.

The claim

Witnesses say an acorn-shaped object fell into the woods near Kecksburg, Pennsylvania, in December 1965 and that the military cordoned off the area and took it away. 1

The official record

Officials said nothing was found, and astronomers attributed the fireball to a meteor. NASA later said fragments from the area came from a Soviet satellite, but that its records of the findings were lost in 1987. 1
