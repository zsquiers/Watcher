Ancient Hindu texts describe real flying machines

Claimed by G. R. Josyer and ancient astronaut writers · 1952 · The Vaimānika Shāstra, revealed by Josyer in 1952 and published in English in 1973

Shown false · The evidence for it was shown to be fake or mistaken.

The claim

Vimanas are flying palaces and chariots in the Hindu epics. The Vaimānika Shāstra claims they were advanced aircraft and gives technical descriptions, which Josyer said came from the ancient sage Bharadvaja. The text is popular with ancient astronaut proponents. 1

The official record

The text dates from 1918 to 1923, dictated by Pandit Subbaraya Shastry, who said it was psychically delivered to him. A 1974 study by aeronautical engineers at the Indian Institute of Science found its aircraft were "poor concoctions" and one design "a decided impossibility." 1
