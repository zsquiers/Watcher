A DIA director was refused access to a hidden UFO program

Claimed by Eric W. Davis · 2019 · Leaked notes attributed to Eric Davis

Disputed · Officials and the claimant give conflicting accounts.

The claim

Notes of a 2002 meeting, leaked online in 2019 and attributed to physicist Eric Davis, say former DIA director Vice Admiral Thomas Wilson found a secret program working on recovered craft and was denied access to it. 1

The official record

The notes have no known provenance. Wilson has called the story fiction and said he never met Davis, and Davis has declined to confirm he wrote them. 1
