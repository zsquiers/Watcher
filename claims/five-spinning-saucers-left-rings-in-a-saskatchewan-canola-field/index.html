Five spinning saucers left rings in a Saskatchewan canola field

Claimed by Edwin Fuhr · 1974 · Witness report and RCMP investigation

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Fuhr said five saucer-shaped objects spun just above the ground near a slough on his farm, and the RCMP found five circles where he said they had been. 1

The official record

The RCMP found nothing to suggest anyone had driven in to make the circles, and Fuhr was the only witness. Decades on, as the Leader-Post put it, there are no concrete answers for what happened on the farm. 1,2
