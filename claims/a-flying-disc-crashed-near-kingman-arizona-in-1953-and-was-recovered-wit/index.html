A flying disc crashed near Kingman, Arizona, in 1953 and was recovered with a small occupant

Claimed by Engineer Arthur G. Stansel ("Fritz Werner"), via Raymond Fowler · 1973 · Raymond Fowler's investigations and a 1976 affidavit

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

An engineer working on the 1953 Nevada atomic tests said he was taken with other specialists to a crashed disc near Kingman, where he saw the body of a being about four feet tall in a silver suit. Raymond Fowler brought the story to public attention in 1973 and later published the man's signed affidavit. 1

The official record

The story rests on one witness, and the calendar pages offered as support prove very little; checks on it failed to produce results, according to the researcher Kevin Randle. No record of such a recovery has been found, and AARO's 2024 historical review found no evidence of any crash-retrieval program. 2,3
