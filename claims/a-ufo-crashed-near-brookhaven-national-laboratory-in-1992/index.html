A UFO crashed near Brookhaven National Laboratory in 1992

Claimed by John Ford and the Long Island UFO Network · 1992 · Long Island UFO Network investigations

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

The Long Island UFO Network, led by John Ford, said a craft crashed in Southaven Park, near Brookhaven National Laboratory, on November 24, 1992, starting a forest fire, and that local officials covered it up. Ford also blamed a later Pine Barrens wildfire on a UFO crash. 1,2

The official record

No physical evidence has been produced, and the local MUFON director who visited found only bent trees. In June 1996 Ford was arrested on charges of plotting to kill three Suffolk County politicians, whom he believed were covering up UFO activity, with radium; two co-defendants pleaded guilty to lesser crimes in exchange for testimony, and a judge found Ford unfit to stand trial and committed him to a psychiatric hospital. 1,2
