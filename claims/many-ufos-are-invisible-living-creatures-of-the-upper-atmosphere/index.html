Many UFOs are invisible living creatures of the upper atmosphere

Claimed by Trevor James Constable · 1958 · They Live in the Sky (1958) and The Cosmic Pulse of Life (1976)

Unverified · No public evidence either way.

The claim

Constable said special photography revealed amoeba-like animals in the sky, from the size of a coin to half a mile across, and that many UFO sightings were these creatures becoming briefly visible. He said radar could provoke them. He used older accounts as support, including the 1925 story of a pilot named Don Wood Jr. who said he watched two glowing discs on a Nevada mesa. 1,2

The official record

Constable's evidence is his own photographs, which show discolored shapes he interpreted as animals. The Wood story first appeared as an anonymous letter in 1959, 34 years after the events, with no other witness or physical evidence. Skeptics suggest the Wood sighting was a mirage, an atmospheric effect or a misremembered sight of birds or debris. 1,2
