The U.S. secretly recovers crashed non-human craft

Claimed by David Grusch · 2023 · Sworn testimony, House Oversight Committee

Officially denied · The government says it is not true.

The claim

Former intelligence officer David Grusch testified that the U.S. has run a decades-long program to retrieve and reverse-engineer crashed craft of non-human origin, and that he learned of it from officials with direct knowledge. 1,2

The official record

AARO's 2024 historical report found no evidence that any U.S. government investigation confirmed extraterrestrial technology, or of hidden reverse-engineering programs. Grusch says the evidence is held in classified programs AARO did not see. 2,3
