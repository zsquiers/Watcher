Setka-MO

1978 to about 1991

Soviet Union and Russia · 1978 to about 1991 · Disbanded after 1991

The Defence Ministry's "Net" group. With its Academy of Sciences twin, it looked at about 3,000 reports; only 300 of the 3,000 or so cases were judged truly anomalous, most of the rest being rocket launches, balloons and other man-made objects. Its 1990 conclusion found no substantive evidence of alien visitation. Both groups were disbanded after the Soviet Union collapsed. 1,2
