Defence UFO archive

1978 to 2002, published 2009

Denmark · 1978 to 2002, published 2009 · Published 2009

Danish Defence received more than 300 UFO reports from 1978; the last was logged in December 2002. The archive was published in January 2009, and according to the Air Force most of the observations were never resolved. 1
