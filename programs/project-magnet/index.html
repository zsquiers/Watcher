Project Magnet

1950 to 1954

Canada · 1950 to 1954 · Ended 1954

Set up by Transport Canada in December 1950 at the urging of engineer Wilbert Smith, who wanted to know whether UFOs used magnetism to fly. Smith's experiments were inconclusive, and the department shut down Project Magnet; it was formally active until mid-1954. 1,2
