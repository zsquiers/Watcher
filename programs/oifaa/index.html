OIFAA

2001 to about 2008, reopened 2013

Peru · 2001 to about 2008, reopened 2013 · Reopened 2013

The Office of Anomalous Aerial Phenomena Research was originally set up in 2001, shut down for the last five years, and reactivated in October 2013 after more sightings were reported. News reports also call it DIFAA. Its current status is not confirmed. 1
