AARO

All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office

United States · All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office · Active, 2022 to now

Created in July 2022 as the successor to AOIMSG, and written into law by the FY2023 NDAA. Investigates UAP in the air, at sea, in space, and on land, and reviews the government's historical UAP record. 1,2

Its March 2024 Historical Record Report (Volume 1) found no evidence that any government investigation had confirmed extraterrestrial technology, or of hidden reverse-engineering programs. 3

First director: physicist Sean Kirkpatrick (2022 to 2023). Current director: Jon T. Kosloski. AARO works jointly with ODNI on intelligence reporting. 1,2
