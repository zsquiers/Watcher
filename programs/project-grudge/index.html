Project Grudge

1949 to 1952

United States · 1949 to 1952 · Became Blue Book 1952

Took over from Project Sign in February 1949 with the aim of easing public anxiety about UFOs, and explained sightings as aircraft, balloons, stars, planets, meteors, illusions and hoaxes. Its August 1949 report found no evidence of advanced foreign technology, and the Air Force announced its end on December 27, 1949. 1

Edward Ruppelt revived it in October 1951 with a more open approach. By March 1952 it had become a separate unit, and soon afterward it was renamed Project Blue Book. 2,3
