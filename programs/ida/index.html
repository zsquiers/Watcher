IDA

Institute for Defense Analyses

United States · Institute for Defense Analyses

A nonprofit founded in 1956 in the Washington area that runs federally funded research centers for the Pentagon, doing systems analysis, weapons testing evaluation, and science and technology studies. It hosted the JASON science advisory group in the 1960s. 1

Harald Malmgren worked there in the early 1960s, and later claimed knowledge of a 1962 UFO shootdown. IDA has no known UAP program. 1
