Flying Saucer Working Party

1950 to 1951

United Kingdom · 1950 to 1951 · Report 1951

A small study team set up in October 1950, commissioned by the ministry's Chief Scientific Adviser, Sir Henry Tizard. Its report, finished in June 1951, put the sightings down to ordinary causes and recommended no further investigation of reported mysterious aerial phenomena. 1
