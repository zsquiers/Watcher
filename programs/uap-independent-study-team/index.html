UAP independent study team

2022 to 2023

United States · 2022 to 2023 · Completed 2023

A 16-member panel chaired by astrophysicist David Spergel. Its September 2023 report found no evidence of extraterrestrial origin and called for better data collection. 1,2

NASA also named a director of UAP research at the same time.
