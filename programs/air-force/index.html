Air Force

Department of the Air Force

United States · Department of the Air Force

Ran the government's first long-running UFO investigations, from the late 1940s to 1969. The best known, Project Blue Book, grew out of Project Sign and Project Grudge, and closed after the Condon Report concluded further study was unlikely to be productive. 1,2,3

It also ran Project Twinkle, which tried to photograph the green fireballs over New Mexico, and the secret Project Mogul, whose balloon trains it later named as the most likely source of the Roswell debris. 4,5
