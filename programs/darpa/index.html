DARPA

Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency

United States · Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency

Created in 1958 as ARPA in response to the Soviet Sputnik launch, and renamed DARPA in 1972. It funds high-risk research that led to the internet's forerunner ARPANET, stealth aircraft, GPS receivers, and drones. 1

It has no known UAP program, but it is often mentioned in discussions of advanced aerospace research and secret technology.
