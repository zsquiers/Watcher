CIAE

CEFAE 2011, CIAE 2019 to now

Argentina · CEFAE 2011, CIAE 2019 to now · Active, 2011 to now

Began on 6 May 2011 as CEFAE, the Comisión de Estudio de Fenómenos Aeroespaciales, and was restructured in April 2019 as CIAE, the Centro de Identificación Aeroespacial. It mainly identifies objects such as space debris, and analyzes public sighting reports on the side. 1
