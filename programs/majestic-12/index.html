Majestic 12

Alleged 1947 committee

United States · Alleged 1947 committee · Documents considered fake

Claimed to be a secret group of officials created by President Truman to manage crashed-saucer recoveries. The documents surfaced in the 1980s. 1

The FBI concluded they were fake, and the National Archives found no record of the group. 1,2
