Belgian wave investigation

1989 to 1990

Belgium · 1989 to 1990 · Report released

On the night of 30 to 31 March 1990, two Belgian Air Force F-16s were sent to investigate radar contacts, but neither pilot reported visual confirmation of the object. The Air Force released a report; later analysis put the radar locks down to interference and the jets locking on to each other. 1
