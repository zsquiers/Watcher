Project Blue Book

1952 to 1969

United States · 1952 to 1969 · Closed 1969

Grew out of Project Sign (1948) and Project Grudge (1949), and took the name Blue Book in 1952. It logged 12,618 reports, 701 of which stayed unidentified. 1,2

Closed in 1969 after the University of Colorado's Condon Report concluded further study was unlikely to be productive. 1,3
