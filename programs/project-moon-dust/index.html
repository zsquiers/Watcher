Project Moon Dust

Recovery of fallen space hardware

United States · Recovery of fallen space hardware · Cold War

A covert Air Force project during the Cold War, run from the Air Force Missile Development Center at Holloman AFB, to recover objects and debris from spacecraft that survived re-entry and to exploit any Soviet hardware found. A related effort was called Project Blue Fly. 1

Files later released under freedom of information requests, including State Department cables, show how it worked. Because it collected things that fell from the sky, it became a frequent subject of UFO crash-retrieval rumors, though the released records concern space debris. 2
