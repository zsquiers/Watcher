Ghost rocket committee

1946

Sweden · 1946 · Ended 1946

Investigated about 2,000 reports of rocket-like objects over Sweden in 1946. Many were probably caused by meteors, but the Swedish Defense Staff publicly stated that some clear observations could not be explained. 1
