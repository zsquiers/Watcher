5412 Committee

Special Group, 1955 to 1970s

United States · Special Group, 1955 to 1970s · 1955, renamed 1964 and 1970

Created in December 1955 under President Eisenhower by National Security Council directive 5412/2. A small panel of senior officials, usually the President's representative and deputies from State and Defense, met with the CIA director to approve or reject covert operations. 1

Also called the Special Group, it was renamed the 303 Committee in 1964 and the 40 Committee in 1970. Its existence was confirmed by the Church Committee in the 1970s. 1,2

Some UFO researchers have suggested a secret group like this could have overseen UFO matters, and the disputed Majestic 12 papers are sometimes tied to it. No documents link the 5412 Committee to UFOs.
