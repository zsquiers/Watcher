Operation Mockingbird

Media influence

United States · Media influence · 1963 wiretap confirmed, wider claims debated

The CIA's Family Jewels documents show Project Mockingbird was a 1963 wiretap of two Washington journalists. 1,2

The Church Committee separately found the CIA had relationships with about 50 U.S. journalists. Whether this was one organized program called Mockingbird is debated. 2,3
