Setka-AN

1978 to about 1991

Soviet Union and Russia · 1978 to about 1991 · Disbanded after 1991

The Academy of Sciences' "Net" group, run in parallel with the military's Setka-MO and managed jointly with the Defense Ministry. It was disbanded with its twin after 1991. 1
