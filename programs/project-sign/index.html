Project Sign

1948 to 1949

United States · 1948 to 1949 · Became Grudge 1949

The Air Force's first UFO study, set up in 1948 by General Nathan Twining and first named Project Saucer. It was run by the Air Materiel Command's intelligence staff at Wright-Patterson Field in Ohio. 1

Edward Ruppelt later wrote that its staff drafted a Top Secret Estimate of the Situation arguing that some UFOs were interplanetary craft, and that Air Force Chief of Staff Hoyt Vandenberg rejected it for lack of proof; no copy has ever surfaced. On February 11, 1949, it was renamed Project Grudge. A report on its work that April found almost all sightings had ordinary explanations, but recommended investigating every one. 1,2
