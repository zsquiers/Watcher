GEIPAN

GEPAN 1977, SEPRA 1988, GEIPAN 2005

France · GEPAN 1977, SEPRA 1988, GEIPAN 2005 · Active, 1977 to now

Founded in 1977 as GEPAN, renamed SEPRA, and renamed GEIPAN in 2005. It investigates reports, many passed on by the Gendarmerie, publishes its case files online, and today classes about 3% of sightings as unidentified. 1
