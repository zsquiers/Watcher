Project Stork

Battelle, 1950s

United States · Battelle, 1950s · Ended

A classified Air Force contract with the Battelle Memorial Institute in Columbus, Ohio, run for the Air Technical Intelligence Center at Wright-Patterson. Jennie Zeidman, who worked on it at Battelle, wrote that its mission was to assess the Soviet Union's capability for technological warfare. 1,2

Its team also analyzed the Air Force's UFO reports, producing Project Blue Book Special Report No. 14. Battelle's UFO work was kept quiet; Zeidman wrote that one absolutely did not mention Battelle in connection with UFOs. 2,3

On American Alchemy, guests have described Stork as a root of later crash-retrieval programs; no document shows that. 4
