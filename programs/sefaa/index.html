SEFAA

Formerly CEFAA, 1997 to now

Chile · Formerly CEFAA, 1997 to now · Active, 1997 to now

Created in 1997 as CEFAA and now called SEFAA, it studies anomalous aerial phenomena in Chilean airspace for flight safety. In 2018 it said only 5% of its cases had been declared unexplained. 1,2,3
