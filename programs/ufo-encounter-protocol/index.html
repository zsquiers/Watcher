UFO encounter protocol

2020

Japan · 2020 · Issued 2020

In 2020 Defense Minister Taro Kono unveiled new protocols telling personnel to document any such phenomena and record it on camera for the necessary analysis. The ministry said there were no known cases of its personnel encountering UFOs. 1,2
