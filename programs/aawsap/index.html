AAWSAP

2008 to 2010

United States · 2008 to 2010 · Closed 2010

Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications Program. A roughly $22 million DIA contract with Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space Studies (BAASS), championed by Senator Harry Reid. 1

Produced 38 technical research papers on advanced aerospace concepts. 1

The contractor's owner, Robert Bigelow, also owned Skinwalker Ranch in Utah, which became the program's main field research site. That's why AAWSAP links to the ranch on the map. 1
