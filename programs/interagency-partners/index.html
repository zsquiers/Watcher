Interagency partners

ODNI, DOE, FBI

United States · ODNI, DOE, FBI

AARO coordinates directly with agencies outside the Pentagon, including the Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI), the Department of Energy, and the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI), as part of a unified government reporting framework. 1

It produces the annual consolidated UAP reports to Congress together with ODNI, and shares cases with partner agencies so reports from different sources end up in one place. 2
