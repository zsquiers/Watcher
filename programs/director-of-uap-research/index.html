Director of UAP research

Mark McInerney, named 2023

United States · Mark McInerney, named 2023

NASA created this post in September 2023, when its independent study team released its report. The director leads NASA's UAP work and helps build a central database for UAP data. 1

The first director, Mark McInerney, was previously NASA's liaison to the Department of Defense on UAP. 1
