Operation Paperclip

1945 to 1959

United States · 1945 to 1959 · 1945 to 1959

Run by the Joint Intelligence Objectives Agency, it brought more than 1,600 German scientists and engineers to the U.S., some with Nazi pasts. 1

Wernher von Braun's rocket team worked at Fort Bliss, then Redstone Arsenal in Huntsville, and went on to build the Saturn V. 1
