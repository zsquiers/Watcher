RAND

RAND Corporation

United States · RAND Corporation

A nonprofit research organization that began in 1946 as Project RAND, an Air Force partnership with Douglas Aircraft, and became independent in 1948. It runs federally funded research centers for the Air Force, the Army, and the Defense Department. 1

Its 2023 study Not the X-Files mapped 101,151 public UAP reports from 1998 to 2022 and found they were more common within about 30 km of military operations areas, the airspace used for military flight training. It suggested many such reports may be authorized military aircraft and recommended outreach to people living nearby. Its researchers have also argued that antigovernment rhetoric harms serious UAP research. 2,3,4
