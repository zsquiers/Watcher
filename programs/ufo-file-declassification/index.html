UFO file declassification

1990s

Spain · 1990s · Files released

The Air Force declassified 80 reports of strange phenomena gathered between 1962 and 1995, including its 1994 report on the 1979 Manises case, declassified in 1996. The files were digitized in 2016. 1,2
