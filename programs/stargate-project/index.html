Stargate Project

1978 to 1995

United States · 1978 to 1995 · 1978 to 1995

Army, DIA, and CIA research into remote viewing, or psychic spying, which began at SRI International in 1972 under earlier names like Gondola Wish and Grill Flame. 1

A 1995 CIA-sponsored review concluded it never produced useful intelligence, and the program was closed. 1
