CRIDOVNI

1979 to now

Uruguay · 1979 to now · Active, 1979 to now

The Commission for the Reception and Investigation of Complaints of Unidentified Flying Objects, created in 1979. It has no budget of its own; about 3% of its cases have no conclusive explanation. 1,2
