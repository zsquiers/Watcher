Operation Northwoods

1962 proposal

United States · 1962 proposal · Proposed 1962, rejected

A Joint Chiefs plan for staged attacks that would be blamed on Cuba to justify an invasion. The Kennedy administration rejected it. 1,2

The documents were declassified in 1997. 2
