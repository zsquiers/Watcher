Sky Canada Project

Office of the Chief Science Advisor, 2022 to 2025

Canada · Office of the Chief Science Advisor, 2022 to 2025 · Reported 2025

A review launched in the fall of 2022 by the Office of the Chief Science Advisor of Canada, Mona Nemer, of how reports of unidentified aerial phenomena from the public are handled. It consulted federal departments, police, the Canadian Space Agency, NAV CANADA and other countries' programs, and commissioned a public survey. 1,2

Its 2025 report found that Canadians report an estimated 600 to 1,000 sightings a year, that one in four survey respondents said they had seen something unexplained, and that reports were scattered across many agencies with few investigated. It made recommendations to improve transparency and scientific study, and found that most survey respondents supported a federal government service to collect reports and publish findings. 2
