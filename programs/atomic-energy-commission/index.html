Atomic Energy Commission

1947 to 1974, now DOE

United States · 1947 to 1974, now DOE

Took over the Manhattan Project's labs and sites in 1947. Its duties later passed to the Department of Energy, which still runs Los Alamos, Oak Ridge, and Hanford. 1
