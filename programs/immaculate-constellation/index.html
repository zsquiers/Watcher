Immaculate Constellation

Alleged UAP data program

United States · Alleged UAP data program · Alleged; Pentagon denies it

Described in a 12-page report that journalist Michael Shellenberger gave Congress, and that Rep. Nancy Mace entered into the record at a House Oversight hearing on November 13, 2024. The report claims an unacknowledged special access program has collected high-quality UAP images and sensor data from military and intelligence sources, hidden from Congress. 1,2

The report's author was later identified as Matthew Brown, a former Pentagon official, who has since spoken publicly. The Pentagon denies the program exists.
