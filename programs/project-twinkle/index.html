Project Twinkle

1949 to 1951

United States · 1949 to 1951 · Closed 1951

Set up in 1949 by the Air Force's Cambridge research laboratories, after a February 1949 conference at Los Alamos, to photograph and measure the green fireballs seen over New Mexico's nuclear sites. 1,2,3

It never got the equipment it planned. Edward Ruppelt wrote that only one of three cameras was available and that nothing was photographed. Its November 1951 final report called the results essentially negative and recommended no further spending. 1,2
