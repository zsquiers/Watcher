MKUltra

1953 to 1973

United States · 1953 to 1973 · 1953 to 1973

The CIA tested LSD, hypnosis, and other mind-control methods, often on people who didn't know or hadn't consented. CIA director Allen Dulles ordered it on April 13, 1953. 1

Most records were destroyed in 1973, but about 20,000 surviving documents were found in 1977 and examined in Senate hearings that year. 1
