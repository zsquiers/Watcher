The UFO desk

Secretariat (Air Staff) 2a, to 2009

United Kingdom · Secretariat (Air Staff) 2a, to 2009 · Closed 2009

The office better known as the UFO desk logged reports from the public to see if they had any defence significance. Nick Pope, who ran it from 1991 to 1994, later became a well-known commentator. 1

The ministry closed the desk and its UFO hotline in December 2009, saying the work served no defence purpose and produced no valuable defence output. 2
