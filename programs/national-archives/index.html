UAP Records Collection

United States · UAP Records Collection

The federal government's record keeper. The FY2024 defense bill, a pared-down version of the UAP Disclosure Act, created a UAP Records Collection here, barred agencies from destroying UAP records, and set a 25-year limit on keeping them secret. Agencies were required to transfer their UAP records. 1,2
