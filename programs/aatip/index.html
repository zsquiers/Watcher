AATIP

About 2007 to 2012

United States · About 2007 to 2012 · Closed 2012 (disputed)

Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program, made public by The New York Times in December 2017. The name is often used interchangeably with AAWSAP. 1,2

The Pentagon says funding ended in 2012. Luis Elizondo says he ran it until he resigned in 2017, which the DoD disputes. 2,3
