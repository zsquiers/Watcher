Robertson Panel

1953

United States · 1953 · 1953

A panel of scientists convened by the CIA to review UFO reports. It concluded they posed no direct threat and recommended public education to reduce interest in the subject. 1

Its report shaped how Project Blue Book handled cases for years afterward. 1,2
