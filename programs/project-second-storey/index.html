Project Second Storey

1952 to 1954

Canada · 1952 to 1954 · Dissolved 1954

A committee set up on 22 April 1952 to collect and catalog sighting reports and settle the government's position. It concluded that UFOs, if real, were not amenable to scientific inquiry, and was dissolved in 1954. 1,2
