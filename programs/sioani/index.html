SIOANI

1969 to 1972

Brazil · 1969 to 1972 · Ended 1972

The Sistema de Investigação de Objetos Aéreos Não Identificados, a regional investigation system of the Air Force's 4th Air Zone in São Paulo, coordinated by Major Gilberto Zani de Mello. Some English sources date it to 1966. 1
