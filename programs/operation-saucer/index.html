Operation Saucer

Operação Prato, 1977 to 1978

Brazil · Operação Prato, 1977 to 1978 · Ended 1978

The first Air Force operation conducted only to investigate UFOs, into lights reported on the island of Colares in Pará. It was carried out between 1977 and 1978 and closed after finding no unusual phenomena; its files were later released. 1
