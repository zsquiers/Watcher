Project Mogul

1947 to 1949

United States · 1947 to 1949 · 1947 to 1949

Top-secret trains of balloons carrying microphones to listen for Soviet nuclear tests, launched from Alamogordo, New Mexico. 1

The Air Force's 1994 report concluded a Mogul flight was the most likely source of the Roswell debris. 1,2
