Office of Strategic Services

United States · Office of Strategic Services · 1942 to 1945

America's wartime intelligence and sabotage agency, led by William "Wild Bill" Donovan. It was built with heavy help from British Security Coordination and William Stephenson. 1

Disbanded in 1945, its people and methods passed to the CIA when it was created in 1947. Its officers included Paul Mellon, heir to the Mellon banking fortune, who served in its Morale Operations Branch. 1,2,3
