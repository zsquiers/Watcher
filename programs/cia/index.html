CIA

Central Intelligence Agency

United States · Central Intelligence Agency

Created by the National Security Act of 1947. Many of its Cold War programs came to light in the 1970s through the Church Committee, the Rockefeller Commission, and the CIA's own internal Family Jewels report. 1,2,3

Its first director, from 1947 to 1950, was Navy admiral Roscoe Hillenkoetter. After retiring he sat on the board of governors of NICAP, the civilian UFO group, from about 1957 to 1962. 4
