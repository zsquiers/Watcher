Project Condign

1997 to 2000

United Kingdom · 1997 to 2000 · Report released 2006

A secret Defence Intelligence Staff study of about 10,000 British reports, titled Unidentified Aerial Phenomena in the UK Air Defence Region. It found no evidence that the objects were hostile or under any type of control, and put the unexplained cases down mainly to poorly understood natural effects it called Buoyant Plasma Formation. 1

The report was released into the public domain on 15 May 2006 after a Freedom of Information request. 1
