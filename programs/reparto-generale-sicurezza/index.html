Reparto Generale Sicurezza

UFO reporting, 1978 to now

Italy · UFO reporting, 1978 to now · Active

Named by Prime Minister Giulio Andreotti after the 1978 wave of sightings as the official body to collect, verify and monitor UFO reports, to guarantee flight and national safety. It publishes the reports it catalogs. 1,2
