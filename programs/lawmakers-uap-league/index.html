Lawmakers' UAP league

2024 to now

Japan · 2024 to now · Active, 2024 to now

A cross-party league founded on 6 June 2024 to study unidentified anomalous phenomena as a security question, chaired by former defense minister Yasukazu Hamada, with former defence ministers among its 80-plus members. In May 2026 it asked the Cabinet Secretariat to set up a central command for UAP response. 1,2,3
