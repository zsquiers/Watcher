PURSUE

Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters

United States · Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters · Public releases since May 2026

The effort behind WAR.GOV/UFO, the Department of War's public archive of declassified UAP files, launched on May 8, 2026 after a presidential directive earlier that year. 1

It has posted batches of records roughly monthly, including military videos, Apollo-era documents, and FBI witness interviews. Critics note that many files lack the context needed to judge them. 2
