Congress

Legislative branch

United States · Legislative branch

Creates and funds UAP offices, mainly through the annual National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA) and intelligence authorization acts. 1

The FY2022 NDAA required a dedicated UAP office, and the FY2023 NDAA wrote AARO and its duties into law, including a review of the government's historical UAP record. 1,2
