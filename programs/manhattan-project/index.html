Manhattan Project

1942 to 1946

United States · 1942 to 1946 · 1942 to 1946

The secret effort to build the atomic bomb, led by Gen. Leslie Groves, with J. Robert Oppenheimer directing the Los Alamos lab. More than 100,000 people worked on it, most without knowing its purpose. 1

It produced the Trinity test in July 1945 and the bombs dropped on Hiroshima and Nagasaki. Its sites are pinned on the map. 1
