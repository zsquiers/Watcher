Ben Rich

Skunk Works director, 1925 to 1995

Also known as Benjamin R. Rich

An engineer who succeeded founder Kelly Johnson as head of Lockheed's Skunk Works from 1975 to 1991. Often called the father of stealth, he oversaw the F-117 stealth fighter, and he later wrote the memoir Skunk Works with Leo Janos. 1,2

UFO writers often quote him as saying in a 1993 talk at UCLA that the U.S. already had the technology "to take E.T. home." Aerospace historian Peter Merlin found that the line was a closing joke Rich had used in talks since at least 1983, and no recording of the UCLA talk is known. A longer quote attributed to him, saying star-travel technologies are "locked up in black projects," has no known source. 3
