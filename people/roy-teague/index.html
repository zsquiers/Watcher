Roy Teague

British police inspector and author

Also known as Inspector Roy Teague

A serving West Midlands Police inspector, with nearly 27 years in policing after six years in the British Army, including service in Northern Ireland and Bosnia. He has spent most of his police career on response, proactive and firearms units. 1,2,3

He says a documentary on Robert Hastings' UFOs and Nukes research in 2016 led him to appeal for police witnesses. Over about five years some 250 serving and retired officers contacted him, a figure he now puts at more than 300, and he collected the accounts in Close Encounters of the Police Kind (2026). He has started a YouTube channel, Policing the Unexplained, and says he now believes beyond reasonable doubt that a non-human intelligence is visiting Earth. 2,3
