Billy Meier

Swiss contactee, born 1937

Also known as Eduard Albert Meier

A Swiss farmer, born in Bülach, who left school before sixth grade, served time for theft and forgery as a teenager, briefly joined the French Foreign Legion, and lost his left arm in a bus accident in Turkey in 1965. 1

He says he has been in contact since childhood with human-looking extraterrestrials he calls the Plejaren, and that on January 28, 1975, he met a Plejaren woman named Semjase. He produced hundreds of photos and films of their "beamships," and founded FIGU, a group built on his contacts and teachings. He also claims to be the seventh reincarnation of a line of prophets that includes Jesus and Muhammad, and his prophecies repeatedly blame Jews for future atrocities. 1

One of his photos became the background of the "I want to believe" poster in The X-Files. 1
