Jeffrey Kripal

Religion scholar at Rice University

Also known as Jeffrey J. Kripal

A historian of religion who holds the J. Newton Rayzor Chair in Philosophy and Religious Thought at Rice University in Houston, where he founded the Archives of the Impossible in 2014. 1

His books, including Authors of the Impossible, treat paranormal and anomalous experiences as worth serious study rather than dismissal. He co-wrote The Super Natural (2016) with Whitley Strieber and appears in the documentary Love & Saucers. 1,2
