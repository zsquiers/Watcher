Clas Svahn

Swedish journalist and archivist

A Swedish journalist who chairs the Archives for the Unexplained in Norrköping, one of the world's largest collections of UFO and paranormal material. A review of his book Files of the Unexplained describes him as neither a debunker nor a naive believer. 1,2
