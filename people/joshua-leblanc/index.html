Joshua LeBlanc

NASA Marshall engineer, died 2025

An engineer at NASA's Marshall Space Flight Center in Huntsville, described in different reports as a nuclear or an electrical engineer. On July 22, 2025, hours after his family reported him missing, he died at 29 when his car left a road in Walker County, Alabama, struck a guardrail and trees, and caught fire. State police investigated the crash, and no evidence of foul play has been reported. He was added to the lists in April 2026 after Fox News covered his death. He is one of three Huntsville-area names on the lists, with Ning Li and Amy Eskridge. 1,2,3
