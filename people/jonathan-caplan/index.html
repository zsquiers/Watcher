Jonathan Caplan

British barrister and UFO author

Also known as Jonathan Caplan KC

A London barrister who was called to the Bar by Gray's Inn in 1973 and took silk in 1991. From 5 Paper Buildings he has worked on major fraud and disaster cases, including the Guinness appeal for Ernest Saunders and the Southall rail crash inquiry, and he represented Associated Newspapers at the Leveson Inquiry. 1

He has followed UFO reports since the early 1970s, and by the late 1970s he was listed as a consultant to the journal Flying Saucer Review. His book Not For Disclosure, published by Century in June 2026, became a Sunday Times bestseller. On Sky News that September he said there was "no question" aliens had visited Earth and that Congress had been misled for 80 years. None of his allegations has been independently verified. 1,2
