Greys

Reported beings: small, grey and large-eyed

Also known as Grey aliens, Zeta Reticulans, Roswell Greys

The beings most often described in abduction stories: small, hairless, grey-skinned humanoids with oversized heads and large black almond-shaped eyes. Betty and Barney Hill's claim that they were abducted in 1961, reported in newspapers in 1965, made the image famous. In 1969 schoolteacher Marjorie Fish linked them to the star system Zeta Reticuli by interpreting a star map Betty Hill drew under hypnosis. 1

From the early 1980s, Greys were tied to the Roswell story, and Whitley Strieber's 1987 bestseller Communion put one on its cover. Ufologist Jenny Randles reported in 1992 that Greys featured in 73% of alleged encounters in the United States, against about 48% in continental Europe and 12% in the UK. 1

UNVERIFIED: no physical evidence of such beings exists. Similar figures appear in earlier fiction, such as H. G. Wells's "The Man of the Year Million" (1893), and Martin Kottmeyer argued that Barney Hill's memories under hypnosis may have drawn on an Outer Limits episode broadcast 12 days before his first session. 1
