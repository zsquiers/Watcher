Harley Rutledge

Physicist, Project Identification

Also known as Dr. Harley D. Rutledge

A physicist at Southeast Missouri State University who from 1973 led Project Identification, an instrumented field study of lights reported around Piedmont, Missouri, described in his 1981 book Project Identification: The First Scientific Study of UFO Phenomena. 1

He concluded that many sightings could be explained as aircraft or atmospheric effects and did not accept that the rest were alien craft. 1
