Jake Barber

Former Air Force helicopter pilot

An Air Force veteran and contract helicopter pilot who said in a January 2025 NewsNation special with Ross Coulthart that he flew missions for a secret program recovering crashed craft, including a white, egg-shaped object he believes is non-human. 1

NewsNation aired a short clip of a smooth egg-shaped object slung beneath a helicopter and said three veterans backed his account. The Defense Department said it was looking into his claims. No independent evidence has confirmed what the object was. 1,2
