Steve Aspin

Experiencer and abduction researcher

He says he has had unusual experiences all his life, including coming across a mutilated cow in Ireland in 1970 and seeing a UFO in 1972, at 16, after a stretch of missing time. He spent some 15 years researching abductions and met researchers including Budd Hopkins and David Jacobs. 1

His self-published book Out of Time argues that abductions run in families across generations and that the program almost certainly dates back to the 1890s. Abduction researchers such as Jacobs and Robert Hastings praised it, but it has had little independent review, and its evidence rests on personal accounts. 1
