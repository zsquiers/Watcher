Nuno Loureiro

MIT plasma physicist, 1977 to 2025

Also known as Nuno Filipe Gomes Loureiro

A Portuguese plasma physicist who directed MIT's Plasma Science and Fusion Center. He was shot in the foyer of his apartment building in Brookline, Massachusetts, on December 15, 2025, and died the next day. The gunman, a former classmate who also carried out the Brown University shooting, later killed himself; in April 2026 the FBI and federal prosecutors found he acted alone, with "no nexus to terrorism." He and Jason Thomas are the two Massachusetts names on the lists. 1,2,3
