Peter Sturrock

Stanford physicist, 1924 to 2024

Also known as Peter A. Sturrock

A Stanford professor of applied physics who became interested in UFOs after hiring Jacques Vallée in the early 1970s and reading the Condon Report, which he concluded showed something needing study. 1

His surveys of aerospace engineers and astronomers in the 1970s found most favored continued study, and in 1982 he helped found the Society for Scientific Exploration, which critics say caters to pseudoscience. 1
