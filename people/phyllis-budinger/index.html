Phyllis Budinger

Chemist, UFO trace analyst

Also known as Phyllis A. Budinger

An analytical chemist who spent 35 years in industry, heading a spectroscopy group at Standard Oil of Ohio, later BP/Amoco, before retiring in 1999. In 2000 she founded Frontier Analysis, a laboratory that analyzes physical material from UFO reports. 1

Her reports cover soil from the Delphos ring in Kansas, residue left on a car at Mt. Clemens, Michigan, a burning fragment that fell at Claymont, Delaware, and samples tied to abduction claims and crop circles. 1,2,3
