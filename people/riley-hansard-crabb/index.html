Riley Hansard Crabb

Director of Borderland Sciences, 1912 to 1994

Also known as Riley Crabb

The second and longest-serving director of the Borderland Sciences Research Foundation, who took it over from Meade Layne in 1959 and ran it from Vista, California. From the early 1960s to the mid-1980s he edited its Journal of Borderland Research, 179 issues and nearly 6,000 pages over 25 years. 1,2,3,4
