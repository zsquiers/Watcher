Ingrid Lane

Neuroscientist, missing since 2023

Also known as Ingrid Coleen Lane

A neuroscientist and bioengineer who had worked with the Mind Research Network at the University of New Mexico. On October 15, 2023, at 37, after a meditation retreat at the Bodhi Manda Zen Center in Jemez Springs, she was last seen on Forest Road 144 in the Jemez Mountains, where she declined a ride and said she planned to hike farther up the road. Her car, with a flat tire and a broken rear window, was found there days later. Searches with dogs and drones found no trace of her apart from an item of hers that her mother and sister found on the road about a mile north of the car, and the sheriff's office said it did not suspect foul play. She had not been found as of November 2026. 1,2,3,4,5

Friends said she was starting work tied to Los Alamos National Laboratory, which LA Magazine could not verify. The magazine has grouped her disappearance with the New Mexico cases in the missing-scientists theory, those of Anthony Chavez, Melissa Casias, Steven Garcia and William Neil McCasland, though she is not on the most-cited lists and no link has been established. 1,5
