Robert Salas

Former Air Force missile officer

Also known as Bob Salas

A former Air Force missile launch officer at Malmstrom Air Force Base in Montana, later an aerospace engineer. 1

He says that in March 1967 a glowing red object hovered over the front gate of his launch site just as the Minuteman missiles in his care went offline. He wrote Faded Giant (2005) about it, and co-hosted the 2010 National Press Club briefing with Robert Hastings. The Air Force's investigation of the 1967 failures found no link to UFOs. 1,2
