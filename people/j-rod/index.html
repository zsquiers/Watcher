J-Rod

Reported being: Dan Burisch's S-4 alien

A being that Dan Burisch, also known as Dan Crain, says he worked with as a microbiologist on a secret project at S-4, the alleged site near Area 51 that Bob Lazar also described. Burisch told the story on the Coast to Coast AM radio show in 2004. 1

UNVERIFIED: Burisch has offered no evidence for the being or the project. The "J-Rod photo" posted after the interview shows a special-effects figure that had already appeared on an effects website, FXmasters, by 2000. 1
