Monica Reza

Metallurgist, missing since 2025

Also known as Monica Jacinto Reza

A metallurgist and materials engineer who in the mid-1990s co-invented Mondaloy, a nickel-based superalloy for rocket engines, at Rocketdyne, and later worked at JPL. On June 22, 2025, she disappeared while hiking near Mount Waterman in the Angeles National Forest, after friends lost sight of her on a trail along a cliff's edge. The sheriff's department found no clear sign of foul play, and she had not been found as of November 2026. She is one of four people tied to Caltech and JPL on the lists, with Michael David Hicks, Frank Maiwald and Carl Grillmair. 1,2,3,4
