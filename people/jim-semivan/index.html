Jim Semivan

Former CIA officer, To The Stars co-founder

Also known as James Semivan

A retired CIA operations officer who spent 25 years in the Directorate of Operations and was a member of its Senior Intelligence Service. In 2017 he co-founded To The Stars with Tom DeLonge and Harold Puthoff, and he has spoken publicly about his own personal encounters. 1,2
