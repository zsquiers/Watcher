William Neil McCasland

Retired Air Force major general, born 1957

Also known as Neil McCasland

A retired Air Force major general who commanded the Air Force Research Laboratory at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, overseeing a $2.2 billion science and technology portfolio, and earlier the Phillips Research Site at Kirtland Air Force Base in New Mexico. 1

In emails published by WikiLeaks in 2016, Tom DeLonge told Clinton campaign chairman John Podesta he had been working with McCasland for four months, and described him as having been in charge of the laboratory where the Roswell wreckage was supposedly shipped. There is no official record of the arrangement, and McCasland never confirmed or denied it; reporting since describes only a brief association, including consulting on a fiction book. 1,2

He was reported missing from his Albuquerque home on February 27, 2026. The Bernalillo County Sheriff's Office and the FBI searched for him and reported no evidence of foul play. His disappearance set off a theory linking him to other scientists who had died or vanished. 1,2
