Chris Bledsoe

North Carolina UFO experiencer and author

Also known as Christopher Bledsoe

A former home builder from Hope Mills, North Carolina, who says a 2007 encounter on the Cape Fear River healed his Crohn's disease and began years of contact with orbs and beings. He says the experiences are tied to his Christian faith and to certain dates such as Easter. 1,2

He wrote UFO of God (2023) and has been featured in documentaries and podcasts. He says scientists, a general, and members of Congress have visited him, including NASA mission controller Tim Taylor. His claims are unverified, and critics question the circle of officials around him. 1,2
