Erol Faruk

Chemist, Delphos soil analysis

Also known as Dr. Erol A. Faruk

A British organic chemist who, while a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Nottingham, obtained soil from the glowing ring left at Delphos, Kansas, in 1971 through the Center for UFO Studies. 1

He found the ring soil was impregnated with an air-sensitive organic compound able to give off light by oxidative chemiluminescence, and used it to propose how the ring had formed. 1
