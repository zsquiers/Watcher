Dylan Borland

Former Air Force intelligence specialist

A former Air Force geospatial intelligence specialist who served on active duty from 2010 to 2013 and later worked as an analyst for defense contractors. 1

At the September 2025 House hearing he testified that in 2012, at Langley Air Force Base, a silent triangular craft about 100 feet long passed within about 100 feet of him. He said AARO interviewed him in 2023 and dismissed his account, and that he has faced reprisals from government agencies since. His claims have not been independently verified. 1
