Richard Arnowitt

American physicist, 1928 to 2014

A theoretical physicist known for the ADM formalism of general relativity and for work on supergravity, later at Texas A&M University. In 1955 he and Stanley Deser were named by the Herald-Tribune among the physicists studying gravity. 1,2
