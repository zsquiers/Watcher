Kit Green

Physician and former CIA analyst

Also known as Christopher C. Green

A physician and former CIA analyst who served as the agency's contract monitor for the remote viewing experiments at SRI in the 1970s, obtained a temporary security clearance for Jacques Vallée in 1974, and recurs throughout Vallée's journals. 1

For the Pentagon's AAWSAP program he wrote a paper on anomalous effects on human tissue. In 2022 he said the injuries he had studied were caused by human technologies, not UFOs. 2
