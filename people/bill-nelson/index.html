Bill Nelson

NASA administrator, 2021 to 2025

A Florida senator who flew on the Space Shuttle Columbia in January 1986, the second sitting member of Congress to fly in space, and who led NASA from 2021 to 2025. He said he had talked with the Navy pilots and did not know what they had seen, adding, "We hope it's not an adversary here on Earth that has that kind of technology," and that Earth need not be the only home of civilized life. In 2023 he released NASA's UAP study, which found no evidence that UAP have an extraterrestrial origin, and named a director of UAP research. 1,2,3,4
