Reptilians

Reported beings: shape-shifting reptiles

Also known as Lizard people, Reptilian humanoids

Reptile-like humanoids that turn up in a few abduction accounts and, far more often, in conspiracy theories. One of the earliest abduction reports came from Ashland, Nebraska police officer Herbert Schirmer, who under hypnosis recalled being taken aboard a craft in 1967 by beings with a slightly reptilian look; skeptics consider his story a hoax. 1

British conspiracy theorist David Icke made the idea famous with his 1999 book The Biggest Secret, which claims that shape-shifting reptilians from the Alpha Draconis star system secretly rule the world and that many leaders, past and present, are related to them. A 2013 Public Policy Polling survey found that 4% of registered US voters believed his ideas. 1

Critics have noted antisemitic themes in Icke's work, including his naming of the Jewish Rothschild family as reptilians, which they say serves as a code for Jews; Icke denies this. Political scientist Michael Barkun traces the idea back to Robert E. Howard's 1929 story "The Shadow Kingdom" and later occult writings. 1,2

UNVERIFIED: there is no evidence that such beings exist.
