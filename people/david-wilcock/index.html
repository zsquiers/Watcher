David Wilcock

Author and broadcaster, 1973 to 2026

An American writer and YouTube broadcaster whose books included The Source Field Investigations (2012), and who promoted claims of secret space programs on Ancient Aliens and on Gaia's Cosmic Disclosures, co-hosted with Corey Goode. He died on April 20, 2026, near Nederland, Colorado, by suicide in the presence of law enforcement. Rep. Tim Burchett said he did not think the death was "a coincidence," and Wilcock was added to lists in the missing-scientists theory, though he was not a scientist; Nick Pope, who died two weeks earlier, was added the same way. 1,2,3
