Felix Moncla

Air Force pilot, lost over Lake Superior in 1953

Also known as First Lt. Felix Moncla Jr.

A U.S. Air Force pilot who, with radar operator Robert Wilson, was scrambled from Kinross Air Force Base in Michigan on November 23, 1953, in an F-89 Scorpion to intercept an unknown radar target over Lake Superior. On radar the jet's return merged with the target and did not separate again, and neither man was found. 1

The Air Force suggested he may have suffered vertigo and crashed; the case became one of the best-known disappearances in UFO literature. 1
