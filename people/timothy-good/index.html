Timothy Good

British UFO author, born 1942

A London-born violinist and author whose Above Top Secret (1987) argued that UFOs are alien craft and that governments hide the evidence. It helped launch the Majestic 12 papers, some of which he later questioned. 1

A 2007 CIA article named the book as a source of the idea that the agency concealed UFO research, and Tom DeLonge has credited Good's books with opening his eyes. 1
