Deep Prasad

Technology entrepreneur and UFO researcher

Also known as Deeptanshu Prasad

An Indian-born Canadian entrepreneur who studied industrial engineering at the University of Toronto and founded the quantum computing startup ReactiveQ in 2018. He later founded the technology company GenMat. 1

He says the 2017 Pentagon UFO videos first drew him to the subject, and that sometime between 2017 and 2019 he was taken aboard a craft by non-human beings in an experience he compared to a kundalini awakening. In 2019 he said he wanted to find and reverse-engineer a UFO and backed UAP eXpeditions, a nonprofit searching for UAP off Southern California. His account is UNVERIFIED and rests on his own statements. 1
