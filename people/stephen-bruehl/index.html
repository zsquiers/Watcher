Stephen Bruehl

Pain researcher, Vanderbilt University Medical Center

A professor of anesthesiology at Vanderbilt University Medical Center who studies chronic pain. He took up UAP research in his spare time after the New York Times revealed the Pentagon's UAP program in 2017, saying he realized nobody was really studying the subject scientifically. 1

He was lead author of the October 2025 Scientific Reports paper with Beatriz Villarroel, which found that flashes on 1950s Palomar sky survey plates were 45 percent more likely within a day of a nuclear test, and a coauthor of her aligned-flashes paper. The authors call the results preliminary, and other astronomers argue the flashes are flaws in the photographic plates. 1,2,3,4

A 2025 paper he led with colleagues sorted 216 well-documented UAP reports from 1947 to 2016 into seven types by their features, chiefly shape. 1
