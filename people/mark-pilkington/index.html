Mark Pilkington

British writer and publisher

A British writer on fringe culture and the publisher of Strange Attractor Press. His book Mirage Men traced how intelligence agencies used UFO stories as cover and disinformation, and he co-produced the 2013 documentary of the same name. 1,2
