Ron Pandolfi

CIA scientist

Also known as Ronald Pandolfi

A physicist in the CIA's Directorate of Science and Technology, described by journalists and researchers as running an informal "weird desk" that tracked UFO, paranormal, and exotic-technology claims in the 1990s. He was in contact with researchers, TV producers, and Silicon Valley entrepreneur Joseph Firmage's UFO projects. 1

UNVERIFIED: Some secondary sources claim he was "Pelican" in the Aviary group of insiders, and that he talked about an antigravity device. TV producer Robert Kiviat later sued Firmage, Pandolfi, and others over an unpaid antigravity documentary. None of these claims has been confirmed, and the CIA has not commented.
