Felix Ziegel

Soviet astronomer, 1920 to 1988

Also known as Felix Zigel

A Soviet astronomer and Moscow Aviation Institute lecturer, generally regarded as a founder of Russian ufology. In 1967 he helped lead the first officially approved Soviet UFO study group, and in November that year he appealed on Soviet central television for sighting reports, which brought a flood of letters; within months the group's work was abruptly cancelled. In 1976 a press campaign attacked ufology as pseudoscience. He also argued that the Tunguska event was a spacecraft crash. 1
