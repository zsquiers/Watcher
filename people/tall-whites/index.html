Tall Whites

Reported beings: Charles Hall's Nevada visitors

Tall, pale, human-like beings that former Air Force weather observer Charles Hall says he met again and again on the Nellis ranges near Indian Springs, Nevada, in the mid-1960s. He wrote about them in his Millennial Hospitality books, starting in 2002, later adapted as the 2020 film Walking with the Tall Whites. 1

Hall's books are usually grouped with accounts of the Nordics. 1

UNVERIFIED: the account rests on Hall's word alone; no records or other witnesses have confirmed it.
