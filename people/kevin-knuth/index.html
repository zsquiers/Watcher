Kevin Knuth

Physicist and UAP researcher

Also known as Kevin H. Knuth

A physics professor at the University at Albany and former NASA research scientist at Ames Research Center. He co-authored a 2019 study estimating the speeds and accelerations of objects in several well-known UAP cases, and helps lead UAPx, a nonprofit that takes instruments into the field. 1,2

He argues that UAP deserve ordinary scientific study, with instruments and data rather than anecdotes. 1
