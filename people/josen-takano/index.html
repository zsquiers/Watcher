Jōsen Takano

Hakui city official and Buddhist priest, born 1955

Also known as Takano Jōsen, Josen Takano, Jousen Takano, 高野誠鮮

Born in Hakui, Japan, in 1955, he is a Nichiren Buddhist priest who joined Hakui city hall in 1984 and began promoting the town through UFOs after reading of the sōhachibon, a strange fire said in a temple document to fly between the local mountains. In 1990 he staged a symposium on space and UFOs that drew 50,000 visitors, and the national funding that followed built Cosmo Isle Hakui, which he stocked by visiting NASA in person and buying Soviet spacecraft from Russia. 1,2,3,4

Later called a "super civil servant" for reviving a dying farm hamlet, he had its Mikohara rice presented to the Pope in 2005, a story he told in a 2012 book that became the 2015 TBS drama Napoleon no Mura. Under a pen name he co-wrote two books about the 1995 alien autopsy film. He told Salon in 2001 that George Adamski's writing had inspired him, but that "I have never seen a UFO." 1,5,6
