Jenny Randles

British UFO author, born 1951

The British UFO Research Association's director of investigations from 1982 to 1994 and the author of dozens of books. She was one of the first to investigate the Rendlesham Forest case and co-wrote Sky Crash about it. 1

She later concluded that probably no unearthly craft was seen at Rendlesham, only misperceptions of everyday things, and argues that abductions relived under hypnosis are real traumas reshaped by UFO belief. 1
