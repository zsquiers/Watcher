Richard Barth

Former Air Force security policeman

Also known as Richard L. Barth

Assigned to Vandenberg Air Force Base in July 1964 with the 4392nd Combat Defense Squadron, which guarded missile test sites. In 2025 he described to researcher Robert Hastings being taken aboard a craft by non-human beings while guarding a Minuteman site in September 1964. UNVERIFIED: his service record places him at Vandenberg, but no record or witness confirms the encounter. 1,2
