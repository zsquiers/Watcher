Lyn Buchanan

Army remote viewer

Also known as Sgt. Lyn Buchanan

An Army sergeant who served as a remote viewer in the Stargate psychic spying program, trained in controlled remote viewing by Paul H. Smith. He later taught remote viewing to the public and wrote The Seventh Sense. 3

In that book he says his main task during the 1991 Gulf War was to access Saddam Hussein and learn his plans and intentions for the coming day. No record confirming this has been released. 4

In a 2026 podcast interview he said he had viewed three of Pat Price's alleged UFO bases, describing Mount Hayes as an automated listening post and Mount Zeil as a port of entry for friendly visitors, and said he never did the Pyrenees session. Project 8200's published mission list does not name him as a principal viewer. 1,2
