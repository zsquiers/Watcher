Edward Teller

Physicist, 1908 to 2003

The Hungarian-born physicist known as "the father of the hydrogen bomb," who helped found Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory with Ernest O. Lawrence and directed it from 1958 to 1960. 1

In the 1980s he campaigned for what became the Strategic Defense Initiative, urging satellites that would use nuclear explosions to fire X-ray lasers at incoming missiles, and set out his case in Better a Shield than a Sword (1987). He and his associate Lowell Wood were accused of deliberately overselling the X-ray laser, which failed several key tests. 1,2
