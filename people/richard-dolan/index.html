Richard Dolan

Historian and UFO author

Also known as Richard M. Dolan

A historian trained at the University of Rochester whose UFOs and the National Security State (2000) is a detailed chronology of the U.S. military and intelligence response to UFOs from 1941 to 1973, with a foreword by Jacques Vallée. 1

He also retells encounter stories. In one, a retired Army colonel who knew about classified projects was at the Wynn hotel in Las Vegas, around 2009, with his wife and a friend who said she was psychic. The friend pointed out a striking blonde woman dressed in blue and said she was not human. The colonel says he then heard a voice in his head telling him, in effect, "nothing to see here, go about your business," and watched a blond man, also in blue, join her. Dolan presents the pair as Nordics. 2,3

UNVERIFIED: the colonel is not named, and the story reaches us only as Dolan tells it.
