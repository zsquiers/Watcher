Lincoln LaPaz

Astronomer and meteorite expert, 1897 to 1985

Also known as Lincoln La Paz, Dr. La Paz

A University of New Mexico astronomer who founded its Institute of Meteoritics in 1945 and led it until 1966. 1

He investigated the green fireballs for the Air Force from December 1948 and saw one himself. At the 1949 Los Alamos conference he argued they were not meteors, because their paths were too flat, their color too green, and no fragments could be found. He suggested they might be artificial, perhaps Soviet spy devices. 1,2

In 1964 he interviewed the Socorro police officer Lonnie Zamora and vouched for him as a witness. 1
