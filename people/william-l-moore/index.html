William L. Moore

UFO researcher and author

Also known as William Moore, Bill Moore

A former teacher who co-wrote The Roswell Incident (1980) with Charles Berlitz, the book that revived the Roswell story, and was research director of the Aerial Phenomena Research Organization (APRO). 1

With TV producer Jaime Shandera he received the Majestic 12 papers on film in 1984. In 1989 he admitted he had cooperated with Air Force intelligence agents who were feeding disinformation to UFO researchers, and had reported on colleagues, including APRO, in exchange for access. 1
