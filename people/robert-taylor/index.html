Robert Taylor

Scottish forestry worker, Dechmont Woods witness

Also known as Robert (Bob) Taylor

A forestry worker for the Livingston Development Corporation who said that on November 9, 1979, spheres from a dome-shaped object in Dechmont Woods seized him and dragged him toward it before he passed out. Police investigated it as an assault, which makes it one of the few UFO reports ever handled as a crime. 1

He stood by his account for the rest of his life and died in 2007. 1
