Trevor James Constable

Radio officer and 'sky creatures' theorist, 1925 to 2016

Also known as Trevor James

A New Zealand-born radio officer who spent 26 years in the U.S. merchant marine and argued that many UFOs are not craft but living creatures of the upper atmosphere, normally invisible. After reading about radionics and Wilhelm Reich's orgone, he photographed the sky on film with an ultraviolet lens and said the discolored shapes that appeared were amoeba-like animals, which he called critters. He set out the idea in They Live in the Sky (1958) and The Cosmic Pulse of Life (1976), both written as Trevor James. 1

He also co-wrote World War II fighter-pilot histories with Raymond Toliver. In 1991 he promoted rain-making devices for a dam project in Malacca, Malaysia, under a $3.2 million contract that the Malaysian government later investigated. 1
