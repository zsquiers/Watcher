Claude Poher

French engineer, founder of GEPAN

Also known as Dr. Claude Poher

A French space engineer with a doctorate in astrophysics who headed the sounding rocket division and then the scientific systems and projects division of France's space agency, CNES. When CNES created GEPAN, its unit for studying unidentified aerospace phenomena, in May 1977, he became its first director, before leaving on sabbatical at the end of 1978. 1,2

He published statistical studies of patterns in sighting reports. Since 2003 he has promoted a gravitation theory he calls the universons, which is controversial; a 2010 journal article on it was withdrawn. 1,2
