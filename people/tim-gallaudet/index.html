Tim Gallaudet

Retired Navy rear admiral and NOAA head

Also known as Timothy Gallaudet, Rear Admiral Tim Gallaudet

A retired Navy rear admiral and oceanographer who later served as acting administrator of NOAA, the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration. 1

At the November 2024 House hearing he testified that in January 2015 he received a Navy email, with the GoFast video attached, warning of near midair collisions with unidentified objects during a training exercise, and that the email vanished from his account and the other recipients' the next day. No record of the email has been released, and the account rests on his own testimony. 1
