Watchers

Fallen angels of the Book of Enoch

Also known as Grigori, Irin

In the Book of Daniel a "watcher, a holy one" comes down from heaven. The apocryphal Books of Enoch, from the 2nd to 1st centuries BC, tell of 200 watchers led by Samyaza who came to Mount Hermon, took human wives and fathered the Nephilim, savage giants. They taught humans metalworking, sorcery and astronomy before God sent the Flood. 1,2

Some ancient astronaut writers read the story as a historical account of extraterrestrials sent to observe humanity, some of whom broke orders, bred with humans and shared their knowledge. 2

Scholars read the Enoch story as one interpretation of the "sons of God" passage in Genesis 6, and no evidence supports the extraterrestrial reading. 1,2
