Jonathan Weygandt

Marine, Disclosure Project witness

Also known as Lance Cpl. Jonathan Weygandt

A former U.S. Marine who enlisted in 1994 and trained as a Stinger missile gunner. He says that in March 1997 he was sent to Peru with an air defense unit to guard a radar station that tracked drug flights. 1

In an October 2000 interview with Steven Greer, released by the Disclosure Project in 2001, he said that in late March or early April 1997 his unit was sent to secure a crash site and that he found an egg-shaped craft about 20 meters long buried in a cliff, leaking a greenish-purple liquid. He said men in black uniforms without nametags arrested him, and that an Air Force lieutenant colonel who would not identify himself threatened him and made him sign papers saying he had seen nothing. 1

He believes the craft was shot down by a HAWK missile. The briefing document calls him both Jonathan and John Weygandt, and it does not say where in Peru the crash was. 1
