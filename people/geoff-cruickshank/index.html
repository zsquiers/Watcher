Geoff Cruickshank

Australian security consultant

Also known as Geoff Cruikshank

A security consultant based in Darwin, Australia, with a background in technology engineering, who works as an operations technologist in the oil and gas industry and describes himself as a former intelligence analyst. He is a member of the Scientific Coalition for UAP Studies. 1,2

He first set out his theory that the 1962 Bluegill Triple Prime nuclear test knocked down a non-human craft in Reddit posts in late 2022, followed by an August 2024 paper, an appearance with Ross Coulthart on NewsNation, and a December 2024 Liberation Times article. Researcher Douglas Dean Johnson argues the object in the films is the test's own rocket booster. 2,3
