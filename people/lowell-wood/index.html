Lowell Wood

Livermore astrophysicist

An astrophysicist at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory and Edward Teller's longtime associate. In 1981 he and Teller began talks with Ronald Reagan about a space-based X-ray laser, and in 1986 they conceived Brilliant Pebbles, swarms of small orbiting interceptors that became the baseline for SDI's first phase in 1990 before being canceled in 1994. He and Teller kept saying the X-ray laser was proceeding well even after a critical 1985 test showed it was not working as expected. 1,2,3
