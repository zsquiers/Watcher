John Burroughs

Air Force security officer, Rendlesham

One of the airmen who went into Rendlesham Forest in December 1980 to look for lights. In statements at the time he said they followed a light for about two miles before they could see it was coming from a lighthouse; skeptics identify it as the Orford Ness lighthouse. 1
