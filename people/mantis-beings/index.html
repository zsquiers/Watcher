Mantis beings

Reported beings: tall and insect-like

Also known as Praying mantis beings, Insectoid aliens

Beings with an insect-like or praying-mantis look that appear in some abduction accounts, where they are often described as the ones in charge. 1,2

The Trinity crash story told by Reme Baca and Jose Padilla describes small, insect-like creatures moving in the wreckage of a craft in 1945.

UNVERIFIED: these accounts rest on testimony alone. Skeptic Robert Sheaffer has reported on mantis and reptilian beings described at UFO conferences in the Skeptical Inquirer. 2,3
