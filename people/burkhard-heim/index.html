Burkhard Heim

German physicist, 1925 to 2001

A theoretical physicist who proposed a unified field theory, Heim theory, that he claimed could allow hyperspace travel. He lost his hands and most of his sight and hearing in a wartime laboratory explosion. In 1952 he presented a theory of propulsion by "dynamic contrabarie" to the International Astronautical Federation. 1

In 1955 the Glenn L. Martin Company contracted him to assist with a gravity control propulsion project; the Herald-Tribune reported that he was "certain that gravity can be overcome." His theory has received little attention in the scientific literature and is regarded as outside mainstream science. 1,2
