Mick West

Science writer and UFO skeptic

A British-American science writer and former video game programmer who runs Metabunk, a forum for investigating claims. In the UFO field he is best known for frame-by-frame analyses arguing that the Navy's Gimbal and GoFast videos show ordinary objects, with their strange motion produced by the camera system and the jet's movement. 1

His book Escaping the Rabbit Hole (2018) is a guide to talking people out of conspiracy theories with facts and respect. 2
