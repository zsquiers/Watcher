Gerald Ford

House Minority Leader, later President, 1913 to 2006

Also known as Gerald R. Ford

As a Michigan congressman he called for a congressional investigation after the Air Force blamed "swamp gas" for the March 1966 sightings in his state, which led to a House Armed Services Committee hearing that April. 1
