Haim Eshed

Former head of Israel's military space program, born 1933

Also known as Chaim Eshed

A retired brigadier general in Israeli Military Intelligence who ran the Defense Ministry's space programs for nearly 30 years and is often called the father of Israel's space capabilities. In 1979, as a colonel, he proposed that Israel build its own intelligence satellites; he was still space director when the Ofek-7 spy satellite launched in 2007, and he retired in 2011. 1

In December 2020, in an interview with the newspaper Yedioth Ahronoth that coincided with a new book of conversations with him, he said aliens of a "Galactic Federation" had signed agreements with the United States and asked not to be revealed because "humanity is not ready yet." He offered no evidence, and the head of the Israel Space Agency said he had gone "too far." 1,2
