Dorrit Hoffleit

Astronomer, 1907 to 2007

Also known as Ellen Dorrit Hoffleit

An astronomer born in Alabama in 1907 who worked at the Harvard College Observatory, hunting variable stars, for most of three decades before moving to Yale University in 1956, where she later took charge of the Bright Star Catalog. She mentored many young women in astronomy and died in 2007. 1

In her 2002 autobiography, Misfortunes as Blessings in Disguise, she recalled that one of Donald Menzel's first acts as observatory director was asking his secretary to destroy a third of the plates sight unseen, which were lost from the record for good. He was clearing large parts of the collection in the name of saving space. 1,2,3
