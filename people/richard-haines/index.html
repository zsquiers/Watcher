Richard Haines

NASA Ames scientist, NARCAP co-founder

Also known as Dr. Richard F. Haines

A research scientist at NASA Ames Research Center from 1967 who worked on human factors in aviation and space and investigated aviation accidents and incidents for the FAA and the National Transportation Safety Board. 1

He collected more than 3,000 pilot and air traffic controller reports of unexplained sightings, wrote three books on the subject and in 1999 co-founded the National Aviation Reporting Center on Anomalous Phenomena (NARCAP) with Ted Roe as its chief scientist. 1
