Anna Paulina Luna

U.S. representative from Florida, born 1989

An Air Force veteran elected in 2022, who in 2025 was chosen to lead a House Oversight task force on declassifying federal secrets. In September 2025 it held a hearing on UAP transparency and whistleblower protection. 1

In August 2025 she said on a podcast that Congress had seen proof of "interdimensional beings"; no such evidence has been made public. She appears in The Age of Disclosure (2025). 1
