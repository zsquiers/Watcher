Michael Gold

Space lawyer, NASA UAP study member

Also known as Mike Gold

A space lawyer who, as NASA's associate administrator for space policy and partnerships, led the Artemis Accords, and who later became president of Redwire Space. He was appointed to NASA's UAP independent study team in 2022 and testified at the November 2024 House hearing, urging Congress to overcome the stigma around reporting UAP while saying the vast majority are probably drones, experimental aircraft or weather, "but there is a percentage that isn't." 1,2,3
