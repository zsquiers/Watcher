Jason Reza Jorjani

Philosopher and author, born 1981

Also known as Jason Jorjani

An Iranian-American philosopher with a PhD from Stony Brook University, who taught science, technology and society at the New Jersey Institute of Technology. His books argue that psychic phenomena and UFOs deserve serious study; his publisher says Prometheus and Atlas (2016) won the Parapsychological Association's book award. 1,2

In 2016 he became editor of the alt-right publisher Arktos Media, and in January 2017 he co-founded the AltRight Corporation with Richard Spencer, leaving that August. NJIT suspended him in September 2017 after an undercover video in which he predicted that concentration camps would return to Europe; he said his words were taken out of context and were a warning, not an endorsement. 1

He has appeared three times on American Alchemy, arguing that Nazi Germany had a saucer program and discussing Andrija Puharich, MKUltra and faked alien abductions. 3,4
