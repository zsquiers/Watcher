Andrew Basiago

Lawyer and time-travel claimant

Also known as Andrew D. Basiago

A Washington state lawyer who says that as a child in the late 1960s and 1970s he took part in a secret DARPA program called Project Pegasus that used teleportation and time travel, and that in the early 1980s he was teleported to Mars, along with a young Barack Obama. 1,2

UNVERIFIED: no documents or witnesses support the claims. A White House spokesman denied that Obama had been to Mars. Basiago ran a long-shot campaign for president in 2016. 1,2
