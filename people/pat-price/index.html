Pat Price

SRI remote viewer

A former Burbank, California, police officer who took part in the CIA-funded remote viewing experiments at SRI in the 1970s. Working from maps and photographs supplied by the CIA, he claimed to see inside Soviet facilities, and he is best known for sketches of cranes and gantries that appeared to match CIA intelligence photographs. 1,2

In 1973 he made a remote viewing transcript on his own, describing underground UFO bases inside four mountains: Mount Hayes in Alaska, Monte Perdido in the Pyrenees, Mount Inyangani in Zimbabwe and Mount Zeil in Australia. The claim is unverified. 2
