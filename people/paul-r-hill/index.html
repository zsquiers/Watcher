Paul R. Hill

NASA Langley aerodynamicist, 1909 to 1990

An engineer who joined NASA's predecessor, NACA, in 1939 and retired as associate chief of the Applied Materials and Physics Division at NASA Langley Research Center. He worked on aircraft design, ramjets and early space station studies. 1,2

On July 16, 1952, he reported seeing four objects in a V over Chesapeake Bay near Langley to Project Blue Book, which concluded it was airplanes. His analysis of reported UFO performance, Unconventional Flying Objects: A Scientific Analysis, was turned down by publishers in his lifetime and published in 1995. He argued that the objects' behavior fit known physics and favored the extraterrestrial hypothesis. 1,2
