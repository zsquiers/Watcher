Ted Phillips

Physical-trace researcher, 1942 to 2020

Also known as Theodore R. Phillips

A Missouri engineer and photographer who began investigating UFO reports in 1964 and was a research associate of J. Allen Hynek, specializing at his suggestion in physical traces left at reported landing sites. His 1975 catalogue for the Center for UFO Studies grew into files said to hold 3,189 trace cases, though a reviewer judged that many of the cases appear extremely dubious. He died in 2020. Most accounts of his work come from UFO-research sites rather than independent reporting. 1,2,3
