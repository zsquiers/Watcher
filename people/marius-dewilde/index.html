Marius Dewilde

French witness, Quarouble landing

A French metalworker, also described as a railway worker, who lived beside the line at Quarouble near the Belgian border and reported a landed craft and two small helmeted beings there on September 10, 1954, during France's great UFO wave of that autumn. 1,2

He reported a second encounter in October 1954, which ufologists generally treat as a hoax, and his later retellings changed as the case grew famous. 1
