Steve Colbern

Materials scientist, Leir's implant analyst

A materials scientist who worked with podiatrist Roger Leir on the objects Leir removed from people who reported abductions, and who took possession of the last of them, from a patient known as Patient Seventeen, after Leir died in 2014. 1,2

The analysis of that object, which used scanning electron microscopy and elemental analysis, featured in Jeremy Corbell's 2017 documentary Patient Seventeen. 1
