Václav Hlavatý

Czech-American mathematician, 1894 to 1969

A relativity mathematician at Indiana University who corresponded with Einstein and solved equations in Einstein's unified field theory, hailed in 1953 as a major achievement. In 1955 he told the Herald-Tribune that gravity is one aspect of electromagnetism and may eventually be controlled like light and radio waves. 1,2
