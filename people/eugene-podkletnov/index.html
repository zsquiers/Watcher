Eugene Podkletnov

Russian ceramics engineer

Claimed that during a 1992 experiment at Tampere University of Technology in Finland, a rotating superconducting disc reduced the weight of objects above it, by 0.3 percent and later by 2 percent. He withdrew his second paper after it was accepted, and lost his university post. Many attempts to reproduce the effect, including one by NASA's Breakthrough Propulsion Physics Project, found nothing. 1,2,3
