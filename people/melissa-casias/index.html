Melissa Casias

Los Alamos lab employee, missing 2025

An administrative assistant at Los Alamos National Laboratory who went missing on June 26, 2025, at 53, after being seen walking along a highway near Taos. State police said it appeared she may have left of her own volition. Her remains were found in Carson National Forest and identified on May 31, 2026, with a handgun nearby; the manner of death was listed as undetermined. Her niece said she had seen no evidence linking her to the other cases. She is one of four New Mexico cases on the lists, with Anthony Chavez, Steven Garcia and William Neil McCasland. 1,2,3,4,5
