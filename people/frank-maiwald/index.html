Frank Maiwald

JPL engineer, died 2024

Also known as Frank Werner Maiwald

An engineer at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory who worked on Earth-observing instruments, including the imaging spectrometer for the Surface Biology and Geology mission. He died in Los Angeles on July 4, 2024, at 61; his obituary gave no cause of death. He is one of four people tied to Caltech and JPL on the lists, with Michael David Hicks, Monica Reza and Carl Grillmair. 1,2
