Bryce DeWitt

American physicist, 1923 to 2004

A pioneer of quantum gravity who formulated the Wheeler-DeWitt equation. The winner of a Babson Gravity Research Foundation essay prize, he was offered in 1955 the directorship of a new gravity research institute at the University of North Carolina, where he taught from 1956 and organized the 1957 Chapel Hill Conference. 1,2
