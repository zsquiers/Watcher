Betty Andreasson

Abduction claimant

A Massachusetts woman who said that on January 25, 1967, beings about three feet tall came through her kitchen wall in South Ashburnham, took her aboard a craft and showed her a giant phoenix. She recalled it under hypnosis arranged by J. Allen Hynek's Center for UFO Studies after she wrote to Hynek in 1975. 1

Raymond Fowler's books, beginning with The Andreasson Affair (1979), made it one of the best-known abduction stories. A Kirkus reviewer found the book crudely built, and said even her biographer would not claim it was all true. 1
