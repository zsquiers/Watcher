Louie A. Galloway

Physicist, Haynesville witness

Also known as Dr. Louie A. Galloway

A nuclear physicist and professor at Centenary College in Shreveport, Louisiana, who saw an intensely bright light in the woods near Haynesville while driving with his family on December 30, 1966. He reported it to the Air Force's Project Blue Book in early 1967, and the case was taken up by the Condon study. 1

He went back to the area several times, estimating from his own measurements that the source had put out 400 to 1,200 megawatts. After the Condon report he and colleagues found the clearing where the light had been, with pine bark blackened on the side facing it, and kept bark samples that were analyzed in France decades later and found to hold cesium-137. 1
