Karl Nell

Retired Army colonel

Also known as Col. Karl Nell

A retired Army colonel who, according to journalist Leslie Kean, also worked on the Pentagon's UAP Task Force with David Grusch. Kean has said she vetted Grusch partly by interviewing Nell. 1

Nell told The Debrief that Grusch's claims of a secret program recovering and reverse-engineering craft of non-human origin were fundamentally correct. He has offered no documents of his own, and his Task Force role can't be confirmed from public records. 1
