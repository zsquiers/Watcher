Skip Atwater

Army remote viewing officer

Also known as F. Holmes Atwater, Frederick H. Atwater

A retired Army officer who was the Operations and Training Officer for the Army's remote viewing program, recruiting and training its viewers. He later became president of The Monroe Institute and has served as president of the International Remote Viewing Association. 1,2

Hal Puthoff told him about Pat Price's 1973 transcript on four alleged UFO bases, and he turned them into blind training targets in Project 8200. The results were never reported up the chain of command; he published the transcripts in 2024 through Center Lane Books. He also monitored Joseph McMoneagle's 1984 Mars session. 1,3,4
