Karl Wolfe

Air Force technician, died 2018

Also known as Sgt. Karl Wolfe

A former Air Force sergeant who enlisted in 1964 and says that in the mid-1960s he held a top secret clearance and worked as an electronics technician in photo surveillance labs for Tactical Air Command at Langley Air Force Base, Virginia. 1,2

He came forward in 2001 as one of the witnesses presented by Steven Greer's Disclosure Project. He said that while fixing equipment at a facility on the base that processed imagery from the first Lunar Orbiter mission, another airman told him, "we've discovered a base on the back side of the moon," and showed him a photo mosaic with towers, spherical buildings and what looked like very large radar dishes. 1,3,4

He died in October 2018, at 74, after being struck by a tractor-trailer while cycling in Lansing, New York. 2,3
