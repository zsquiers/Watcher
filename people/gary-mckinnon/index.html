Gary McKinnon

British hacker

Also known as Gary McKinnon, Solo

A Scottish systems administrator who, under the name "Solo," broke into more than 90 U.S. military and NASA computers in 2001 and 2002. He says he was looking for evidence that the government was hiding UFO information and free-energy technology. 1

U.S. prosecutors called it the biggest military computer hack ever. After a ten-year fight, Home Secretary Theresa May blocked his extradition in 2012 on health grounds. None of the evidence he describes finding has been produced. 2
