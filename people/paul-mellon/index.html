Paul Mellon

Philanthropist and OSS officer, 1907 to 1999

Heir to the Mellon banking fortune and son of Treasury Secretary Andrew W. Mellon. In World War II he served with the Office of Strategic Services' Morale Operations Branch in Europe, rising to the rank of major. 1

He and his first wife, Mary Conover Mellon, funded the Bollingen Foundation, set up in 1945, named after Carl Jung's country home and at first dedicated to spreading Jung's work. Former Pentagon official Christopher Mellon, an advocate for UAP transparency, is also a member of the Mellon family. 1,2,3
