Freeman Dyson

British-American physicist, 1923 to 2020

A theoretical physicist at the Institute for Advanced Study. From 1957 to 1961 he worked on Project Orion, a design for spaceflight by nuclear pulse propulsion, and in 1960 he proposed searching for alien civilizations by the infrared glow of structures around their stars. In 1955 the Herald-Tribune listed him among physicists backing gravity research. 1,2
