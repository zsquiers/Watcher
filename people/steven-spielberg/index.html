Steven Spielberg

Film director and producer

The director of Close Encounters of the Third Kind (1977), whose title comes from J. Allen Hynek's classification and for which Hynek was scientific consultant, and of E.T. the Extra-Terrestrial (1982). He was an executive producer of Taken (2002), a miniseries built around the Roswell crash and abductions, and directed Disclosure Day (2026), about the moment humanity learns it is not alone. 1,2,3,4
