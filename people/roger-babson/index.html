Roger Babson

Economist, 1875 to 1967

An entrepreneur and economist who predicted the 1929 crash and founded Babson College. In 1948 he founded the Gravity Research Foundation to find ways to shield against gravity; he traced the wish to his sister's childhood drowning, writing that she "was unable to fight gravity." 1,2
