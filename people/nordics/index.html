Nordics

Reported beings: tall, blond and human-looking

Also known as Nordic aliens, Space Brothers

Tall, human-looking beings, about 6 to 7 feet, with long blond hair, blue eyes and fair skin, usually described as kind and telepathic and often said to come from the Pleiades star cluster. George Adamski was among the first to say he had met one, in the 1950s, and in 1952 George Van Tassel claimed telepathic contact with a Nordic being called "Ashtar Sheran." 1

Such encounters were most common among 1950s contactees, especially in Europe, and grew rarer as the Grey took over most accounts. Cultural historian David J. Skal suggested the image was partly inspired by the 1951 film The Day the Earth Stood Still, and scholars note the "Aryan" features these beings are often given. 1

UNVERIFIED: no contactee has produced evidence of these beings that has held up.
