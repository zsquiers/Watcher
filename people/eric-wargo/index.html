Eric Wargo

Anthropologist and writer on precognition

A science writer and editor in Washington, D.C., with a PhD in anthropology from Emory University. He writes about science fiction, consciousness and the paranormal on his blog, The Nightshirt. 1

His book Time Loops argues that the future can affect the present without paradox, and that some dreams and hunches are memories of our own future experiences. Supporters such as religion scholar Jeffrey Kripal praised it, while mainstream science does not accept that precognition exists. 1,2
