Richard Geldreich

Independent UAP history researcher

Also known as Richard Geldreich, Jr.

An independent researcher who writes about the history of UFOs and UAP on Medium, describing himself as a UAP OSINT (open-source intelligence) and history buff and a software developer. His posts dig through old newspapers and government records, on subjects including UFO sightings at Muroc Air Field (now Edwards Air Force Base) on the same day as the Roswell incident, the early contactees and ufologists Millen Cooke, Gerald Light and Meade Layne, the 1949 Air Force report behind what newspapers called Project Saucer, and what he calls the Menzel Gap, missing astronomical data from roughly 1955 to 1970. 1,2,3,4,5

Some of his pieces are openly speculative, including theoretical analyses he says he produced with an AI model. His work is self-published and has not been peer reviewed. 6
