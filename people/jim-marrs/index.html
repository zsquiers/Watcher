Jim Marrs

Journalist and conspiracy author, 1943 to 2017

Also known as James Marrs

A Fort Worth newspaper reporter whose 1989 book Crossfire became a main source for Oliver Stone's film JFK and a New York Times bestseller. 1

He went on to write bestselling books asserting government conspiracies about UFOs, remote viewing, secret societies, and the September 11 attacks, and was a member of Scholars for 9/11 Truth. He died in 2017. 1
