David Huggins

Painter and experiencer, Hoboken, New Jersey

A painter in Hoboken, New Jersey, who worked part-time at a local deli. He says that since he was eight, growing up in rural Georgia, he has had repeated encounters with beings he does not claim to understand, and he has recorded them for decades in surreal oil paintings, few of which were shown publicly before 2017. 1,2,3

His account includes a long sexual relationship with a female being he calls Crescent, and he says she bore his children. The 2017 documentary Love & Saucers follows him without endorsing or mocking his story; its director has said he believes Huggins believes it. There is no evidence beyond his own account and paintings, and critics have read the story as dream, fantasy or allegory. 4,5
