Robert Sheaffer

Writer and UFO skeptic, born 1949

A UFO skeptic who wrote the Skeptical Inquirer's "Psychic Vibrations" column from 1977 to 2017 and was a founding member, with Philip Klass and James Oberg, of the skeptics' UFO subcommittee. 1

He concluded that Jimmy Carter's 1969 UFO was Venus, and that the glowing object reported at Malmstrom in 1967 was probably Mars. 1,2
