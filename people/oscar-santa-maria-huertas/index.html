Óscar Santa María Huertas

Peruvian Air Force pilot

A Peruvian Air Force pilot who in April 1980 took off from La Joya air base near Arequipa in a Sukhoi jet to intercept an object hovering near the base. He said he fired 64 rounds into it without effect and chased it until it outran his jet. 1

He later told his account publicly, including in a chapter of Leslie Kean's 2010 book on official UFO witnesses. 2
