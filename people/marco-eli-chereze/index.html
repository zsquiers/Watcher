Marco Eli Chereze

Brazilian military police officer, died 1996

Also known as Marco Chereze

A soldier in the Minas Gerais Military Police who died in Varginha on February 15, 1996, at the age of 23, less than a month after the reported creature sightings there. His autopsy found the cause was septicemia, a bacterial blood infection. 1

Ufologists later said he had taken part in a night operation near the Jardim Andere neighborhood on January 20, 1996, to capture one of the creatures, and that his death was connected to it. A military police inquiry found no link between his death and any such operation. 1
