Wilbert Smith

Radio engineer, 1910 to 1962

Also known as Wilbert B. Smith

Led Project Magnet, the Canadian government's UFO study, from 1950 to 1954. It grew out of his November 1950 memo, which reported that U.S. scientist Robert Sarbacher told him America's flying saucer studies were led by Vannevar Bush. 1
