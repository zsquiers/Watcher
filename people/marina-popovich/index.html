Marina Popovich

Soviet test pilot, 1931 to 2017

A Soviet Air Force colonel and test pilot known as "Madame MiG," who set more than 100 aviation records and was married to the cosmonaut Pavel Popovich. In her book UFO Glasnost and in lectures she claimed that Soviet pilots had reported more than 3,000 UFO sightings and that the Soviet Air Forces and KGB had recovered fragments of five crashed UFOs. No official Soviet or Russian record confirms those claims. 1,2
