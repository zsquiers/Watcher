Dolores Cannon

Hypnotherapist and author, 1931 to 2014

A self-taught hypnotherapist and publisher who became a leader of the New Age movement and a promoter of fringe theories about aliens and alternative realities. She first claimed to reach Nostradamus through hypnosis, then from 1985 worked with people who reported alien abductions, and she developed what she called Quantum Healing Hypnosis, which she said could heal serious illnesses. The skeptic Robert Todd Carroll noted she had no medical training and that there is nothing quantum about the technique. 1,2
