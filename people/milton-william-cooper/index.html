Milton William Cooper

Conspiracy author and broadcaster, 1943 to 2001

Also known as William Cooper, Bill Cooper

An American conspiracy theorist and shortwave broadcaster, best known for his 1991 book Behold a Pale Horse. He said he had seen secret documents about government dealings with aliens while in Naval Intelligence, but public records only indicate enlisted Navy service, and ufologists found he had copied their research, including several items they had fabricated as pranks. 1

He moved on to the militia movement and broadcast a radio show from Eagar, Arizona. In November 2001 he shot and critically wounded an Apache County sheriff's deputy who had come to arrest him and was killed by another deputy. 1,2
