Jay Stratton

First director of the Navy's UAP Task Force

A longtime Naval Intelligence official, loaned to the Defense Intelligence Agency, across whose desk nearly everything related to UFO activity came from 2008 until 2021. He was the first director of the UAP Task Force, set up in August 2020, and left it in 2021. In The Age of Disclosure (2025) he said, "I have seen with my own eyes non-human craft and non-human beings," and he has since written a memoir. He has released no evidence for those claims, and critics of the film point to the lack of any physical evidence. 1,2,3
