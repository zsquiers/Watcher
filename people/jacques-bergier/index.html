Jacques Bergier

Writer and chemical engineer, 1912 to 1978

A French chemical engineer, member of the Resistance, spy, journalist and writer, who was held in the Mauthausen-Gusen concentration camp from March 1944 to May 1945. With Louis Pauwels he wrote The Morning of the Magicians (1960), whose tales of ancient astronauts came before Erich von Däniken's, and he later wrote about extraterrestrials in history. 1,2
