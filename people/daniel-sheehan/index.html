Daniel Sheehan

Public-interest lawyer and disclosure advocate, born 1945

A constitutional and public-interest lawyer who worked on the Pentagon Papers and Karen Silkwood cases and founded the Christic Institute, whose 1988 lawsuit over the La Penca bombing was dismissed with heavy fees imposed; critics said he handled matters poorly by chasing unsubstantiated allegations. 1

He says that during the Carter administration he was shown classified parts of Project Blue Book, including photographs of a crash retrieval, a claim with no documentation. Through his New Paradigm Institute he has represented UAP whistleblowers and was one of the leading advocates for the 2023 UAP Disclosure Act. 2,3
