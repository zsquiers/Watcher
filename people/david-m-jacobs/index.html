David M. Jacobs

Historian and abduction researcher, born 1942

Also known as David Michael Jacobs

An American historian, now a retired associate professor of history at Temple University, who earned his PhD at the University of Wisconsin in 1973 with a dissertation on the UFO controversy in America. It became The UFO Controversy in America (1975), with a foreword by J. Allen Hynek, and he taught a course on UFOs in American society for more than 25 years. 1

He interviewed hundreds of people who reported abductions, using hypnotic regression as well as ordinary interviews, and wrote Secret Life (1992), with a foreword by John Mack, The Threat (1998) and Walking Among Us (2015). With Budd Hopkins and Mack he helped design a 1991 Roper poll on experiences linked to abduction. 1,2
