Nick Redfern

British UFO author, born 1964

Also known as Nicholas Redfern

A British author of books on UFOs, cryptozoology and conspiracy theories, and an advocate of government disclosure, who has dug up thousands of pages of British government UFO files. 1

His 2005 book Body Snatchers in the Desert argued that Roswell may have involved secret aircraft tests on Japanese prisoners of war, and his 2010 book Final Events introduced the Collins Elite. 1,2
