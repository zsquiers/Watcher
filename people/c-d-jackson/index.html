C. D. Jackson

Time Inc. executive and White House adviser, 1902 to 1964

Also known as Charles Douglas Jackson, CD Jackson

A Time Inc. executive who ran psychological warfare efforts in World War II and led the National Committee for a Free Europe, the parent of Radio Free Europe. 1

He served as President Eisenhower's special assistant for psychological warfare and Cold War strategy in 1953 and 1954. 1
