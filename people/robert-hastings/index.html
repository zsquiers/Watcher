Robert Hastings

UFO researcher, born 1950

Also known as Robert L. Hastings

A researcher whose father was stationed at Malmstrom Air Force Base. Hastings says that in March 1967, as a teenager working in the base's air traffic control tower, he watched controllers track five UFOs near the nuclear missile sites on radar. 1

He interviewed more than 160 military veterans for UFOs and Nukes (2008, second edition 2017) and hosted a 2010 press conference at the National Press Club where former officers described objects over nuclear sites. Skeptics such as Benjamin Radford say his cases recycle reports already explained or discredited. 1
