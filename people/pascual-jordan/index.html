Pascual Jordan

German physicist, 1902 to 1980

A co-founder of quantum mechanics who helped develop matrix mechanics. He joined the Nazi Party in 1933 but defended Einstein's physics, and later served in West Germany's parliament. In 1955 the Herald-Tribune reported that the Glenn L. Martin Company had put him under contract for its gravity research. 1,2
