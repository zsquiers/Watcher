Erich von Däniken

Swiss author, born 1935

Also known as Erich von Daniken, von Daniken

The best-known promoter of the ancient astronaut theory, the idea that extraterrestrials visited Earth in the distant past and were remembered as gods. His 1968 book Chariots of the Gods? became an international bestseller, and his books had sold about 70 million copies by 2017. 1,2

Archaeologists and historians reject his claims, pointing to the documented human builders of the monuments he cites, and some of his evidence has been shown to be mistaken or borrowed. 1,2
