Wernher von Braun

Rocket engineer, 1912 to 1977

Led Nazi Germany's V-2 rocket program, which used concentration-camp slave labor, then came to the U.S. under Operation Paperclip. His team built the Army's Redstone and Jupiter rockets in Huntsville and, at NASA, the Saturn V that carried Apollo crews to the Moon. 1

He made no UFO claims himself. Carol Rosin, his spokeswoman in his last years, later said he warned her that an alien threat would one day be invented to justify weapons in space. That claim is UNVERIFIED and rests only on her account.
