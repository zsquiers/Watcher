Jesse Michels

Investor and podcast host

A former investor at Peter Thiel's family office who hosts American Alchemy, a YouTube show and podcast interviewing whistleblowers, scientists and fringe thinkers on UFOs, antigravity and ancient technology. 1,2

Several claims covered on this site first reached the public through his interviews, including Harald Malmgren's account of a craft shot down in 1962. 3
