Bob Lazar

Says he worked on alien craft

Also known as Robert Lazar

In 1989 he told Las Vegas reporter George Knapp that he had worked on alien spacecraft at a site called S-4 near Area 51. No record of the degrees he claims has been found, and his story remains unverified. 1,2
