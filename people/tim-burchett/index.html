Tim Burchett

U.S. representative from Tennessee, born 1964

A congressman from Knoxville since 2019 and one of the loudest UFO voices in the House. In 2023 he said the government had been covering up since the 1940s, that it had possibly recovered a craft and beings, and that the technology may be being reverse-engineered; in 2025 he said aliens have secret underwater bases. 1

He offered no evidence for these claims. He appears in The Age of Disclosure (2025). 1
