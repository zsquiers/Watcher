Ning Li

Chinese-American physicist, 1943 to 2021

At the University of Alabama in Huntsville in the 1990s she published papers claiming that rotating ions in a superconductor could produce an antigravity effect, and her work drew funding from NASA's Marshall Space Flight Center. In 1999 she founded AC Gravity LLC; Larry Smalley, chair of the university's physics department, believed in the work enough to leave the university and join her. The company won a $448,970 Defense Department grant in 2001; no results from that research were ever made public. 1,2

Her absence from public view led to online claims that she had vanished; in fact she was injured in 2014, cared for by her family, and died in 2021. 1,2
