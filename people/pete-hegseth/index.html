Pete Hegseth

Secretary of Defense, from 2025

Confirmed as Secretary of Defense in January 2025, a post styled Secretary of War from September 2025. After President Trump's February 2026 order to release UAP and alien-life files he said "we've got our people working on it right now," and in May 2026 the department published the first PURSUE release of files. In April 2026 House Oversight chair James Comer and Rep. Eric Burlison wrote to him about scientists who had died or vanished; the department replied that it had no active national security investigations of any of them. 1,2,3,4,5
