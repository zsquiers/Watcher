Steven Garcia

Albuquerque federal contractor, missing since 2025

A contract property custodian at the Albuquerque site of the Kansas City National Security Campus, part of the National Nuclear Security Administration, who left his home in southwest Albuquerque on foot on August 28, 2025, carrying a handgun. Police said he might be a danger to himself. He has not been found. He is one of four New Mexico cases on the lists, with Anthony Chavez, Melissa Casias and William Neil McCasland. 1,2,3
