Michael Shermer

Founder of Skeptic magazine

The founder and publisher of Skeptic magazine, for 18 years a monthly columnist for Scientific American, and a fellow at Chapman University. 1

He separates two questions: whether intelligent life exists elsewhere, and whether it has come here, which he says the evidence doesn't support. 1
