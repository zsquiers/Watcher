Amy Eskridge

Huntsville anti-gravity researcher, died 2022

A researcher in Huntsville, Alabama, who co-founded the Institute for Exotic Science and spoke about anti-gravity research in the area. She died on June 11, 2022, at 34. Police ruled her death a suicide, and her family said she had suffered ongoing pain and did not find her death unusual. She is one of three Huntsville-area names on the lists, with Ning Li and Joshua LeBlanc. 1,2
