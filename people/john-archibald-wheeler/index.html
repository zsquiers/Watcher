John Archibald Wheeler

American physicist, 1911 to 2008

A Princeton physicist who popularized the term "black hole" and co-wrote the textbook Gravitation. In 1955 the Herald-Tribune listed him among physicists giving "positive backing" to gravity research. 1,2
