Kenji Terauchi

Japan Air Lines captain, Flight 1628

Also known as Captain Kenji Terauchi

A Japan Air Lines captain, his name sometimes spelled Kenju, who had been flying for 29 years when he commanded a JAL Cargo Boeing 747 from Paris to Tokyo by way of Reykjavík and Anchorage in November 1986. 1,2

On November 17, 1986, over eastern Alaska, he reported three objects pacing his plane, "two small ships and the mother ship," and said the objects followed it for about 400 miles. The FAA interviewed the crew after they landed in Anchorage and found them professional and rational. 1,2,3

The FAA later called him a "UFO repeater" who had reported two earlier sightings and two more in January 1987. Steven Greer's Disclosure Project briefing says that after he spoke publicly he was moved to a desk job for a long time before flying again. 1,2,4
