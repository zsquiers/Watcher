Paul H. Smith

Army remote viewer and instructor

Also known as Paul Smith, Dr. Paul Smith

An Army intelligence officer recruited in 1983 to the government's remote viewing program at Fort Meade, Maryland, where he served until 1990 and wrote the program's controlled remote viewing training manual. 1,3

He later trained other well-known remote viewers, including Lyn Buchanan, and teaches the method today. A 1995 CIA-sponsored review found the program never produced useful intelligence. 1,2
