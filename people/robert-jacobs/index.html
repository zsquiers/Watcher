Robert Jacobs

Former Air Force officer

Also known as Bob Jacobs

As a lieutenant in 1964 he was on-site commander of an Air Force team filming missile launches from Vandenberg Air Force Base from a mountaintop near Big Sur. Since 1982 he has said the team filmed a UFO disabling a missile warhead. The project engineer said the film showed decoy warheads. 1
