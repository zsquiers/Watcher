Bruce Rapuano

Biomedical scientist and abduction author

Also known as Dr. Bruce E. Rapuano

A cell biologist with a degree in neurobiology and psychology from the University of Pennsylvania, a PhD in neuropharmacology from the University of Connecticut and a law degree from New York Law School, who has written more than thirty peer-reviewed medical papers. 1

In Dominion Lost (2023), a book of almost 450 pages printed through Amazon, he describes alien abductions he says he experienced from childhood and draws on his medical training to argue how reported nasal implants, mind control and missing time might work. 1
