Mark Probert

Trance medium, 1907 to 1969

The main trance medium working with Borderland Sciences Research Associates in its early years, who said he channeled a group of discarnate teachers called the Inner Circle. Meade Layne relied on his sessions as confirmation of the etheric theory of flying saucers, and Probert's messages formed the core of the group's earliest answers to the "ether ship mystery." 1,2
