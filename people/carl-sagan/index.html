Carl Sagan

Astronomer, 1934 to 1996

A Cornell astronomer and science popularizer. In 1966 he served on the Air Force's Ad Hoc Committee to Review Project Blue Book, whose call for a university study led to the Condon Committee, and in 1968 he spoke at the House UFO symposium. 1

Skeptical of extraterrestrial explanations but convinced the subject deserved study, he organized the 1969 AAAS symposium on UFOs, which included both proponents and skeptics, and popularized the maxim "extraordinary claims require extraordinary evidence." 1
