Paul Bennewitz

Physicist and businessman, 1927 to 2003

Also known as Paul Frederick Bennewitz

Ran Thunder Scientific Corporation, an electronics firm next to Kirtland Air Force Base in Albuquerque. From 1979 he filmed lights and recorded signals near the base and became convinced they came from alien craft, and reported this to the Air Force in 1980. 1

Air Force intelligence agents, including Richard Doty, and researcher Bill Moore fed him forged documents and false stories, apparently to protect classified projects at the base. He came to believe in an alien underground base at Dulce, New Mexico, and in a coming invasion, and was hospitalized after a breakdown in the mid-1980s. Greg Bishop's 2005 book Project Beta tells the story. 1,2
