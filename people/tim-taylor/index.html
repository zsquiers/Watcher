Tim Taylor

NASA mission controller

Also known as Timothy Taylor

A NASA mission controller, Taylor appears prominently in Diana Walsh Pasulka's 2019 book American Cosmic and is closely tied to Chris Bledsoe, whom he has visited and studied. He has claimed involvement with military and intelligence programs and has said he briefed senior officials on UAP. 1

UNVERIFIED: his claimed military and intelligence work and briefings have not been independently confirmed, and some researchers have criticized how readily they have been accepted. Not to be confused with Travis Taylor, the engineer who served as chief scientist for the Pentagon's UAP Task Force.
