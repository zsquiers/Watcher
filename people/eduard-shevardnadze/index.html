Eduard Shevardnadze

Soviet foreign minister under Gorbachev

Mikhail Gorbachev's foreign minister, who in 1985 put the Soviet "Star Peace" proposal for international cooperation in space, and a ban on space strike weapons, before the United Nations as the Soviet alternative to Reagan's Strategic Defense Initiative. 1,2
