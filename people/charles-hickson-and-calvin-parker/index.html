Charles Hickson and Calvin Parker

Pascagoula abduction claimants

Also known as Charles Hickson, Calvin Parker

On October 11, 1973, Charles Hickson and his teenage relative Calvin Parker reported that while fishing on the Pascagoula River in Mississippi they were taken aboard an oval craft by three creatures with pincers and examined. Officers who secretly recorded them alone heard their stories stay consistent. 1

Hickson became a UFO celebrity and died in 2011. Philip Klass found discrepancies, noted Hickson refused a polygraph by an experienced examiner, and concluded it was a hoax. Parker told his own story in a 2018 book and died in 2023. 1
