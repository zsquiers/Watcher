Thomas R. Wilson

Navy vice admiral, DIA director 1999 to 2002

Also known as Thomas Wilson, Tom Wilson

A retired Navy vice admiral who directed the Defense Intelligence Agency from 1999 to 2002 after serving as intelligence director of the Joint Staff. Notes posted online in 2019 claim he told the physicist Eric W. Davis in 2002 that he had been refused access to a crash-retrieval program; Wilson denies the meeting ever happened and calls the notes fiction. 1
