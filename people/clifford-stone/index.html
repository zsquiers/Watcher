Clifford Stone

Retired Army sergeant

A retired Army sergeant from Roswell, New Mexico, who said his nuclear, biological and chemical assignments were cover for a team that recovered crashed craft, and that he had seen living and dead extraterrestrials. He testified for Steven Greer's Disclosure Project in 2001. He also used Freedom of Information requests to document the Air Force's real programs for recovering fallen space objects, such as Project Moon Dust, which he presented as UFO retrieval work. No independent record supports his retrieval claims. 1
