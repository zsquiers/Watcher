Maurice Masse

French lavender farmer, Valensole witness

A lavender farmer in Valensole, Provence, who said that early on July 1, 1965, he came upon a landed craft in his field, described as egg-shaped or as a spherical vehicle, with two small beings beside it. He said one of them pointed a tubelike object at him and he was paralyzed, and that he watched them look at his plants before they flew away. 1,2

According to his wife, he considered the encounter a spiritual experience and looked on the spot as hallowed ground that should stay in his family forever. 1
