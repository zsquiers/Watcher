Leonard Stringfield

UFO researcher

An Ohio UFO researcher who in the 1970s and 1980s collected and published secondhand accounts of crashed craft and recovered bodies in a series of reports on crash retrievals. His 1991 report gave wide circulation to the Cape Girardeau story. 1
