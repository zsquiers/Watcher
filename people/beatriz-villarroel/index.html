Beatriz Villarroel

Astronomer, VASCO project leader

An astronomer at the Nordic Institute for Theoretical Physics (Nordita) in Stockholm and the Instituto de Astrofísica de Canarias. Since 2017 she has led VASCO, which compares 1950s sky photographs with modern surveys to look for stars and points of light that appear or vanish. 1

Her 2025 papers argue that brief flashes on 1950s Palomar plates may come from flat, reflective objects in orbit before Sputnik, and that they line up with nuclear tests and UFO reports. Other astronomers, notably Nigel Hambly, argue the flashes are flaws in the photographic emulsion. 2,3,4
