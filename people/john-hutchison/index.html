John Hutchison

Canadian inventor

Also known as John Hutchinson

A Canadian inventor who, from around 1979, has claimed that high-voltage equipment, much of it military surplus, set up in his apartment produces the "Hutchison effect": heavy objects levitating, metal fracturing or turning to jelly, and metal and wood fusing together. 1,2

UNVERIFIED: his evidence is mainly his own word and videos, and the effect has not been reproduced, even by him. He has said he demonstrated it for scientists from U.S. Army intelligence, but no source confirms any military evaluation or funding. 1,2
