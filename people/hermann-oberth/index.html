Hermann Oberth

Rocket pioneer, 1894 to 1989

A German-Romanian physicist whose 1923 book on rockets into space helped found the field; a young Wernher von Braun assisted his early experiments. He worked on German rockets during World War II and for the U.S. Army in Huntsville from 1955 to 1958. 1

In 1954 he said publicly that flying saucers were real, probably from another star system, and flown by beings far more advanced than humans. He offered no evidence beyond the sighting reports themselves. 1
