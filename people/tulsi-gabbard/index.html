Tulsi Gabbard

Director of National Intelligence, 2025 to 2026

Sworn in as Director of National Intelligence on February 12, 2025. In an August 2025 interview she said she believed extraterrestrial life might exist but had to be careful with what she shares, promising to keep looking for the truth. After President Trump ordered agencies in February 2026 to release files on UAP and alien life, her office co-led with the Pentagon the first PURSUE release in May 2026. She resigned that June after her husband was diagnosed with cancer. 1,2,3
