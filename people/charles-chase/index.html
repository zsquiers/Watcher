Charles Chase

Aerospace engineer, Skunk Works veteran

He worked at Lockheed Martin's Skunk Works from 1986 to 2018, apart from a few years in the early 1990s, where he founded and led its Revolutionary Technology Programs, the group at the front end of its new technology. An expert in plasma systems, he became vice president of engineering at the space company Momentus in 2022. 1,2

He has pursued an unproven idea for propulsion: drawing a push from the fluctuations of the quantum vacuum, the restless energy of empty space. A 2024 National Science Foundation grant funded his attempt at what would be the first measurement of such a force. UFO podcasts tie him to secret breakthroughs, but no public evidence supports those claims. 3
