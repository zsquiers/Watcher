Mikhail Gorbachev

Soviet leader, 1931 to 2022

The last leader of the Soviet Union. At their first meeting, in Geneva in 1985, Ronald Reagan asked him whether the Soviets would help if the United States were attacked from outer space; Gorbachev recalled in 2009 that he answered "No doubt about it." At the 1986 Reykjavik summit his insistence that SDI research be confined to laboratories helped the talks collapse. 1,2,3
