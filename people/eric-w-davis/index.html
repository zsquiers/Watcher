Eric W. Davis

Physicist and Pentagon UFO program consultant

Also known as Eric Davis, Dr. Eric Davis

An astrophysicist at Harold Puthoff's EarthTech International who worked as a subcontractor and then a consultant for the Pentagon's UFO programs from 2007, including AAWSAP. 1,2

In 2020 he told The New York Times he had given a classified briefing to a Defense Department agency about retrievals from "off-world vehicles not made on this earth." Notes of a 2002 meeting with Vice Admiral Thomas Wilson, leaked in 2019 and attributed to Davis, describe Wilson being denied access to a hidden UFO program. Wilson has called them fiction, and Davis hasn't confirmed writing them. 1,2
