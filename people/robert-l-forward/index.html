Robert L. Forward

Physicist and science fiction writer, 1932 to 2002

A physicist who built early detectors for gravitational waves and worked at Hughes Aircraft's research labs, where he received 18 patents. He later consulted for NASA and the Air Force on speculative propulsion such as solar sails, antimatter and negative matter, and wrote science fiction known for its scientific credibility. 1
