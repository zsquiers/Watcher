Antônio Villas Boas

Farmer, 1934 to 1991

Also known as Antonio Vilas-Boas, Antonio Villas-Boas, Vilas Boas

A Brazilian farmer whose 1957 account of being taken aboard a craft is one of the earliest widely reported abductions. He later became a lawyer. 1
