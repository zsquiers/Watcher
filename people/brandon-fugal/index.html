Brandon Fugal

Owner of Skinwalker Ranch

A Utah commercial real estate executive who bought Skinwalker Ranch from Robert Bigelow in 2016. He hired a team of researchers to study the reported anomalies there, which became the History Channel series The Secret of Skinwalker Ranch. 1,2
