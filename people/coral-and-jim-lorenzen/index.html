Coral and Jim Lorenzen

Founders of APRO

Also known as Coral Lorenzen, Jim Lorenzen, L. J. Lorenzen

Husband-and-wife founders of the Aerial Phenomena Research Organization in 1952. Coral ran the group and wrote several books on UFO sightings and abduction reports, and Jim handled its technical side. APRO ended after Coral's death in 1988. 1
