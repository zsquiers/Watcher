Donald Keyhoe

Marine Corps aviator and author, 1897 to 1988

Also known as Donald E. Keyhoe, Major Keyhoe

A retired Marine Corps major whose 1950 book The Flying Saucers Are Real argued that the Air Force was hiding what it knew. 1

He led NICAP from 1957 to 1969 and pressed Congress to hold open UFO hearings. 1
