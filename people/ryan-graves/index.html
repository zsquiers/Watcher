Ryan Graves

Former Navy pilot

A former F/A-18 pilot who has said his squadron saw unidentified objects almost daily off the East Coast in 2014 and 2015. He testified at the House hearing in July 2023. 2

He founded Americans for Safe Aerospace, a nonprofit that gives pilots and other witnesses a trusted way to report UAP. 1
