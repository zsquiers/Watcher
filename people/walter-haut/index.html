Walter Haut

Army Air Forces press officer, 1922 to 2005

The public information officer at Roswell Army Air Field who issued the July 8, 1947, press release announcing the capture of a "flying disc." In a 1989 interview he said he knew nothing about what was recovered. 1

He co-founded the International UFO Museum and Research Center in Roswell in 1991. A 2002 "sealed affidavit," written by ufologist Donald Schmitt and signed by Haut, was published after his death and went far beyond his earlier accounts; it was criticized for lack of evidence and inconsistencies. 1
