Carol Rosin

Space-weapons opponent

A corporate manager at Fairchild Industries from 1974 to 1977, where she served as spokesperson for Wernher von Braun in his last years. She later campaigned against anti-satellite weapons and the Strategic Defense Initiative, and in 2001 appeared as a witness for Steven Greer's Disclosure Project, where she said von Braun had warned her that an invented alien threat would one day be used to justify weapons in space. 1,2
