David Morehouse

Army officer and remote viewer

Also known as David A. Morehouse

An Army officer who says he was recruited into the military's remote viewing unit after a head injury, joining toward the end of the program. His 1996 memoir Psychic Warrior describes Extended Remote Viewing sessions and says he viewed events including the Gulf War. 1,2

He faced court-martial charges and left the Army; he says the charges were pressure to stop him exposing the program, while others connected to the unit have challenged his account. 1,2,3
