Budd Hopkins

Artist and researcher, 1931 to 2011

An abstract painter who became a leading investigator of reported abductions. 1

In January 1990 he met Harvard psychiatrist John Mack, whose own abduction research grew out of that meeting and the experiencer interviews Hopkins shared with him. 2,3
