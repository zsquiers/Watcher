Stanley Deser

American physicist, 1931 to 2023

A physicist known for the ADM formalism of general relativity, developed with Richard Arnowitt and Charles Misner, and for co-discovering supergravity. In 1955, at the Institute for Advanced Study, he and Arnowitt suggested that newly discovered high-energy particles might be a link between gravity and nuclear energy. 1,2
