T. Townsend Brown

Physicist and inventor, 1905 to 1985

Also known as Thomas Townsend Brown, TT Brown

An American physicist who claimed that high-voltage capacitors produce a thrust he linked to gravity. It came to be called the Biefeld-Brown effect, a name Brown probably coined to present Denison University professor Paul Alfred Biefeld as his collaborator, though Denison has no record of their working together. Most physicists attribute the thrust to ion wind. 1

He worked for the Navy in the 1930s and during World War II, including research on minesweeping. In 1952 he proposed Project Winterhaven, a military program to develop electrogravitic propulsion, which was not adopted. 1

In 1956 he helped found NICAP, the leading civilian UFO group of its era, and was briefly its first director. His work is often cited in claims about secret antigravity research, but no such program has been confirmed. 1
