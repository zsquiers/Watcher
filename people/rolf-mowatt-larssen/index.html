Rolf Mowatt-Larssen

CIA officer and Energy Department intelligence chief

Also known as Rolf Mowatt-Larssen, RML

He spent 23 years as a CIA officer, rising to chief of its Europe Division and of its weapons of mass destruction department, then served three years as director of intelligence and counterintelligence at the Department of Energy. He became a senior fellow at Harvard's Belfer Center in 2009. 1

His 2020 memoir ties his spy career to his religious conversion. In a 2026 interview on the American Alchemy podcast he described a 1991 experience at Mount Athos in Greece in which he says he went back in time and lived for months as a medieval monk, and he suggested the Energy Department holds deeper secrets than the CIA. He offered these as personal experiences, not documented evidence. 2,3
