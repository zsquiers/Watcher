Joseph McMoneagle

Army remote viewer, born 1946

Also known as Joe McMoneagle

An Army intelligence soldier from Miami who enlisted in 1964, was badly hurt in a helicopter crash in Vietnam, and retired in 1984 as a chief warrant officer with the Legion of Merit. From 1978 he was known as Remote Viewer No. 1 in the Army's psychic spying unit at Fort Meade, Maryland, and he trained at the Monroe Institute. 1

In a 1984 session he described pyramids and tall, thin people on ancient Mars, a session he wrote about in his 1993 book Mind Trek. He has said remote viewing works across time, while giving differing estimates of his own accuracy. 1,2,3

After the program closed in 1995 he ran a remote viewing business and appeared on television, including a 1994 ABC special whose editing the skeptic Brian Dunning criticized. 1
