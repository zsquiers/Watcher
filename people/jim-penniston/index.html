Jim Penniston

Air Force security officer, Rendlesham

A sergeant at RAF Woodbridge who went into Rendlesham Forest in December 1980. He later said he encountered a "craft of unknown origin" and touched it, finding it warm; there was no public mention of this at the time, and no other witness corroborates it. 1
