David Grusch

Former intelligence officer

Also known as Dave Grusch

An Air Force veteran and former intelligence officer who represented his agency on the UAP Task Force. 1

In July 2023 he testified to the House Oversight Committee that the government runs a secret program to recover crashed craft. AARO's 2024 historical report found no evidence of such a program. 1,2,3
