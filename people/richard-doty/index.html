Richard Doty

Former Air Force intelligence agent

Also known as Richard C. Doty

A special agent with the Air Force Office of Special Investigations at Kirtland Air Force Base in the late 1970s and 1980s. He has acknowledged feeding UFO disinformation to researchers, most notably to Paul Bennewitz, an Albuquerque businessman who had been recording signals near the base and came to believe in an alien underground base at Dulce, New Mexico. The campaign contributed to Bennewitz's breakdown. 1

Researchers identified him as Falcon, the disguised informant in the 1988 TV special UFO Cover-Up? Live!, and Bill Moore named him as a key contact. He has told shifting stories, sometimes claiming the alien material was real, and appears in the 2013 documentary Mirage Men. 1
