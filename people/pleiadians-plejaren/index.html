Pleiadians (Plejaren)

Reported beings: Billy Meier's contacts

Human-looking beings that Swiss contactee Billy Meier says come from a world near the Pleiades and have visited him since 1975, starting with a woman he calls Semjase. He calls them the Plejaren and says they let him photograph and film their "beamships." 1,2

In ufology the name Pleiadians is also used more broadly for the Nordics, the tall, blond beings said to come from the Pleiades star cluster. 2

DISPUTED: Meier's ex-wife said the craft were models made from household items, two of his "alien women" turned out to be dancers from The Dean Martin Show, and scientists, skeptics and most ufologists consider his photos hoaxes. 1
