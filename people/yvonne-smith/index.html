Yvonne Smith

Hypnotherapist and abduction researcher

Also known as Yvonne R. Smith

A certified hypnotherapist who began working with trauma clients in the early 1990s and says patterns of alien abduction experiences surfaced in their sessions. She founded the Close Encounter Research Organization (CERO) as a support group for experiencers, in the tradition of Budd Hopkins and John Mack. 1

She wrote Chosen, about claimed alien hybrid programs, and Coronado, transcripts of hypnosis sessions with people who reported a shared experience at a 1994 conference at the Hotel del Coronado in San Diego. Critics of hypnotic regression warn that it can create false memories. 1
