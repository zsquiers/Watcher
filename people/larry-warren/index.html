Larry Warren

Claimed Rendlesham witness

A former Air Force security airman who says he saw a landed craft and beings during the 1980 Rendlesham Forest incident. He first told his story under a pseudonym in 1984 and set it out in Left at East Gate (1997) with Peter Robbins. Lt. Col. Charles Halt and other key witnesses say he was not with them, and Jenny Randles wrote that where Warren was is not clear. 1
