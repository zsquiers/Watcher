Lawrence Preston Gise

Atomic Energy Commission official

Also known as Lawrence Gise, L. P. Gise

An Atomic Energy Commission official who was assistant director of its Division of Military Applications in the late 1950s, then left the AEC in 1958 to help set up the Pentagon's Advanced Research Projects Agency, later DARPA. He later served as the AEC's regional director in Albuquerque, and retired early to his family's ranch near Cotulla, Texas. He was the maternal grandfather of Amazon founder Jeff Bezos. 1,2

In interviews recorded shortly before his death in 2025, Harald Malmgren said that in late 1962 Gise let him handle debris from a non-human craft, as a test of whether the material would affect his mind. Researcher Douglas Dean Johnson found no record of such a meeting, and an Energy Department records search found that Malmgren never held the clearance it would have required. 3,4
