The Cosmic Pulse of Life

1976

By Trevor James Constable

Trevor James Constable's fuller case that many UFOs are living creatures of the atmosphere, published by Merlin Press in Santa Ana, California, and reissued by Pocket Books in 1978 as Sky Creatures: Living UFOs. It retold older accounts as evidence, including the 1925 Don Wood Jr. story. 1,2
