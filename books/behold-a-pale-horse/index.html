Behold a Pale Horse

1991

By Milton William Cooper

Milton William Cooper's book combining Majestic 12 and alien-treaty claims with theories about the Illuminati and a New World Order. It became widely read on the conspiracy fringe and in the militia movement, whose manifesto The Guardian called it. 1
