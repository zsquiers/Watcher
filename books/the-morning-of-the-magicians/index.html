The Morning of the Magicians

1960

By Jacques Bergier

Louis Pauwels and Jacques Bergier's bestseller of "fantastic realism," mixing alchemy, secret history, ufology and claims about the Nazis and the occult; it was published in English as The Dawn of Magic and The Morning of the Magicians. Its tales of ancient astronauts predated Erich von Däniken's, and historians blame it for spreading myths about occultism in Nazism. French scientists rebutted it in 1965. 1
