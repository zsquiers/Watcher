UFOs and Nukes

2008 · Robert Hastings

Subtitled Extraordinary Encounters at Nuclear Weapons Sites. Robert Hastings draws on interviews with more than 160 military veterans to argue that UFOs have watched, and at times interfered with, U.S. nuclear weapons. A second edition followed in 2017. 1
