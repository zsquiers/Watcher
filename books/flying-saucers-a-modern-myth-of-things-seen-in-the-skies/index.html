Flying Saucers: A Modern Myth of Things Seen in the Skies

1958

By Carl Jung

The Swiss psychiatrist analyzed the saucer wave as a modern myth, asking why anxious Cold War people were seeing round, glowing symbols of wholeness in the sky. He left open whether something physical lay behind some reports. Published in German in 1958 and in English in 1959. 1
