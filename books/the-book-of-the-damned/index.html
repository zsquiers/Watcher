The Book of the Damned

1919

By Charles Fort

Charles Fort's first collection of what he called damned data: reports of strange lights in the sky, falls of fish and frogs, and other anomalies that the science of his day ignored. It predates the flying saucer era by decades, and such oddities are still called Fortean in his honor. 1,2
