Uri

1974

By Andrija Puharich

Andrija Puharich's account of Uri Geller, subtitled A Journal of the Mystery of Uri Geller. It claims Puharich and Geller communicated with super-intelligent computers from outer space, which warned of disaster unless humanity changed its ways and had chosen Geller to help. It sold well, but reviewers called it credulous. 1,2
