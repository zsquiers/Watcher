1975

By David M. Jacobs

Historian David Jacobs's study of how Americans, the press, the Air Force and scientists argued over flying saucers from the airship scares of 1896 to the 1970s, based on his doctoral dissertation and published by Indiana University Press with a foreword by J. Allen Hynek. 1
