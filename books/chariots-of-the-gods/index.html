Chariots of the Gods?

1968

By Erich von Däniken

Erich von Däniken's bestseller argued that ancient monuments and myths, from the pyramids to the Nazca Lines, record visits by extraterrestrial astronauts mistaken for gods. It launched the modern ancient astronaut idea. Archaeologists rejected its evidence, critics showed that some of its ideas were borrowed from the 1960 book The Morning of the Magicians, and von Däniken later conceded that one of his examples, an iron pillar in India, was man-made. 1
