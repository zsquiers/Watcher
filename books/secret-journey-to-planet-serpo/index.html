Secret Journey to Planet Serpo

2013

By Len Kasten

Len Kasten's book presents Project Serpo as a true story, retelling the alleged 1965 to 1978 mission of twelve U.S. military personnel to a planet in the Zeta Reticuli system. It draws on the anonymous Serpo releases, which claimed to come from Defense Intelligence Agency files and the team commander's diary; no such documents have ever been authenticated. 1,2
