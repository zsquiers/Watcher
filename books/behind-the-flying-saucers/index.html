Behind the Flying Saucers

1950

By Frank Scully

The first bestselling UFO book. It told the Aztec, New Mexico, crash story, with bodies of small humanoids recovered by the military, based on accounts from Silas Newton and "Dr. Gee." Both were exposed as con men in 1952. 1,2
