The Seventh Sense

2003 · Lyn Buchanan

Lyn Buchanan's account of his years as a military remote viewer, subtitled The Secrets of Remote Viewing as Told by a "Psychic Spy" for the U.S. Military. It includes his claim that during the Gulf War he was tasked to read Saddam Hussein's plans each day. Publishers Weekly predicted skeptics would vigorously dispute it. 1,2
