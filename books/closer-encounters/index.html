Closer Encounters

2021

By Jason Reza Jorjani

Written in response to the U.S. government's 2021 UAP report, it weighs seven explanations for close encounters, among them extraterrestrials, a breakaway human civilization, time travelers and simulation avatars, and argues they are facets of one reality. The publisher's description presents the UAP report as a step toward a totalitarian regime rather than disclosure. 1
