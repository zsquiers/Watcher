Out of Time

2023 · Steve Aspin

Subtitled The Intergenerational Abduction Program Explored. Aspin combines his own experiences with years of reading and interviews to argue that alien abductions pass down through families and began by the 1890s. It was published through Grosvenor House, a self-publishing service. 1,2
