Not For Disclosure

2026 · Jonathan Caplan

Subtitled UFOs: The World's Best Kept Secret. Barrister Jonathan Caplan argues that the public has been asking the wrong question: not whether UFOs are real, but why their existence has never been disclosed. His publisher says the book includes new material on JFK and on secret meetings held by President Truman and Defense Secretary James Forrestal, along with previously unpublished Majestic 12 documents. The Spectator called it the most persuasive defence yet of the cover-up theory, while flagging some of its more eyebrow-raising claims. 1,2,3
