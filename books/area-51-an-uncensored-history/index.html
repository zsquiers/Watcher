Area 51: An Uncensored History

2011 · Annie Jacobsen

Annie Jacobsen's history of the Nevada test site, drawing on interviews with veterans of the U-2, A-12, and other secret aircraft programs flown there. Its final chapter claimed, from a single unnamed source, that Roswell was a Soviet plot, a claim historians and reviewers widely rejected. 1
