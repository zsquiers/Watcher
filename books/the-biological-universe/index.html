The Biological Universe

1996

By Steven J. Dick

A history of the twentieth-century debate over extraterrestrial life and the limits of science, from life in the solar system to planets around other stars. One chapter traces the UFO controversy and the rise and fall of the extraterrestrial hypothesis among scientists. 1,2
