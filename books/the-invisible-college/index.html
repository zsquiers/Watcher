The Invisible College

1975 · Jacques Vallée

Named for the informal network of researchers who quietly investigated cases and shared data across borders. Vallée asks whether a force that has influenced the human race comes from outside or from within human consciousness, and proposes that UFOs may act as a "control system" on human beliefs. 1
