Challenge to Science

1966 · Jacques Vallée

Subtitled The UFO Enigma, written by Jacques Vallée with his wife, Janine Vallée, and published by Regnery in Chicago. It argues that when witnesses are sincere and detailed, the shapes, sizes and behavior of the objects they report cannot be overlooked. 1,2,3
