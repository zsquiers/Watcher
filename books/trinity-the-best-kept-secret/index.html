Trinity: The Best-Kept Secret

2021

By Jacques Vallée

Self-published by Jacques Vallée and Paola Harris, it retells Reme Baca and Jose Padilla's story of an avocado-shaped craft that crashed near the Trinity test site in August 1945, and argues it has obvious similarities to Roswell. It drops the live creatures and body recovery of earlier versions without explanation. 1,2
