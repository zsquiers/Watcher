The Andreasson Affair

1979 · Raymond Fowler

Raymond Fowler's account of Betty Andreasson, a Massachusetts woman who described under hypnosis being taken from her home by small beings in 1967. It is credited with introducing alleged alien implants into abduction accounts; critics question memories recovered under hypnosis. 1
