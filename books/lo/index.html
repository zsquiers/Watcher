Lo!

1931

By Charles Fort

Charles Fort's third book, in which he proposes that a hidden force teleports people, animals and objects, coining the word teleportation. He uses the idea to explain falls of animals, poltergeists and disappearances such as the Mary Celeste's, briefly revisits unidentified objects in the sky, and attacks mainstream astronomy. 1
