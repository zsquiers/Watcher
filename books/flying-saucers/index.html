Flying Saucers

1953

By Donald Menzel

Harvard astronomer Donald Menzel's skeptical account argued that saucer reports came from misidentified stars, aircraft, and clouds, or from unfamiliar atmospheric effects such as mirages. It set the template for scientific debunking. 1
