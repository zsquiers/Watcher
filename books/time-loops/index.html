Time Loops

2018

By Eric Wargo

Subtitled Precognition, Retrocausation, and the Unconscious. It draws on lab experiments, dreams and writers' accounts to argue that we can sense our own future, as J.W. Dunne proposed in the 1920s, and that effects from the future can shape the past without paradox. 1,2
