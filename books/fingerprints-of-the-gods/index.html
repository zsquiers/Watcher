Fingerprints of the Gods

1995 · Graham Hancock

Graham Hancock's bestseller argued that an advanced lost civilization, wiped out at the end of the last Ice Age, left its mark on monuments such as Giza and Tiwanaku. Unlike von Däniken he did not invoke aliens. Archaeologists reject the theory as pseudoarchaeology. 1,2
