UFOs: Operation Trojan Horse

1970

By John Keel

John Keel's second book, published by Putnam, drawn from what he said were interviews with thousands of witnesses across 20 states. He argued that UFOs are not spacecraft from other planets but displays staged by "ultraterrestrials," beings able to manipulate matter and human perception, who in earlier eras appeared as fairies, demons and the 1897 mystery airships. Kirkus noted its conclusion was close to Jacques Vallée's Passport to Magonia but questioned its witnesses. It was later reprinted as Why UFOs. 1,2,3,4
