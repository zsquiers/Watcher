UFO Crash at Roswell

1991

By Kevin Randle, Donald Schmitt

Kevin Randle and Donald Schmitt gathered witness accounts of strange debris and, in later tellings, bodies, helping turn Roswell into the model crash-retrieval story. Several of the witness accounts were later questioned or discredited. 1,2
