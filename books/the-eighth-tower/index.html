The Eighth Tower

1975

By John Keel

Published by Saturday Review Press and built from material left out of The Mothman Prophecies, it set out Keel's "superspectrum" hypothesis: a vast range of energies beyond human perception in which non-human intelligences operate, placing UFOs in the same category as ghosts and religious apparitions that have shaped human belief. Kirkus dismissed it as "less a book than a mental marinade." Anomalist Books later reissued it. 1,2,3,4
