Secret Life

1992

By David M. Jacobs

David Jacobs's Secret Life: Firsthand Accounts of UFO Abductions, from Simon & Schuster with a foreword by John Mack, drew on hypnosis sessions with people reporting abductions to argue that aliens run a systematic program of abductions. Critics fault its reliance on hypnosis, which can create false memories. 1
