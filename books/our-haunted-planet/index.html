Our Haunted Planet

1971

By John Keel

A Fawcett paperback in which Keel ranged over vanished continents, ancient towers, men-in-black lore and the fading of the old gods, building on his idea of ultraterrestrials: intelligences that have shadowed humanity throughout history rather than visiting from elsewhere. 1,2
