RAND maps UAP reports

2023

RAND's study Not the X-Files analyzed 101,151 public UAP reports made to the National UFO Reporting Center from 1998 to 2022 and found sightings were more common within about 30 km of military operations areas. It suggested many reports may be authorized military flights and recommended outreach to nearby residents. 1,2
