Unidentified Flying Objects: The True Story of Flying Saucers

1956

A semi-documentary, also released as UFO, about how the U.S. government handled flying saucer reports, told through Air Force press officer Albert M. Chop, who pointed producer Clarence Greene to the footage it uses. It shows the 1950 Mariana film from Great Falls, Montana, with Nicholas Mariana as himself, and the 1952 Tremonton film, with Navy photographer Delbert Newhouse as himself, recreates Kenneth Arnold's sighting, and ends with the 1952 Washington flap. Its clips have been reused in UFO documentaries ever since. 1
