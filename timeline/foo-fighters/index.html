Foo fighters

1944

From late 1944, Allied night-fighter crews over Europe, notably the 415th Night Fighter Squadron, reported balls of light that seemed to pace their aircraft. They called them foo fighters, after a comic strip. Similar lights were reported over the Pacific. Suggested explanations include electrical discharges like St. Elmo's fire, ball lightning, and German jets and rockets. The CIA's 1953 Robertson Panel noted they would have been called flying saucers had the term existed. 1,2
