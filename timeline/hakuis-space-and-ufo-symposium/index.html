Hakui's space and UFO symposium

1990

Hakui city employee Jōsen Takano staged the International Symposium on Space and UFOs, which drew about 50,000 visitors to a town of 20,000. Its success brought national regional-development funding for Cosmo Isle Hakui, which opened in 1996. 1
