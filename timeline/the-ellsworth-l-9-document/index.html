The Ellsworth L-9 document

1977

A document said to come from a FOIA request describes an intruder alarm at Ellsworth's L-9 missile launch facility on November 16, 1977, where security airmen met figures in glowing suits and a hovering craft. It has long been considered a hoax: MUFON's Bob Pratt exposed it in 1984, researchers cite its spelling and technical errors, and it is strongly suspected to be a fabrication by Richard Doty, who was stationed at Ellsworth then. 1
