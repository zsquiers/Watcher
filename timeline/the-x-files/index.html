The X-Files

1993

Chris Carter's Fox series about two FBI agents investigating unsolved paranormal cases, including what appears to be a government plan to hide evidence of extraterrestrial life. Inspired especially by Kolchak: The Night Stalker, it tapped into public mistrust of governments and large institutions. The background of its famous "I want to believe" poster is a photo by Billy Meier. 1,2
