Marconi's mystery signals

1920

In 1920 Guglielmo Marconi, the radio pioneer, said his stations had picked up unexplained long-wavelength signals that might come from another world, possibly Mars. Skeptics noted that only his own stations heard them, and they were likely natural or earthly interference. 1
