Pat Price describes four UFO bases

1973

Working at SRI as a contractor, remote viewer Pat Price produced a transcript on his own, without a monitor or established protocols, describing underground UFO bases inside Mount Hayes in Alaska, Monte Perdido in the Pyrenees, Mount Inyangani in Zimbabwe and Mount Zeil in Australia. SRI staff were surprised, as they had not tasked him with it. The claims are unverified. 1,2
