AARO FY2025 report

2026

Released in July 2026, months late, AARO's report on June 2024 to May 2025 counted 319 new reports and resolved 114 of them, plus 256 older cases, mostly as balloons, birds, drones, aircraft and satellites. New satellite analysis explained 238 cases as sunlight flashing off satellites. AARO said nothing it resolved pointed to alien or advanced foreign technology, but about 200 reports remain unexplained, mostly for lack of data. 1,2
