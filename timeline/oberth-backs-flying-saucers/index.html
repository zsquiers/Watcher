Oberth backs flying saucers

1954

Hermann Oberth, a founder of rocket science, wrote in The American Weekly in October 1954 that flying saucers were real and probably came from another star system, piloted by beings far ahead of humanity. A year later he joined Wernher von Braun's Army rocket team in Huntsville. 1
