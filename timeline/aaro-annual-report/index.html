AARO annual report

2024

AARO's fiscal year 2024 consolidated annual report, released in November 2024, counted 757 new UAP reports received from May 2023 to June 2024. Most cases resolved so far were balloons, birds, drones, or satellites, and many others lacked enough data to analyze. 1
