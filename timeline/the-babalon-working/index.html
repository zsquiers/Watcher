The Babalon Working

1946

In January and February 1946, rocket engineer Jack Parsons and science fiction writer L. Ron Hubbard performed a series of rituals in Parsons' Pasadena home and the Mojave Desert, based on Aleister Crowley's Thelema, meant to bring the goddess Babalon into human form. Parsons believed it succeeded when the artist Marjorie Cameron arrived at the house; they married that October. Cameron reported seeing a strange object in the sky in 1946, which Parsons recorded as a materialization of Babalon, and later occultists tied the rituals to the 1947 flying saucer wave. 1,2
