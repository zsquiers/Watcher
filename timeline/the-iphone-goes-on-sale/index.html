The iPhone goes on sale

2007

Apple's iPhone went on sale in June 2007, and smartphones soon put a camera in almost every pocket. Skeptics point out that clear UFO photos did not become common as a result; small phone lenses tend to turn distant lights at night into blurry blobs. 1
