Aerospace engineers' society forms a UAP committee

2021

The American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics set up its Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena Integration and Outreach Committee to bring aerospace professionals, scientists and policymakers together on UAP and air safety, with groups on sensors and on interviewing pilots. 1
