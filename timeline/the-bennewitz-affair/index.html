The Bennewitz affair

1980

Albuquerque physicist Paul Bennewitz told Kirtland Air Force Base that he had filmed lights and recorded signals he believed came from alien craft. Instead of dismissing him, Air Force Office of Special Investigations agents, including Richard Doty, with help from researcher Bill Moore, fed him fake documents and stories, apparently to steer attention away from classified work at the base. He came to believe in an alien base at Dulce and suffered a breakdown in the mid-1980s. 1,2
