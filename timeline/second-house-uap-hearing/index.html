Second House UAP hearing

2024

On November 13, 2024, House Oversight subcommittees heard from former officials and journalist Michael Shellenberger, whose report alleging a hidden UAP data program called Immaculate Constellation was entered into the record by Rep. Nancy Mace. The Pentagon denies the program exists. 1,2
