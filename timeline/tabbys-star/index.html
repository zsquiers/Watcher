Tabby's Star

2015

Citizen scientists studying Kepler data found that the star KIC 8462852, about 1,470 light-years away, dimmed irregularly by as much as 22 percent. After Tabetha Boyajian's 2015 paper, astronomer Jason Wright suggested an alien megastructure as one possibility, which made the star famous. In 2017 NASA reported that the long-term dimming was likely an uneven dust cloud, and a 2018 study agreed that the blocking material is most likely dust. 1,2,3
