Veterans' nuclear UFO press conference

2010

On September 27, 2010, researcher Robert Hastings and former missile officer Robert Salas hosted a press conference at the National Press Club in Washington, where former Air Force personnel said unidentified objects had hovered over nuclear weapons sites, sometimes as missiles went offline. The Air Force had found no such link. 1,2,3,4
