Unidentified: Inside America's UFO Investigation

2019

A History channel docuseries about the Pentagon's secret UFO investigation, featuring Luis Elizondo and Christopher Mellon, with Tom DeLonge as an executive producer. It covered the Navy videos, which the Navy confirmed were authentic. The Intercept's Keith Kloor questioned Elizondo's account of his role. 1
