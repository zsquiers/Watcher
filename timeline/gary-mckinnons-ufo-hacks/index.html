Gary McKinnon's UFO hacks

2002

British systems administrator Gary McKinnon broke into more than 90 U.S. military and NASA computers in 2001 and 2002, he says to look for hidden evidence of UFOs. He was charged in the United States, but in 2012 Britain refused to extradite him on health grounds. 1,2
