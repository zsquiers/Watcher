U.S.–Soviet nuclear accidents agreement

1971

On September 30, 1971, Secretary of State William Rogers and Soviet Foreign Minister Andrei Gromyko signed the Agreement on Measures to Reduce the Risk of Outbreak of Nuclear War, worked out by groups attached to the SALT talks. In Article 3 each side undertook to notify the other immediately "in the event of detection by missile warning systems of unidentified objects, or in the event of signs of interference with these systems or with related communications facilities," if this could create a risk of nuclear war. Urgent notices go over the Washington–Moscow hotline. It is sometimes called the 1971 SALT treaty, but SALT I itself was signed in May 1972. 1,2,3,4
