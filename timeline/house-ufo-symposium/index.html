House UFO symposium

1968

On July 29, 1968, the House Committee on Science and Astronautics held a one-day Symposium on Unidentified Flying Objects, chaired by Rep. J. Edward Roush, with scientists including J. Allen Hynek, James McDonald, and Carl Sagan. 1
