Zond 4 re-entry

1968

On the night of March 3, 1968, hundreds of people from Kentucky to Pennsylvania saw a procession of fiery objects, which NORAD identified as pieces of the Soviet Zond 4 launch falling back to Earth. A few witnesses described a cigar-shaped craft with a row of lighted windows, and the Condon Report used the case to show how a known event can be misperceived. 1,2
