Ben Rich's "take E.T. home" talk

1993

Retired Skunk Works director Ben Rich ended a talk to UCLA engineering alumni with a slide of a flying saucer. Attendee Jan Harzan, later head of MUFON, recalled him saying the U.S. now had the technology to take E.T. home, and the line became one of the most quoted in UFO lore. Rich had used the same closing joke since at least 1983. 1
