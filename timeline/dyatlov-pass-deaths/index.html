Dyatlov Pass deaths

1959

Nine Soviet hikers died on a mountain in the northern Urals after cutting their way out of their tent at night. Some had serious injuries, and hikers to the south reported orange spheres in the sky, which fed theories about secret weapons and UFOs. A Russian inquiry reopened in 2019 concluded in 2020 that an avalanche most likely drove them out into the cold. 1
