Men in Black

1997

Barry Sonnenfeld's comedy, an Amblin production with Steven Spielberg as executive producer, turns the "men in black" of UFO folklore into a secret agency that polices aliens living on Earth. It drew inspiration from Gray Barker's 1956 book They Knew Too Much About Flying Saucers, and its agents explain away real cases, among them the Michigan swamp gas reports, Roswell, the 1952 Washington flap and the Mantell incident. 1
