The mystery airship wave

1896

From November 1896, beginning in Sacramento, California, and moving east through 1897, thousands of Americans reported lights and cigar-shaped airships in the night sky, and newspapers ran many invented stories. Historian Mike Dash concluded most simple sightings were planets and stars, and many complex ones were hoaxes and practical jokes. 1
