UFOs: Past, Present, and Future

1974

A documentary written by Robert Emenegger and narrated by Rod Serling, Burgess Meredith and José Ferrer, which Emenegger was asked in 1971 to make using only official Defense Department and NASA material. Its interviewees included J. Allen Hynek and Col. William Coleman, a former Project Blue Book spokesman. Emenegger said he was promised footage of a 1964 landing at Holloman Air Force Base; the film shows a dramatized version, and the belief that real footage was used was later debunked. Jacques Vallée narrated a 1979 rerelease. 1
