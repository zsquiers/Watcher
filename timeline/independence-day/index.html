Independence Day

1996

Roland Emmerich's blockbuster about a worldwide alien attack, in which a fighter craft that crashed near Roswell in 1947 has been kept at Area 51 with three alien bodies. Emmerich conceived it while answering a question about whether he believed in alien life. 1
