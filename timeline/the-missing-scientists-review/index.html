The missing-scientists review

2026

On April 15, 2026, the White House said it was working with the FBI to review together the cases of scientists and others who had died or gone missing, and on April 20 House Oversight chair James Comer and Rep. Eric Burlison wrote to the Department of Energy, the Pentagon, the FBI and NASA, calling the cases a possible grave threat to national security. The FBI said it was looking for connections. NASA said nothing related to it indicated a threat. As of November 2026 no link had been established and no FBI report had been released. 1,2,3
