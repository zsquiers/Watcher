Wilbert Smith's flying saucer memo

1950

Canadian radio engineer Wilbert Smith wrote that U.S. scientist Robert Sarbacher told him Americans believed flying saucers were real, and that their study, led by Vannevar Bush, was more highly classified than the hydrogen bomb. The November 1950 memo stayed Top Secret in Canada until 1969 and led to Project Magnet. No U.S. record confirms what Sarbacher said. 1
