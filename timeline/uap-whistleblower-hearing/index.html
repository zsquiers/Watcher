UAP whistleblower hearing

2025

On September 9, 2025, a House Oversight task force chaired by Rep. Anna Paulina Luna held a hearing on UAP transparency and whistleblower protection. Military witnesses described sightings, and Rep. Eric Burlison played a video of a Hellfire missile appearing to strike an orb off Yemen in 2024. The Pentagon did not comment on the video. 1,2,3
