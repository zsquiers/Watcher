Aligned flashes on 1950s sky plates

2025

Astronomer Beatriz Villarroel and colleagues reported star-like flashes lined up in a row on single 1950s Palomar survey plates, and fewer flashes than expected inside Earth's shadow. They suggested flat, reflective objects in high orbit years before Sputnik, but said rare optical ghosting couldn't be ruled out. 1
