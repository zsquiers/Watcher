The Abyss

1989

James Cameron's film, in which a Navy submarine's encounter with an unidentified submerged object leads a civilian diving crew to a non-terrestrial intelligence living deep in the ocean. It was shot in the tank of an unfinished nuclear power plant near Gaffney, South Carolina, and won the Academy Award for Best Visual Effects. It has no documented link to a real case, though its theme mirrors reports of unidentified submerged objects. 1
