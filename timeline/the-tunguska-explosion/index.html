The Tunguska explosion

1908

On June 30, 1908, a huge blast over the Siberian forest flattened trees across more than 2,000 square km. Scientists attribute it to a meteor or comet fragment exploding in the air. A 1946 short story by Soviet writer Alexander Kazantsev imagined it as the nuclear explosion of a spaceship from Mars, and the idea became a lasting UFO legend. 1
