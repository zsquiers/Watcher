PURSUE files released

2026

On May 8, 2026, following a presidential directive, the Department of War launched WAR.GOV/UFO and its first release of UAP files under PURSUE. Further batches followed roughly monthly. They include Apollo-era records, military infrared videos, and FBI witness interviews. Most are previously unreleased and many lack context. 1,2
