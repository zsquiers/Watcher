Project Ozma

1960

From April to July 1960 Frank Drake pointed a 26-meter radio telescope at the nearby stars Tau Ceti and Epsilon Eridani, listening near the hydrogen line for artificial signals. No extraterrestrial signals were detected; a false signal on April 8 turned out to come from a high-flying aircraft. The whole project cost about two thousand dollars. 1,2,3
