COMETA report

1999

A private group of retired French generals and experts, many of them former auditors of France's Institute for Higher National Defence Studies, published UFOs and Defense: What Should We Prepare For?, concluding that unknown flying objects were almost certainly real and that the extraterrestrial hypothesis was the most probable explanation. 1,2,3
