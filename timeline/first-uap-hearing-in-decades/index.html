First UAP hearing in decades

2022

On May 17, 2022, a House Intelligence subcommittee held the first public congressional hearing on UFOs in half a century. Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence Ronald Moultrie and Deputy Director of Naval Intelligence Scott Bray showed new video and said most cases had ordinary explanations, while some remained unexplained. 1
