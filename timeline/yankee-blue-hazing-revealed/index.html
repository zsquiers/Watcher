Yankee Blue hazing revealed

2025

In June 2025, The Wall Street Journal reported that AARO had found that for decades some officers joining classified Air Force units were shown fake UFO material and told they would help reverse-engineer alien craft, under a fictitious program sometimes called Yankee Blue. The ritual was most likely a cover for real secret aircraft, and a Defense Secretary memo stopped it in 2023. The Pentagon acknowledged the pranks and promised a second volume of its historical report. 1
