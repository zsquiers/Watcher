Collider doomsday fears reviewed

1999

Before Brookhaven's Relativistic Heavy Ion Collider began running, fears in the press that it could create a black hole or set off some other catastrophe led lab director John Marburger to appoint four physicists to review them. Their 1999 report found every such scenario "firmly excluded by existing empirical evidence, compelling theoretical arguments, or both." Similar fears later surrounded CERN's Large Hadron Collider. 1,2,3
