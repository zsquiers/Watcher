First Starlink satellites launched

2019

SpaceX launched its first 60 Starlink satellites in May 2019. The "trains" of bright moving dots that followed set off waves of UFO reports, and AARO's FY2025 report explained 238 cases as sunlight flashing off satellites. 1,2
