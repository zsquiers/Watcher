Love & Saucers

2017

Brad Abrahams's documentary about David Huggins, a Hoboken painter who says he has had encounters with otherworldly beings since childhood and has painted them for decades. Religion scholar Jeffrey Kripal appears in it. The film leaves viewers to judge whether the experiences are dreams, hallucinations or real. 1,2
