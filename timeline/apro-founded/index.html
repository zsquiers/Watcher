APRO founded

1952

Jim and Coral Lorenzen founded the Aerial Phenomena Research Organization in Sturgeon Bay, Wisconsin. It moved to Tucson in 1960, ran field investigations with a network of scientific consultants, and lasted until 1988, making it one of the longest-running civilian UFO groups. 1
