UFO Cover-Up? Live!

1988

A two-hour syndicated TV special, hosted by Mike Farrell and aired October 14, 1988, that brought Majestic 12 and Area 51 stories to a mass audience. Two disguised informants called Falcon and Condor described captured aliens who liked strawberry ice cream. Researchers later identified Falcon as Air Force agent Richard Doty, who has given conflicting accounts. 1,2
