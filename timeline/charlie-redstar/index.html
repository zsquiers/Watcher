Charlie Redstar

1975

Through the spring and summer of 1975, people around Carman, Manitoba, reported a red ball of light that came to be called Charlie Redstar. Crowds gathered in fields to watch for it, and the sightings were part of a wave of UFO reports across Canada that year. 1
