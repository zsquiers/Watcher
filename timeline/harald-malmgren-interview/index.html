Harald Malmgren interview

2025

Weeks before his death in February 2025, former trade official Harald Malmgren told interviewer Jesse Michels that he had handled debris from a craft shot down in 1962 and seen evidence of non-human beings. His claims are unverified and disputed. 1,2,3
