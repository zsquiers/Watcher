Hangar 18

1980

A Sunn Classic Pictures film about a crashed alien craft hidden in a hangar at an Air Force base, derived from the Roswell incident and rumors about Area 51. Its title is believed to come from the hoaxer Robert Spencer Carr, who in 1974 named Hangar 18 as the place where bodies from the 1948 Aztec crash hoax were stored. Its TV ads promised to reveal the truth about UFOs. 1
