AARO's GoFast analysis

2025

In February 2025, AARO published its analysis of the 2015 GoFast video. It concluded the object was about 13,000 feet up, not skimming the water, and was drifting with the wind at ordinary speeds, with the apparent speed coming from the jet's own motion (parallax). AARO said it could not identify the object itself. 1
