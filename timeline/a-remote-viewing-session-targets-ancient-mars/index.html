A remote viewing session targets ancient Mars

1984

On May 22, 1984, a remote viewer, later confirmed as Joseph McMoneagle, was handed a sealed envelope, which he was not allowed to open, holding a card that read "The planet Mars. Time of interest approximately 1 million years B.C." Focusing on it, he described pyramids, an obelisk, carved channels and very tall, thin people sheltering from a great storm. McMoneagle says the session was requested by an unnamed Army official, not the CIA, and that neither he nor his monitor knew the target was Mars. The transcript was approved for public release in August 2000. 1,2,3
