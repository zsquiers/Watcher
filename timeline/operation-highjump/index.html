Operation Highjump

1947

The Navy's largest Antarctic expedition, with about 4,700 men, 13 ships and 33 aircraft under Richard Byrd as officer in charge, set up the Little America IV base and photographed huge stretches of coastline. On December 30, 1946, the flying boat George 1 crashed in a blizzard, killing three men; a fourth died in an unloading accident in January. The ships withdrew in late February 1947 as winter approached early. On March 5, Chile's El Mercurio ran Byrd's warning that in a new war, the United States could be attacked by planes flying over one or both poles. 1,2,3
