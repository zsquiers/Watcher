French aerospace society opens a UAP commission

2008

France's aerospace engineering society, 3AF, created a technical commission to study unidentified aerospace phenomena, first called PAN and then SIGMA, which became SIGMA2 in 2013 with a focus on cases that remain unexplained. It has published progress reports, including a 2021 report of nearly 400 pages. 1
