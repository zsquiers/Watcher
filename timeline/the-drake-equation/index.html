The Drake equation

1961

At the first scientific meeting on the search for extraterrestrial intelligence, held at Green Bank in 1961, Frank Drake wrote an equation estimating the number of communicating civilizations in the galaxy, not to give a precise answer but as a way to stimulate scientific dialogue. The ten participants, among them Carl Sagan, called themselves "The Order of the Dolphin." Critics note that most of its factors are guesses. 1,2
