Air Marshal Goddard's UFO talk

1969

Retired RAF Air Marshal Sir Victor Goddard told an audience at Caxton Hall in London that UFOs might come from an invisible, paraphysical world rather than from other planets. Writer John Keel quoted the talk in his 1970 book Operation Trojan Horse. 1
