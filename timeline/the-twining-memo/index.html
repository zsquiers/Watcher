The Twining memo

1947

On September 23, 1947, Lieutenant General Nathan Twining, head of the Air Materiel Command at Wright Field, answered a request from Brigadier General George Schulgen for its view of the "flying discs." He wrote that "the phenomenon reported is something real and not visionary or fictitious," and recommended a detailed study, which became Project Sign. A 2020 Skeptical Inquirer review argues the memo shows the military was accepting anecdotal reports at face value. 1,2
