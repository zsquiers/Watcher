Japan's forces told to report UFOs

2020

After the Pentagon released its Navy UFO videos, Japanese Defense Minister Taro Kono ordered the Self-Defense Forces, from September 14, 2020, to record, photograph and analyze any unidentified objects they encounter. He said Japanese pilots had never met a UFO, and that he didn't really believe in them himself. 1
