The Aquarius document

1980

A purported Air Force Office of Special Investigations message dated November 17, 1980, one of the false documents Richard Doty and others fed to Paul Bennewitz, referred to a "Project Aquarius" and to "MJ Twelve," saying the program's results were "still classified top secret." In 1983 Doty showed Linda Moulton Howe fabricated "presidential brief papers" at Kirtland, and a "Project Snowbird" to fly recovered craft appears only in the same circle of claims, with no document behind it. William Moore admitted taking part in the disinformation in 1989, and Doty later admitted deceiving researchers. Records released in 2017 show the real Project Aquarius was an ocean-surveillance program, "not aliens." 1,2,3,4
