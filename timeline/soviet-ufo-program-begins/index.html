Soviet UFO program begins

1978

After the 1977 Petrozavodsk sighting, the Soviet Defense Ministry and Academy of Sciences began a secret study of UFO reports known as Setka, or the Network. By 1990 it had looked at about 3,000 cases and explained roughly 90 percent as rocket launches, balloons and other human activity. 1,2
