The "gravitics" boom

1955

On November 20, 1955, the New York Herald-Tribune reported that aircraft and electronics firms including Glenn L. Martin, Convair, Bell Aircraft, Sikorsky, Lear, Clarke Electronics and Sperry Gyroscope had scientists working on gravity, with physicists such as J. Robert Oppenheimer, Freeman Dyson, John Wheeler and Edward Teller backing the research. It also said that no one had yet "produced the slightest alteration" in gravity. A British industry report, The Gravitics Situation, followed in December 1956. 1,2
