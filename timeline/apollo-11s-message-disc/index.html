Apollo 11's message disc

1969

NASA gathered goodwill messages from the leaders of 73 countries, along with the names of Congress's leaders and NASA's top officials. GCA Corp. of Burlington, Massachusetts, shrank them onto a photomask, and Sprague Electric of North Adams etched them at 1/200 scale onto a silicon disc about the size of a half dollar. Buzz Aldrin dropped it on the Sea of Tranquility in July 1969, its rim reading "From Planet Earth, July 1969." 1,2
