Blue Book Special Report 14

1954

Battelle's statistical study of 3,201 Air Force UFO reports from 1952 to 1954 was completed in 1954. Four analysts sorted cases into knowns, unknowns and insufficient information; about 22 percent remained unidentified, and the share of unknowns rose with the quality of the report. Navy physicist Bruce Maccabee was among the researchers who later reanalyzed its statistics. 1,2,3
