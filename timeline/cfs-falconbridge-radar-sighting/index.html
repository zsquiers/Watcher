CFS Falconbridge radar sighting

1975

On November 11, 1975, staff at the Canadian radar station near Sudbury, Ontario, tracked and watched a brilliantly lit circular object that climbed from 42,000 to 72,000 feet over 14 minutes, according to a NORAD message to Canada's National Research Council. Police in the area also reported sightings. Suggested explanations included Venus, clouds and balloons. 1,2
