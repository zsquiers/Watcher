The Phenomenon

2020

James Fox's documentary, narrated by Peter Coyote, on the Pentagon UFO videos and the history of sightings since World War II, which argues for more scientific study. It features Harry Reid, John Podesta, Christopher Mellon, Bill Richardson, Leslie Kean and Jacques Vallée, with witnesses to the Ariel School, Westall, Lonnie Zamora and Rendlesham cases. Reviews were mostly favorable. 1
