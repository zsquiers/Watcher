Starfish Prime

1962

On July 9, 1962, a 1.4-megaton warhead launched from Johnston Atoll exploded about 400 km (250 miles) above the Pacific, the largest nuclear test ever conducted in space. Its electromagnetic pulse knocked out about 300 streetlights and set off burglar alarms in Hawaii, 1,450 km away. The artificial radiation belts it created disabled six or more satellites over the following months, including Telstar 1 and Britain's Ariel 1. Some UAP researchers link these tests to Harald Malmgren's claim of a 1962 shootdown; the tests are documented, the shootdown is not. 1,2
