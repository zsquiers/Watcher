The Day the Earth Stood Still

1951

Robert Wise's film about an alien visitor who lands in Washington with a robot to deliver a warning to all humanity. Wise said the Pentagon refused to take part after reading the script, so the Army hardware seen on screen came from the 3rd Armored Cavalry instead. 1
