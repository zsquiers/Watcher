Reagan's alien-threat remarks

1985

At his first meeting with Mikhail Gorbachev, in Geneva in November 1985, Ronald Reagan asked whether the Soviet Union would help if the United States were suddenly attacked from outer space; Gorbachev later recalled answering, "No doubt about it." On December 4 Reagan told students at Fallston High School in Maryland that a threat "from another planet" would make countries forget their differences, and he used the image again at the United Nations in 1987. He offered it as a picture of cooperation; none of these remarks ties it to SDI. 1,2,3,4
