Green fireballs over New Mexico

1948

From late 1948, pilots, Air Force intelligence officers and scientists around Albuquerque and Los Alamos reported bright green balls of fire crossing the night sky, flatter and greener than ordinary meteors. On December 5, 1948, an Air Force transport crew and an airliner crew both reported one. The meteor expert Lincoln LaPaz saw one himself a week later. 1,2,3
