Billy Meier's Pleiadian contact claims

1975

Swiss farmer Billy Meier says that on January 28, 1975, he met Semjase, a woman from the Plejaren, and he soon began producing photos and films of their "beamships" over the Swiss countryside. The pictures made him the world's best-known contactee of the period; his ex-wife later said they were of models he built. 1
