UAP Disclosure Act

2023

In July 2023 Senators Chuck Schumer and Mike Rounds proposed a law to declassify UAP records, with an independent review board and power to take possession of recovered material. In the final FY2024 defense bill the House stripped the review board and those powers, but a UAP Records Collection at the National Archives was created. 1
