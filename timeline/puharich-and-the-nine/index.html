Puharich and the Nine

1952

At his Round Table Foundation in Maine, Andrija Puharich held sessions with an Indian medium who said he channeled the Nine, a council of higher beings. The same year Puharich briefed the Pentagon on ESP as a possible psychological warfare tool. 1,2
