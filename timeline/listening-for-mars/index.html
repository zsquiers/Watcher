Listening for Mars

1924

When Mars passed unusually close to Earth in August 1924, Amherst astronomer David Todd campaigned for a National Radio Silence Day so receivers could listen for Martian signals. The Navy and Army reportedly told their radio stations to monitor for anything unusual. Strange signals were reported, but they were explained, and there was no evidence Mars was trying to talk. 1
