Sputnik 1 launched

1957

The Soviet Union put the first artificial satellite into orbit on October 4, 1957. A month later came the November 1957 sighting wave, including Levelland, Texas, and from then on satellites, rocket bodies and re-entries became a steady source of UFO reports. 1
