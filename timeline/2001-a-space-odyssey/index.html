2001: A Space Odyssey

1968

Stanley Kubrick's film, written with Arthur C. Clarke, follows a mission to Jupiter to investigate an alien monolith. Kubrick asked Carl Sagan how best to depict extraterrestrial intelligence, though Sagan's advice is unlikely to have had any direct influence on the film. 1
