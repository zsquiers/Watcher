Eighth PURSUE release

2026

On November 20, 2026, the Pentagon posted its eighth batch of UAP files: 40 documents and three videos. They include a 1952 CIA briefing on about 1,500 flying saucer reports, three CENTCOM infrared clips from 2020, 2023 and 2026, Lockheed engineer Kelly Johnson's 1953 sighting, and a 2009 plan for an underground vault for exotic material that there is no record was ever built. 1,2
