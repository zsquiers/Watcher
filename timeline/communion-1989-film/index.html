Communion (1989 film)

1989

Philippe Mora's film, adapted by Whitley Strieber from his 1987 book, with Christopher Walken as Strieber, which Strieber presents as a true account of his own encounters with "visitors." The filmmakers held special screenings for people who said they had been abducted, and Strieber later said the film probably overstates the case in how it shows the beings. 1
