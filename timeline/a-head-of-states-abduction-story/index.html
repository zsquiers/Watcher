A head of state's abduction story

1997

Kirsan Ilyumzhinov, president of the Russian republic of Kalmykia, later said that in September 1997 aliens took him from his apartment aboard their ship while his aides searched for him. He repeated the story on Russian television in 2010, prompting a lawmaker's request to investigate whether he had revealed state secrets. 1,2
