Gemini 4's mystery object

1965

During the Gemini 4 mission in June 1965, astronaut James McDivitt saw and photographed a white cylindrical shape with a pole sticking out of one corner, while Ed White slept. McDivitt always said it was a man-made piece of debris he couldn't identify, not an alien craft. Space historian James Oberg argues it was most likely the mission's own spent Titan II second stage. 1,2
