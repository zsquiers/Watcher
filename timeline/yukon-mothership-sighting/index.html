Yukon mothership sighting

1996

On the night of December 11, 1996, at least 31 people at Fox Lake, Carmacks, Pelly Crossing and Mayo in the Yukon reported a huge craft with rows of lights, which one called the size of a football stadium. Satellite analyst Ted Molczan later matched it to a Soviet rocket stage falling back to Earth that evening. 1,2
