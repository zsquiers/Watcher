General McCasland goes missing

2026

Retired Air Force Maj. Gen. William Neil McCasland, a former commander of the Air Force Research Laboratory, was reported missing from his Albuquerque home on February 27, 2026. Within weeks, posts and podcasts tied his disappearance to other scientists who had died or vanished, drawing the attention of the FBI, the White House and Congress. 1,2
