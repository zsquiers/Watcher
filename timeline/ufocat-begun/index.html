UFOCAT begun

1967

Psychologist David Saunders, a co-principal investigator on the Air Force-funded Colorado UFO project, started UFOCAT, a computer catalog of UFO reports, seeded with about 6,000 cases from Jacques Vallée. He gave it to the Center for UFO Studies in 1976. The 2023 version has more than 300,000 entries, over 192,000 of them primary reports. CUFOS cautions that it lists many cases more than once and that its sources vary in reliability. 1
