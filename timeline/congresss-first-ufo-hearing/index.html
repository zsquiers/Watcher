Congress's first UFO hearing

1966

After the Air Force's J. Allen Hynek suggested some Michigan sightings were "swamp gas," House Minority Leader Gerald Ford of Michigan called for a hearing. The House Armed Services Committee met on April 6, 1966, and the Air Force soon funded the University of Colorado study led by Edward Condon. 1
