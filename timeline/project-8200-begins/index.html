Project 8200 begins

1982

Skip Atwater, operations and training officer of the Army's remote viewing unit at Fort Meade, turned Pat Price's four alleged UFO bases into blind training targets, using Price's transcripts and information from Hal Puthoff. Joseph McMoneagle viewed Mount Zeil on 3 August 1982, and other viewers followed in later years, including Paul H. Smith on Mount Hayes. The results were never reported up the chain of command; Atwater published the transcripts in 2024 through Center Lane Books. 1,2,3
