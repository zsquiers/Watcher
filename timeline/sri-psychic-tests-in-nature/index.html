SRI psychic tests in Nature

1974

Physicists Russell Targ and Harold Puthoff published their tests of Uri Geller and others at Stanford Research Institute in the journal Nature. The SRI work led to the government's remote viewing programs, later known as Stargate. Critics faulted the experiments' controls. 1
