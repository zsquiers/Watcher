The Fourth Kind

2009

A pseudodocumentary by Olatunde Osunsanmi, set in Nome, Alaska, that takes its title from J. Allen Hynek's classifications and passed off staged "archival footage" as real. It was loosely inspired by real disappearances there, which investigators attributed mainly to alcohol and the cold. Universal admitted using the names of real Alaska newspapers for fake news stories and, in a settlement, paid $20,000 to the Alaska Press Club. 1
