Deep Prasad's abduction account

2019

Canadian technology entrepreneur Deep Prasad said that sometime between 2017 and 2019 he was taken aboard a craft by non-human beings in an experience he compared to a kundalini awakening, and that it turned him toward UFO research. The account rests on his own statements and has not been independently verified. 1,2
