Tesla: Talking with the Planets

1901

In a 1901 Collier's Weekly article, Nikola Tesla wrote that the regular signals he had picked up at his Colorado Springs lab in 1899 were probably messages from another planet. Later researchers suggest he heard natural radio noise or, possibly, Marconi's transmissions. 1
