The Army's Gateway Process assessment

1983

Asked to assess the Gateway Experience, the Monroe Institute's taped training built on Robert Monroe's Hemi-Sync brain hemisphere synchronization technique, Army Lt. Col. Wayne McDonnell wrote a 29-page report dated June 9, 1983. To explain the physical side of the process he drew on the biomedical models developed by Itzhak Bentov. It was released in the CIA's Stargate collection, with page 25 missing, and went viral on TikTok in 2021. It is one officer's theoretical assessment, not a test showing the technique works. 1,2,3
