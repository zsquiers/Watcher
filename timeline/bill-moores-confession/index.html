Bill Moore's confession

1989

At the July 1989 MUFON symposium in Las Vegas, researcher Bill Moore said he had spent years cooperating with Air Force intelligence agents, including Richard Doty, who were feeding disinformation to UFO researchers, and that he had reported on fellow researchers in return for access. He said the insiders called themselves the Aviary, using bird codenames. The admission damaged trust in the Majestic 12 papers he had helped circulate. 1,2
