Sturrock panel meets at Pocantico

1997

Funded by Laurance Rockefeller, Stanford physicist Peter Sturrock brought UFO investigators before a panel of nine scientists at the Pocantico Conference Center in New York. The panel found the evidence inconclusive but said puzzling cases deserved further scientific study. 1,2
