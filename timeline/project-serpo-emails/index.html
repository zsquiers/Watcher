Project Serpo emails

2005

Anonymous emails to a UFO discussion group run by Victor Martinez described Project Serpo, an alleged secret exchange in which twelve U.S. military personnel traveled to a planet called Serpo in the Zeta Reticuli system between 1965 and 1978. The story echoed one Air Force agent Richard Doty had offered journalist Linda Moulton Howe in 1983. No evidence has ever been produced. 1
