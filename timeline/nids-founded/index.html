NIDS founded

1995

Las Vegas real-estate developer Robert Bigelow formed the National Institute for Discovery Science, a privately financed group that studied UFOs, cattle mutilations, black triangle reports and other anomalies. In 1996 it began studying Skinwalker Ranch in Utah, which Bigelow bought, with former Los Alamos non-lethal weapons official John B. Alexander among its scientists. Others associated with NIDS included Eric W. Davis, Kit Green, Colm Kelleher, Edgar Mitchell, Hal Puthoff and Jacques Vallée. A reporting hotline opened in 1999 took more than 5,000 reports, many explained as missile launches and meteors. NIDS disbanded in October 2004. 1,2
