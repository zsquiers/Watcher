Sky-plate flashes and nuclear tests

2025

A companion paper in Scientific Reports, led by Stephen Bruehl, found the Palomar flashes were 45 percent more likely within a day of a nuclear test from 1949 to 1957, and rose with the number of UFO reports on the same day. The authors said what the links mean remains to be determined. 1
