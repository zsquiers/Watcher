Ancient Aliens

2009

A History channel series that explores the ancient astronaut idea "in a non-critical, documentary format," with episodes on UFOs, abductions, Roswell and Rendlesham Forest, and guests including Linda Moulton Howe and Nick Pope. Historians, archaeologists and scientists have criticized it, and Smithsonian magazine accused it of piling on fictions and distortions. 1
