Bluegill Triple Prime

1962

The fourth attempt at the Bluegill high-altitude test, after a rocket failure, a launch-pad explosion that spread plutonium on Johnston Atoll, and another failed launch. It detonated a warhead officially described as submegaton, believed to be about 400 kilotons, about 48 km (30 miles) above Johnston Island on October 26, 1962, during the Cuban Missile Crisis. Films of the blast later became the center of a claim that it knocked down a UFO. 1,2,3
