Close Encounters of the Third Kind

1977

Steven Spielberg's film takes its title from J. Allen Hynek's classification of close encounters, and Hynek was hired as its scientific consultant; he said that although it is fiction it is "based for the most part on the known facts of the UFO mystery." The French scientist Lacombe was modeled on Jacques Vallée. The Air Force and NASA declined to cooperate. Its climax was filmed at Devils Tower in Wyoming, and the Cleveland Scene has called its police chase an homage to the 1966 Portage County chase. 1,2
