To The Stars Academy launches

2017

In October 2017, musician Tom DeLonge launched To The Stars Academy of Arts and Science with Harold Puthoff, Chris Mellon, and Luis Elizondo. In December it helped publicize two Navy UAP videos alongside The New York Times' AATIP story. 1,2
