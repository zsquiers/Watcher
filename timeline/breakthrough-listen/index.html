Breakthrough Listen

2015

Announced by Yuri Milner at the Royal Society in London on July 20, 2015, with Stephen Hawking, the program committed $100 million over 10 years to search for signals from other civilizations with the Green Bank and Parkes radio telescopes. Its best candidate, a narrow signal called BLC1 found in 2019 data while observing Proxima Centauri, was shown in 2021 to be radio interference from Earth, "an electronically drifting intermodulation product of local, time-varying interferers." 1,2,3,4
