Drones over U.S. bases in England

2024

From 20 November 2024, small drones were seen near and over RAF Lakenheath, RAF Mildenhall and RAF Feltwell in eastern England, and later RAF Fairford. U.S. Air Forces in Europe said the number of drones fluctuated and that they ranged in sizes and configurations, but did not say who was behind them; it said there was no impact on residents, facilities or assets. The flights came during a week of escalation in Ukraine, after Ukraine first fired Western-supplied missiles into Russia and Russia answered with a new intermediate-range missile. 1,2
