Project Blue Book (TV series)

2019

A History channel drama created by David O'Leary, with Robert Zemeckis among its producers and Aidan Gillen as J. Allen Hynek, that dramatizes real cases including the Flatwoods monster, the Lubbock Lights, Betty and Barney Hill, the 1952 Washington flap, Roswell, Kelly-Hopkinsville and Skinwalker Ranch. The skeptic Robert Sheaffer argued its inaccuracies go beyond the usual liberties. 1
