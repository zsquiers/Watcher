Japan's parliamentary UAP group

2024

Members of Japan's Diet from several parties formed a group, founded on June 6, 2024 and chaired by Yasukazu Hamada, to press the government to set up an office to study UAP and work with the United States. Former defense minister Shigeru Ishiba, later prime minister, was an adviser. The group said that if the objects are foreign spy drones or secret weapons, they are a security threat. 1,2
