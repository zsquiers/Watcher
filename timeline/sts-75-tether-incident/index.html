STS-75 "tether incident"

1996

On February 25, 1996, during Space Shuttle Columbia's STS-75 mission, the cable of the Tethered Satellite System snapped after about 19 km had been reeled out, and the satellite drifted away. Shuttle camera footage of the sunlit tether, surrounded by dozens of glowing, notched discs, became one of the most widely shared "UFO swarm" videos. 1
