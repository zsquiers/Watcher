NARCAP founded

1999

NASA Ames scientist Richard Haines and Ted Roe set up the National Aviation Reporting Center on Anomalous Phenomena, modeled on NASA's Aviation Safety Reporting System, so that pilots could report unusual sightings confidentially and without ridicule. 1,2
