Fermi asks "Where is everybody?"

1950

Over lunch at Los Alamos in 1950, physicist Enrico Fermi asked colleagues including Edward Teller why, if intelligent life is common, there was no sign of it. The "Fermi paradox" became a central puzzle for the search for extraterrestrial intelligence. In 1981 Frank Tipler argued that because no self-replicating "von Neumann probes" have been seen, no other civilizations exist; Carl Sagan and William Newman disputed his reasoning. 1,2
