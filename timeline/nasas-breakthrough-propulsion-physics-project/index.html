NASA's Breakthrough Propulsion Physics Project

1996

NASA funded a small project from 1996 to 2002, with about $1.2 million in total, to study propulsion ideas that would need breakthroughs in physics. Its manager, Marc Millis, reported that antigravity-like approaches were non-viable: its test of Podkletnov's gravity shielding found no effect. 1,2
